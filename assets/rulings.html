Unofficial fan index, not affiliated with FryxGames, Stronghold Games or BoardGameGeek. Questions and answers are paraphrased and the quote is verbatim, so follow the link to the post before you settle an argument with it.

Whose answers count

Only posts from these BoardGameGeek accounts are included. Other frequent posters in the rules forums are players, so their answers are left out even when they are right.

Reading the labels

Ruling, FAQ
A rules answer. The banner is green for a ruling and blue for an answer filed as a frequently asked question.
Design intent
What the designer meant, where that settles a question the rules leave open.
Erratum
The printed card or rulebook is wrong and the post gives the correct version.
Variant
An official or endorsed variant, not the standard rules.
Pre-release
Posted before the game came out in September 2016, while the rules were still changing.
Prototype
From the 2022 fan prototype thread for the Automa, before the published version.
Superseded
A later official post on the same question says something else. The card links to it.
Hedged
The poster said "I think", "I would say" or similar.
Inferred
The post does not name the card, or answers a question that has to be read from context.
House rule OK
The poster said to play it however your group likes.
Reversed
The same poster took it back later in the same thread.

Sources and limits

Read on 29 October 2026: every thread in the Rules, General, Strategy, Variants, News, Crowdfunding, Reviews and Sessions forums of the base game and its expansions on BoardGameGeek, plus the FryxGames and Stronghold Games company forums. That is 7,550 threads and 107,145 posts, 2,458 of them by the accounts above.

Some answers read firmer here than in the post, usually because an "I think" got lost in the paraphrase. Superseded answers were matched within one expansion at a time, so an answer without that label is not proof that nothing later replaced it.

For the Automa, Nick Shaw wrote on 13 October 2026 that forum answers from him or Dávid Turczi supersede the rulebook wording.

Ares Expedition, the dice game and fan-made content are separate and are not covered.
