# Base game: official BGG rulings

664 rulings posted on BoardGameGeek by FryxGames, Stronghold Games and the Automa designers. [README.md](README.md) says who is who, how this was built, and what the tags mean. Under each heading the rulings run in date order, so a later one can overrule an earlier one.

## Rules


### Action costs

- **Is it a misprint that an action shows an energy production decrease before the arrow while raising TR?** No. If an action has a cost it is shown before the arrow (e.g. pay 1 energy production to raise your terraform rating); costs are not shown with a minus sign, and the explanation text clarifies it.  
  "If an action has a cost, you see this cost before the arrow, for example pay 1 energy production to raise your terraform rating." ([Enoch, 2016-09-18](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1639526/article/23734306#23734306)) `inferred`
- **Can you still add a resource with an action if you cannot or will not pay the cost of taking another player's resource?** No. The left-hand side of an action is a cost: paying is optional but without paying you do not get the effect. You can also take animals from your own cards, though that makes the card much weaker.  
  "The left hand side of an action is a cost. If the cost is not paid, you do not get the effect." ([Jonathan, 2016-10-11](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1651305/article/23926421#23926421)) `inferred`
- **Is a red-bordered resource cost of a blue card action optional?** No. Costs of an action (left of the red arrow) are always mandatory to perform the action, even for red-bordered resources (e.g. Ants removing 1 microbe to add one); the optional exception applies only to the immediate effects of playing a card.  
  "Costs of an action (on the left side of the red arrow) are always mandatory to perform the action, no matter what." ([Enoch, 2016-11-03](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1665700/article/24119589#24119589))
- **Must the cost part of a blue card action (before the arrow) always be paid, even with a red-bordered resource?** Yes. Any cost for using a blue card action (before the red arrow) must be paid to get the reward, regardless of red borders. This applies to Ants and Predators, but it is not a requirement to play the card, only to use its action.  
  "Any cost for using an action on a blue card (before the red arrow) MUST be payed in order to get the reward (after the arrow). Regardless of red borders and such." ([Jacob, 2016-11-17](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1673797/article/24242509#24242509))
- **Why is a cost before the arrow on an action not written with a '-' sign?** Iconography convention: an effect is written with a minus, a cost is not (costs before the arrow have no minus).  
  "We had a heated discussion about this but concluded that an effect is written with a minus, but a cost isn't." ([Enoch, 2017-11-27](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1890770/article/27542874#27542874)) `intent`
- **Are resource removals that are costs (such as for Ants and Predators) mandatory?** Fryxen says that if it's a cost it's always mandatory, citing Ants and Predators. The terse reply does not spell out the surrounding discussion (the preceding post is cut off).  
  "Also, if it's a cost it's always mandatory. Ants and Predators." ([Jacob, 2017-12-31](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1705209/article/27832782#27832782)) `hedged` `inferred`

### Awards

- **Is a value of 0 still eligible for second place in an award?** There are no special rules: if all players tied for second place have 0, they all get 2 VP. If no player has anything counting, all share first place and get 5 VP.  
  "We don't have any special rules in this case. If all players on second place happens to have 0 in that category, then they all get some very cheap 2 VPs!" ([Enoch, 2016-09-19](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1640321/article/23737963#23737963)) `inferred`
- **Is it acceptable to give no award VP to a player with zero in the category?** It is more thematic that a player with zero gets nothing; there is a strong case for a house rule, though the official rule has no such exception.  
  "It is more thematic, I agree, so you have a strong case for a houserule here." ([Enoch, 2016-09-21](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1640321/article/23756754#23756754)) `intent` `house-rule-ok`
- **Why do the Thermalist and Miner awards count leftover resources while Banker counts production?** Design intent: Thermalist and Miner count held heat, steel and titanium so those resources stay relevant at the end, while the Banker award keeps MC production relevant without over-rewarding TR.  
  "Also, in combination with the miner and thermalist awards, steel, titanium, and heat (and even energy) also stay relevant." ([Jacob, 2016-11-14](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1671736/article/24211152#24211152)) `intent`
- **How are awards scored when players tie for first or second place?** A tie for 1st place means no 2nd place: each tied winner gets 5 VP and the others none. If the tie is for 2nd place, the 1st place player gets 5 VP and each of the two 2nd place players gets 2 VP.  
  "Tie for 1st place -> no 2nd place. Ties are friendly, so that would be 5 VP each for the winners, none for the others." ([Jacob, 2017-12-22](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1906162/article/27781521#27781521))

### Banker (award)

- **Does the Banker award count TR as part of MC production?** No. Banker only counts the specific MC production on the board, not including the TR.  
  "Banker only counts the specific MC production on the board, not including the TR." ([Jacob, 2016-06-03](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1585476/article/22849316#22849316)) `prerelease`
- **What does the Banker award count?** Banker counts the MC production on your player board; it does not add any TR.  
  "Banker counts MC production on your player board, not adding any TR." ([Enoch, 2016-10-19](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1654256/article/23988305#23988305))

### Blue card actions

- **Can a card with an action be used in the same round it is played?** Yes. Action cards may be used in the same round that you play them. Action cards are played upright and tipped sideways when their action is used, then tipped back in the production phase; each card's action can be used once per round.  
  "Actions cards should be allowed to be used on the same round that you play them." ([Jacob, 2014-12-10](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1274610/article/17693384#17693384)) `prerelease`
- **Do all actions on a blue card trigger together, and how often can a blue card be used?** Blue cards have actions or effects. Effects always work. A card with an action (red arrow) may be used only once per generation, and if a card has more than one action you must choose which one action to perform that generation.  
  "A blue card with ACTION (red arrow) may only be used once per generation. If there are more than one action to choose from on a card, you must therefore choose which one action to perform that generation." ([Isaac, 2016-09-06](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1633430/article/23626667#23626667))
- **Are actions on blue (active) cards optional, and how often can they be used?** Actions on blue cards are always optional and may be used only once per generation.  
  "Actions on blue cards are always optional, but may only be used once per generation." ([Enoch, 2016-11-08](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1668655/article/24160065#24160065))
- **How often can actions be used: blue cards, corporations, converting heat/plants, standard projects?** Actions on blue cards (and corporations like UNMI) may be used only once per generation (mark with a player marker). All other actions, such as converting heat and plants, playing cards or standard projects, are unlimited.  
  "actions on blue cards (or corporations like UNMI) may only be used once per generation." ([Enoch, 2016-12-01](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1661666/article/24368152#24368152))
- **Can both actions of a blue card with a choice of actions be used in one generation?** No. The card provides one action per generation; if it offers a choice (e.g. collect resources or spend them), you must choose one, never both.  
  "The card still only provide you ONE action per generation, so you cannot use both of them." ([Enoch, 2016-12-01](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1661666/article/24368392#24368392))
- **Do the rules for playing a card (paying costs, performing all effects) also apply to using an action?** Using any action is similar to playing a card: you pay the cost and get the effect. If you cannot pay the cost you cannot use the action, and if you cannot perform the effects (with the same 3 exceptions as for playing cards) you cannot do the action either.  
  "Using any action is similar to playing a card (this answers most of Ken's questions). You pay the cost and get the effect." ([Jacob, 2016-12-03](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1682744/article/24387139#24387139))
- **How often can actions be used in a generation, in particular blue card actions?** Blue cards with actions can be used only once per generation; if a card gives 2 optional actions only one can be activated. All other actions (converting plants or heat, playing cards, standard projects, claiming milestones or awards) can be done many times, and blue card effects (not actions) can be activated any time.  
  "The only exception is the blue cards with actions on them - they can only be used once. If the card gives you 2 optional actions, then you can only activate one of them." ([Enoch, 2016-12-19](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1692099/article/24532146#24532146))
- **How do actions on blue cards work: frequency, multiple actions, costs and effects?** Blue card actions can be used once per generation (mark with your player marker until production). If a card has more than one action, choose which one to use. Pay any cost before the arrow first, then perform the effect after the arrow with the same restrictions as playing a card: you must perform it in full, except raising an already maxed global parameter, removing opposing resources, or adding resources you can't collect. If you can't perform the effect or pay the cost, you can't take the action.  
  "Blue cards with actions can only be used once per generation (mark them as used with your player marker until the production phase)." ([Jacob, 2017-01-10](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1705209/article/24723217#24723217))
- **Are playing a blue card and using its action separate actions, and must they happen in the same turn?** They are 2 separate actions. You do not have to play the card and activate it in the same turn.  
  "But you don't have to play the card and activate it on the same turn, though." ([Enoch, 2017-06-26](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1801879/article/26225582#26225582))
- **Which actions are limited to once per generation?** All actions are shown with red arrows, but only actions on blue cards and corporations (UNMI, Viron, Celestic) can be activated once per generation. Other actions (standard actions on the board, exchanging heat or plants on the player board, claiming milestones/awards, playing cards) are not restricted.  
  "Other actions (Standard actions shown on the game board, exchanging heat or plants on the player board, claiming milestones/awards, or playing cards) are not restricted." ([Enoch, 2017-11-27](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1890770/article/27542874#27542874))
- **Can a blue card action be used when it gains nothing (e.g. Symbiotic Fungus with no microbe card, Martian Zoo with no animals)?** Yes. Per the FAQ, actions must be able to be performed in full except for raising an already maxed global parameter, removing opposing resources, or adding resources you cannot collect; the same applies to any action, including standard projects, converting plants or heat, and trading with a colony tile.  
  "Yes you can." ([Jacob, 2019-09-06](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2270815/article/32879610#32879610))

### Builder (milestone)

- **How many building tags does the Builder milestone require, given that the early board image posted online differs?** The Builder milestone requires only 8 building tags; the early pre-finalized board image that showed another number is wrong.  
  "Builder milestone only requires 8 building tags." ([Jonathan, 2017-03-31](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1751738/article/25500088#25500088))

### Card text and icons

- **How should the 'VP / resource' icon on cards be read?** It means VP per resource, as in 'km/h': Livestock gives 1 VP for each animal on it and Herbivores gives 1 VP per 2 animals on it. Each card also carries a small text explaining the icons.  
  ""1 VP for each animal on this card." for Livestock, and "1 VP per 2 animals on this card." for Herbivores. This usage is consistent with daily use of language such as km/h = kilometers per hour." ([Jacob, 2016-03-15](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1541954/article/22116062#22116062)) `prerelease`
- **A translated card's explanation text disagrees with its icons; which is authoritative?** All current and coming translations derive from the English original. Most people read the iconography correctly even if the explanation text is badly translated, and the errors should be corrected in the next print.  
  "All current and coming translations derives from the English original." ([Enoch, 2016-10-19](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1655619/article/23989148#23989148)) `intent` `inferred`
- **The French Zone de Mohole text says energy production but the icon shows heat; which is right?** The icon is correct. Only the text is translated, icons are always the original and correct ones; English Mohole Area increases heat production.  
  "Only the text was translated; the icons are always original and correct, regardless of translation." ([Jacob, 2017-05-29](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1787926/article/26002400#26002400))
- **Why do some greenery cards say to raise oxygen in the text; is that an additional oxygen step?** No. The text is only explaining the icons and adds nothing not shown by icons; a second oxygen raise would have a second oxygen symbol on the card. The reminder exists because the greenery-raises-oxygen link is sometimes overlooked. Exceptions are cards marked with an asterisk.  
  "If the card would give you a second raise in oxygen there would have been an additional oxygen-symbol on the card." ([Enoch, 2017-06-01](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1789073/article/26023200#26023200))
- **Is the iconography always sufficient, given cards like Mining Area versus Mining Rights?** Cards with an asterisk are an exception to the rule that icons are sufficient; for those the text must be read.  
  "Yes, cards with an asterisk are an exception." ([Jonathan, 2017-06-04](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1789073/article/26046071#26046071))
- **How do icon shapes map to game concepts?** As taught by Lord_Aethan: all square icons are resources; square icons on a brown background are production; all round icons are tags (except VP and oxygen); all hexagonal icons are tiles.  
  "1. All square icons are resources 2. Square icons on brown background are production of resources. 3. All round icons are tags (except VP and Oxygen) 4. All hexagonal icons are tiles." ([Jonathan, 2017-06-05](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1790969/article/26050753#26050753)) `intent`
- **Do card text mistranslations in other language editions change the card effects?** No: the icons are correct in all editions, so players should rely on the icons when a translated text is wrong. A later printing was expected to fix translation mistakes.  
  "I'm sorry to hear that. The icons are are correct though, so you should be fine anyway." ([Jacob, 2017-06-10](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1787926/article/26100048#26100048))
- **When the translated card text of a non-English edition contradicts the icon (e.g. text says increase M€ production but the icon shows -1), which is correct?** The icon is correct. Card and board iconography is identical in all language editions; only the black text differs, and some cards are mistranslated in non-English editions.  
  "Yes, because the card- and board iconography is the same for all language editions. The only difference is the black text." ([Jonathan, 2017-06-18](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1721911/article/26162443#26162443))
- **On a card where icons and text differ without an asterisk, which governs?** Icons are generally the stricter and clearer reading, while the text is looser and explains the function. If there is an asterisk or any question about the icons, read the text. It is a matter of whether they contradict each other without an asterisk.  
  "Ruleswise, the icons are often most clear, but if there is an asterisk or question about the icons, read the text." ([Jacob, 2021-03-21](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2624702/article/37304776#37304776))
- **Will the card icon/text conflicts be corrected?** The designers will add a few asterisks and change a couple of explanation texts in the next English printing; some of the reported conflicts are real, and some follow normal rules despite the wording.  
  "we're going to add a few asterisks and change a couple of explanation texts in the next english printing." ([Jacob, 2021-03-23](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2624702/article/37317259#37317259)) `erratum`
- **Which wins when a card's text and icons seem to differ?** An asterisk makes the card text authoritative; otherwise the text only helps explain the icons and does not replace them. If an interpretation conflicts with the icons or the text it is probably wrong.  
  "when there is an asterisk, text becomes authorative, like in this case. Otherwise, it is meant to help with understanding the icons, not replacing them." ([Jacob, 2021-07-23](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2695672/article/38140808#38140808))
- **How should card text and icons be read together?** The text is an explanation of the icons and can be imprecise; only an asterisk makes the text authoritative over the icons. If text and icons cannot be made to agree, the interpretation is probably wrong.  
  "Only an asterisk makes the text authoritative over the icons since they explain a deviation from the normal iconography. If you can't make the text and icons agree, they your interpretation is most probably wrong" ([Jacob, 2021-12-09](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2772171/article/39005315#39005315))
- **How should card text and icons be interpreted when they seem to disagree on what a card does?** The icons and the text mean the same thing; the text is only an explanation and a reminder, not the rule. If the text is unclear, check the icons, and if the icons are unclear, check the text. Icon conventions (resources, production box, red border for affecting opponents, placement rules, oxygen for greenery, TR for raising parameters) come from the rulebook. Only an asterisk in the iconography means the effect deviates from the ordinary rules, as explained in the text.  
  "if you don't understand the text, check the icons, and if you don't understand the icons, check the text. [...] Only when there is an asterisk in the iconography does the effect deviate from ordinary rules, as explained in the text." ([Jacob, 2022-01-23](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2799908/article/39314251#39314251))
- **What do wild, rainbow and asterisked resource icons mean?** The wild resource icon (white with a question mark) means any standard resource. Non-standard resources are always specified with an asterisk. The rainbow resource means different types of resources, for example 1 heat and 1 microbe. Other deviations should have asterisks explaining the effect.  
  "The convention is to use the wild resource icon (white with question mark) as any standard resource. Non-standard resources are always specified with an asterisk." ([Jacob, 2023-12-05](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3202002/article/43404497#43404497)) `intent`
- **How do card text, icons, asterisks and red borders relate?** Cards normally target only themselves and you; cards that put resources on other cards carry an asterisk, and interaction with opponents is always shown by a red border. The text is only an explanation and should follow the icons; deviations are noted with an asterisk and upper-case words in the text (e.g. Applied Science's resources on other cards that already have resources).  
  "So, in short, follow the icons. If you are unsure about the icons, read the text. If you are uncertain about the text, look at the icons; they should say the same." ([Jacob, 2023-12-05](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3202002/article/43404497#43404497)) `intent`
- **When a card's text and icons disagree, which wins?** The icons should be trusted if in doubt, because text can be incomplete or misinterpreted. Only when there is an asterisk in the icons does the text win over the icons.  
  "so go with the icons if you have any doubt. Only when there is an asterisk in the icons will the text win over the icons." ([Jacob, 2025-09-27](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3583075/article/46665948#46665948))
- **How to interpret a card when icons and text seem to disagree?** Icons represent the actual effect, except where there is an asterisk or an obviously non-standard icon, in which case check the text. The text is only an explanation and may not cover every nuance; if unsure read the text, and if the text is unclear check the icons; if they disagree your interpretation is probably wrong.  
  "The icons represent the actual effect - except when there is an asterisk or a obviously non-standard icon." ([Jacob, 2026-07-11](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1635174/article/47914513#47914513))

### Cards triggering their own effects

- **Does the same tag-trigger rule apply to Decomposers and Ecological Zone, and does Ecological Zone place 2 animals immediately for its own two matching tags?** Yes. Ecological Zone immediately places 2 animals on itself for its own two matching tags, and the same each-tag-triggers rule applies to Decomposers and Ecological Zone.  
  "Yes, that is right :)" ([Jonathan, 2016-09-12](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1621865/article/23680480#23680480))
- **Does Immigrant City's effect trigger on its own city tile?** Yes. All cards always trigger themselves, with no exceptions (like Insects counting its own microbe tag). Playing Immigrant City therefore first reduces MC production by 2 and then immediately increases it by 1 (as the post puts it), rather than making an exception.  
  "In Terraforming Mars, all cards always trigger themselves too. No exception." ([Enoch, 2016-10-18](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1655663/article/23977504#23977504))
- **Can a card's own tags trigger its own effect while it is played?** Yes. A card's tags often trigger the card's own effect while being played; e.g. Decomposers adds a resource to itself when played because of its microbe tag.  
  "For example, Decomposers adds a resource to itself when being played because of its microbe tag triggering the card effect." ([Jacob, 2017-01-10](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1705209/article/24723217#24723217))
- **What does 'including this' / 'including these two' mean on tag-triggered effects such as Viral Enhancers and Ecological Zone?** The phrase reminds you that the card triggers its own effect: Viral Enhancers (science/microbe tags) triggers on its own tag, and Ecological Zone (plant/animal tags) triggers its effect twice for its own two tags. The wording is used consistently across all cards.  
  "the text "including this" reminds you that the card triggers its own effect. Ecological Zone has plant/animal tags so the text "including these two" reminds you that the card triggers its own effect twice." ([Enoch, 2017-06-26](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1801431/article/26226747#26226747))

### Cities

- **What counts as a city, and are Ganymede Colony and Phobos Space Haven cities?** A city is a city tile (grey side of a greenery tile); the Capital special tile also counts as a city. All cities are on Mars except Ganymede Colony and Phobos Space Haven, hence the ON MARS restriction on some cards.  
  "A city in Terraforming Mars refers to city tiles, the grey ones on the back of every greenery tile. The Capital special tile is also considered a city" ([Jonathan, 2016-09-06](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1633708/article/23628320#23628320))
- **Which cards count all city tiles and which only city tiles on Mars?** The normal city icon counts all city tiles in play, on Mars or not (Energy Saving, Rover Construction, Immigrant City, Pets, Immigration Shuttles). Only cards with an asterisk at the city icon and capital-letter text count city tiles on Mars only (Zeppelins, Martian Rails, Tharsis Republic's MC production effect).  
  "the normal iconography indicates all city tiles in play, whether on Mars or not" ([Jacob, 2016-09-06](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1633708/article/23628509#23628509))

### Components

- **What is the generation marker on the terraforming rating chart for?** Its game-mechanic purpose is to know when the game ends in SOLO play (14 generations). In multiplayer it is only thematic.  
  "The main game-mechanic reason is to know when the game ends when playing SOLO(14 generations).For multiplayer it is just a nice thematic thought" ([Isaac, 2016-08-05](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1616736/article/23376288#23376288)) `prerelease`
- **What to do when a color runs out of player cubes?** Use player markers of an unused color to mark production or blue card actions; the color only really matters on the game board. Do not use copper cubes, since they can get mixed with resources on the player board.  
  "I would use player markers of an un-used color to mark your production or actions taken on blue cards. The color is only really important on the game board." ([Jacob, 2016-08-22](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1625920/article/23509489#23509489)) `prerelease`
- **Should a player's own colour cubes be used as resources on cards, and what are player markers on blue cards for?** No. Player markers are only placed on a blue action card to show the action was used this generation (removed after the generation). All resources, on player boards or on cards, are marked with the copper/silver/gold cubes.  
  "You are not supposed to use your player markers as resources on cards! You only put a player marker on a card to remember that you have used the action this turn" ([Enoch, 2016-09-05](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1632638/article/23620834#23620834))
- **What is the generation marker for?** It is a thematic add-on, necessary for solo play and good for tracking the direction of the draft.  
  "It is also necessary for the solo-play, and good to keep track on the direction of the draft." ([Enoch, 2016-10-09](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1651021/article/23909017#23909017)) `intent`
- **Does the generation marker matter for anything in the game?** It has a thematic function and helps remember the drafting direction and first player; it was included in the general rules instead of only for solo. The length of a generation is not defined.  
  "It has a thematic function, no doubt. [...] helps you to remember drafting direction and first player." ([Enoch, 2016-12-16](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1690596/article/24511405#24511405)) `intent`
- **Are there rules or card changes in the second printing?** The 2nd print has some rewording in the rules and on some cards, but nothing that actually changes the game, only clarifications. The insert is changed to a flat box-liner and promos are not included.  
  "2nd print the insert is changed to a flat one (so called box-liner) and there are some re-wording in the rules and on some cards. Nothing that actually change the game - only clarifications." ([Enoch, 2017-03-08](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1737428/article/25282507#25282507))
- **On the early board image posted online, is the card placement bonus in the southern hemisphere correct?** On the finalized board, the hex to the left of the southern-hemisphere hex with a card placement bonus also has a card as its placement bonus; the early image omits it.  
  "The hex to the left of the one with card placement bonus in the southern hemisphere should also have a card as placement bonus." ([Jonathan, 2017-03-31](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1751738/article/25500088#25500088))
- **What do you do if you run out of player cubes of your color (e.g. in a 2-player game)?** Each color has 40 cubes; 2-player games can use more than that. If you run out you can supplement with the unused colors. How to handle it is a matter of taste; only on the game board must you use your own color's markers, so other colors can be used on the production track or to mark used cards.  
  "We usually uses another colour for the production track or for marking used cards, because it's only important to use your own player markers on the game board." ([Enoch, 2017-06-29](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1803000/article/26256346#26256346))
- **What happens when you run out of player cubes?** Running out normally only happens in a 2-player game. Use an unused player color to mark production and used cards, and your own color to mark tiles and TR.  
  "use an unused player color to mark production and used cards, and your own color to mark tiles and TR." ([Jonathan, 2017-09-20](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1851477/article/26962845#26962845))
- **Are the player cubes limited, and what happens if a player runs out?** Running out is usually due to misusing player markers as resources; in a 2-player game it can still happen. Then use cubes of another unused player colour: it only matters to tell tiles on the board apart, not to mark production on your own blue cards. Each colour has 40 markers and the count will not be increased.

### Discounts and cumulative effects

- **Can stacked discounts reduce a card's cost to zero?** Yes, the cost can be zero (Indentured Workers costs 0 from the start).  
  "The cost can be zero. In fact, there is one card in the game which costs 0 from the start: Indentured Workers." ([Jonathan, 2016-08-29](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1629227/article/23561956#23561956)) `prerelease`
- **Do effects stack?** Effects are cumulative: multiple discounts stack, a card can trigger several effects and trigger things with several of its tags, and raising a parameter 2 steps increases TR 2 steps.  
  "Effects are cumulative. So multiple discounts will be very useful together, and a card can trigger several effects and may trigger stuff with several of its tags." ([Jacob, 2016-12-03](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1682744/article/24387139#24387139))
- **Are effects and discounts cumulative?** Effects are in general cumulative: a card can trigger several effects and each single effect of a card may trigger. Discounts are cumulative but a cost may not go below zero.  
  "Discounts are cumulative, but a cost may not go below zero." ([Jacob, 2017-01-10](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1705209/article/24723217#24723217))
- **Can a card cost be negative after discounts?** No. A cost may never be negative; a 1 M€ card discounted by 2 costs 0 M€.

### Drafting

- **In which direction do players draft in the draft variant, and how does the generation marker help?** The generation marker is also used in the draft variant: on even generations you draft clockwise and on odd generations counter-clockwise.  
  "It is also used when playing the draft variant; even generation you draft clockwise, and on odd generations you draft counter-clockwise." ([Jacob, 2016-08-06](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1616736/article/23379090#23379090)) `variant`
- **Did the designers try drafting the initial 10 cards?** Yes, during playtesting, and it was dropped: you need to choose freely among your cards and the two corporations to build a base; drafting at the game start increases luck instead of minimizing it.  
  "During playtesting, we have tried to draft the initial 10 cards also, but this is not good." ([Jonathan, 2016-08-20](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1624350/article/23493424#23493424)) `prerelease`
- **Should the starting hand be drafted or mulliganed when a player has few playable cards?** The designers advise against drafting the starting hand or using a mulligan: picking from 10 cards and 2 corporations is a strategic moment that drafting or a re-draw would negate, and a poor hand is less bad than it looks (use standard projects, save M€, keep cards for later). The official draft variant does not apply to setup.  
  "I advice against drafting the starting hand or using mulligan." ([Jacob, 2016-10-02](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1647488/article/23854907#23854907)) `intent`

### End of game

- **When does a multiplayer game end versus a solo game?** In solo you have 14 generations to complete the terraforming; in multiplayer the game ends with the generation in which the terraforming is complete, so length can differ from game to game. Nothing prevents playing solo longer than 14 generations casually.  
  "In solo play you do it by yourself and have 14 generations to do it, but in multiplayer the game ends with the generation when the terraforming is complete." ([Jacob, 2015-08-22](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1422198/article/20127567#20127567)) `prerelease` `house-rule-ok`
- **At the end of the game, how many times may plants be converted to greenery tiles (e.g. with 24 plants)?** Plants can be converted multiple times at the end of the game with no restriction. These tile placements also give placement bonuses, which can yield enough plants for yet another greenery.  
  "Plant can be converted multiple times at the end of the game; no restriction. These tile placements give placement bonuses too, and can result in the player getting enough plant for yet another greenery tile." ([Jacob, 2016-06-03](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1585476/article/22849316#22849316)) `prerelease`
- **Why can plants be converted to greenery after the last generation, and what does it affect?** The extra greenery placements make plant production relevant in the last generation and can affect the Landlord and Miner awards and the tie-breaker. Production of other resources can matter in the last generation for Miner, Banker and Thermalist and the tie-breaker. Greenery tiles must be placed next to your existing tile, so a player with no good spot may hold back.  
  "The main reason for the extra greenery placements is to make plant-producing cards relevant during the last generation. Also, this placement may affect the outcome for the awards Landlord and Miner, as well asthe tie-breaker" ([Jacob, 2016-06-03](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1585476/article/22852974#22852974)) `prerelease`
- **At game end, may a player convert all their plants to greenery at once, or one greenery per round of players?** The player may do as much converting as possible on their turn: e.g. with 14 plants convert 8 into a greenery, and if the placement bonus gives plants to reach 8 again, convert another, then the next player may convert. Oxygen is already maxed so no TR is gained.  
  "The player may do as much converting as possible." ([Jacob, 2016-08-15](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1622067/article/23449974#23449974)) `prerelease`
- **Can the game stall endlessly if no player wants to raise the last global parameter, and should there be a forced end?** It is up to the players whether anyone wants to end the game; there is no forced end. Incentives to end include being in the lead, gaining least from continued play, grabbing TR, tile space running out, or running out of useful cards. No limit is set for when the deck runs out.  
  "Yes, it is up to the players if anyone wants to end the game (and if no one does - what's the problem?)." ([Jacob, 2016-09-08](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1634868/article/23652998#23652998)) `intent`
- **Does the first player token move after the final production when greeneries are placed?** No. Do not shift the first player; there is no new generation.  
  "Do not shift 1st player. There is no new generation." ([Jacob, 2016-10-02](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1647565/article/23854811#23854811))
- **After the final production, do players convert plants into greeneries one at a time or all at once in player order?** Each player may do as many plant conversions as they can before the next player goes. Collect placement bonuses for those greeneries; they may give enough resources to place yet another greenery, or win the Miner award or tie-breaker.  
  "Each player may do as many conversions as they can before the next player goes." ([Jacob, 2016-10-02](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1647565/article/23854811#23854811))
- **How are greenery tiles placed from plants after the last production phase?** After the last production phase, players in the preserved player order may convert plants (8 each) into one or more greenery tiles, placed one at a time, triggering placement bonuses and other effects before the next tile. When that player can't place more, the next player goes; after everyone has had a chance, end game scoring begins.  
  "The greenery tiles are paid for (with 8 plants each) and placed one at a time, triggering placement bonuses and other effects before the next tile is placed." ([Jacob, 2017-01-10](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1705209/article/24723217#24723217))
- **When placing greenery tiles at the end of the game, are blue card effects (e.g. bonus for placing greenery) still triggered?** Yes, effects from blue cards are triggered by the end-of-game greenery placements.  
  "Yes, that's exactly what I meant. :)" ([Jacob, 2017-04-24](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1705209/article/25705102#25705102))
- **Why do final production and final greenery placement happen at the end?** Design intent: together with placement bonuses and awards such as Landlord and Miner, they make many cards interesting to play even in the last generation and can decide the game. A streamlined version without them would make the end game less interesting.  
  "This, together with associated placement bonuses and the awards, makes a lot of cards interesting to play even on the last generation." ([Jacob, 2017-10-10](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1860712/article/27131948#27131948)) `intent`
- **Does the first player place all their end-game greeneries at once or alternate with others?** Player 1 places as many greeneries as possible (can be more than 2), then the next player goes. Placement bonuses and other triggers still apply, which could give enough plants for another greenery or an award advantage.

- **What must be possible in order to play a card, and what are the exceptions?** To play a card you must be able to perform its functions, with 3 exceptions: (1) you may play a card that raises temperature, oxygen or oceans even if that parameter is already maxed out, ignoring the increase; (2) you may play a card that gives resources even if you cannot collect them (animals and microbes needing another card); (3) removing red-bordered (opposing) resources is optional.

This has 3 friendly exceptions: 1) you may play a card that increases temperature, oxygen or ocean even if that parameter is already maxed out; just ignore increasing it." - **Which effects of a card may be left unperformed when playing it?** Only three exceptions: you may play a card that (1) raises global parameters that have already reached their goal (no TR), (2) adds resources you can't collect (e.g. microbes with no microbe card to hold them), or (3) removes resources from any player (red-bordered resource icons) if you can't or don't wish to. All other effects must be performed or the card cannot be played.

### Order of effects

- **Who chooses the order when several effects or triggers happen at the same time?** The acting player chooses the order. If it is not in the action phase, the first player chooses the order.  
  "If several effects or triggers are to be performed at the same time, the acting player chooses the order. If it is not in the action phase, the first player chooses order." ([Jacob, 2017-01-10](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1705209/article/24723217#24723217))
- **In what order is a card played: reveal, requirements, payment, effects?** First reveal and declare the card, confirm requirements and pay. It is now being played; all its effects and all effects triggered by playing it with its tags wait to be resolved in the order chosen by the active player. If the player can't perform all effects (apart from the exceptions), the whole action is taken back.  
  "When playing a card, you first reveal and declare that you're playing the card, then you confirm that you meet the requirements, and you pay for the card." ([Jacob, 2017-01-10](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1705209/article/24723217#24723217))
- **In what order must a card's effects and the triggers they cause be resolved?** You may resolve effects and triggers in any order you like, as long as an effect is performed before the trigger it causes (and, e.g., a temperature step can be taken before placing the bonus ocean it triggers). Resolving triggers right away, or following the order printed on the card, is good habit but not a rule. Players should announce what they do.  
  "Performing triggers Before moving on to the next effect is good habit, but is not a rule. You choose triggers and effects in any order you choose (but must of course first perform an effect in order to get any trigger for it)." ([Jacob, 2017-01-24](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1713999/article/24860984#24860984))
- **Can you place a greenery, raise oxygen, raise temperature, place the bonus ocean and only then take the greenery's placement bonus?** Theoretically the sequence is maybe allowed as an extremely rare edge case, but the obvious thematic intent is to finish placing the greenery before resolving the triggers it caused.  
  "is an extremely rare edge case where the sequence would theoretically be allowed (maybe), but where the obvious thematic intent would be to finish with the greenery before resoving the triggers." - **In what order are payment and effects resolved when playing a card such as Giant Ice Asteroid?** Pay first (titanium and discounts may be used, but not titanium gained from playing that card; any requirement box must be met before). - **How should the order of card effects, placement bonuses and triggers be understood?** Pay first. All effects of the card go individually into a pile of effects (a card raising temperature 2 steps is 2 effects). You may perform any available effect; effects it triggers (placement bonuses, other players' bonuses) are added to the pile. Perform each effect before the effects it adds; otherwise choose order freely.  
  "So there is just a very basic chain: * Pay first. * Perform an effect before any effects added by performing that effect." ([Jacob, 2018-06-06](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1705209/article/29253888#29253888))
- **With Subterranean Reservoir, which effects can be performed before the ocean is placed?** After paying and revealing, the ocean placement and Media Group's +3 M€ are in the pile. Effects created by placing the ocean (printed placement bonuses including South Pole -6 M€, adjacency bonuses, TR, Arctic Algae) may not be performed before the ocean is actually placed. All waiting effects must be performed before another action can be taken.  
  "You may not perform any of those new effects before you actually place the ocean tile." ([Jacob, 2018-06-07](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1705209/article/29256588#29256588))
- **At what moment is a card considered played, and can Media Group's +3 M€ be taken before the event's own effects?** Order: 1) check requirements, 2) pay, 3) perform effects and triggered effects in any order, 4) place the card. A card/action is considered played the moment you've paid for it, so triggers on playing are among the card's own effects; Media Group's 3 M€ can be chosen before the event's own effects.  
  "A card/action is considered to be played the moment you've payed for it, so any trigger on that will be among the card's own effects." ([Jacob, 2018-06-10](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1705209/article/29279781#29279781))
- **When must a card's cost and requirements be satisfied relative to its effects?** The cost at the top left must be paid, and any requirement at the top of the card must be met, before any effects take place. For other actions the cost is stated before the red action arrow.  
  "The cost at the top left must be payed, and any requirement at the top of the card must be met before any effects take place." ([Jacob, 2019-03-30](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1655663/article/31621425#31621425))
- **In what order are a played card's effects and the effects it triggers performed?** Effects can be performed in any order. A card's effects and the effects triggered by its tags form a pool that must be emptied; effects triggered by performing one effect are added to the pool only once that effect is performed, so they cannot be done before it.  
  "A, B, C, d, and e are now in a pool of options that must sooner or later be emptied" ([Jacob, 2019-03-30](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1655663/article/31621425#31621425))
- **Does the player choose the order of effects of a played card (e.g. Mars University before Research draws cards) in the physical game?** Yes, in the physical game the player chooses the order of effects; the digital version differs by not letting you choose the order. The designers are pushing the programmers to correct the order in cases where it matters.  
  "there is this small difference to the physical game that you can"t choose the order of effects." ([Jacob, 2019-07-11](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2234446/article/32432377#32432377)) `inferred`
- **When playing a card, are triggers from other cards (e.g. Arctic Algae on ocean placement) resolved only after all of the played card's instructions, and what comes before effects?** Payment and requirements come first. After that the card's effects and the triggers they cause can be resolved in any order, as long as everything can be carried out.  
  "Completely correct. Payment and requirements first, then effects and triggers in any order, as long as everything can be carried out." ([Jacob, 2020-06-11](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2331247/article/35081433#35081433))
- **Can the player choose the order of effects in the digital version?** In the board game players choose the order of effects; in the digital game they cannot, and the order is programmed to be most beneficial to the player.  
  "you can't choose the order of effects in the digital version." ([Jacob, 2020-10-21](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2525032/article/36092950#36092950)) `intent`
- **Within one action, in what order are its effects (tile placement, placement bonuses, triggered effects) resolved?** Effects that an action creates (oxygen from a greenery, placement bonuses, etc.) wait and are performed in the order the player chooses, but a cause always comes before its effect, so greenery -> oxygen -> TR is a chain. Multi-step effects (gain 5 M€, raise temperature 2 steps) split into single steps, yet a trigger worded 'if 1 or more X' sees the whole effect: Sabotage's 7 M€ loss pays Mons Insurance once, not 7 times.  
  "When effects are 'created' (oxygen from greenery, placement bonuses etc.), they wait to be performed in the order the player chooses." ([Jacob, 2021-01-25](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2587413/article/36828470#36828470))
- **Are placing a greenery, raising oxygen and raising TR separable in time?** Greenery -> oxygen -> TR is a cause-effect chain and is treated as such. Effects that get 'created' (oxygen from a greenery, placement bonuses etc.) wait to be performed in whatever order the player chooses.  
  "Greenery -> oxygen -> TR is a cause-effect chain and is treated as such." ([Jacob, 2021-01-25](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2587413/article/36828470#36828470))
- **When does a card played leave to its final place, and when do its tags count?** The card leaves hand and enters a 'being played zone' where its tags count until all effects are done, then goes to its final destination (this is why tags on event cards count while being played). Tags are played at the same time as the card; effects triggering on tags are immediately added to the pile of effects.  
  "the card leaves hand and enters a 'being played zone' where its tags count until all is done and the cards go to its final destination." ([Jacob, 2021-03-21](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1705209/article/37305326#37305326))
- **If Decomposers is already in play when you play Topsoil Contract, do you get its effect right away from the played card's tags?** Correct: a card's effects turn on as it is played and then triggers fire, so you gain 1 M€ immediately. The being-played zone is a model to understand the rules, not an official rule.  
  "Correct. By the way, the BPZ is not an official rule, but rather a good model to understand the rules." ([Jacob, 2021-03-23](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1705209/article/37317147#37317147))
- **Is gaining 2 steel divisible into gaining 1 steel and 1 steel as separate effects?** No. A given amount of one resource type is not divisible; most other things are.  
  "No. A given amount of one resource type is not divisible. Otherwise most things are." ([Jacob, 2023-01-11](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2993276/article/41495901#41495901))
- **When a play triggers several effects, in what order are they resolved and can changed circumstances cancel a triggered effect?** As soon as an effect triggers it goes into a pool of pending effects. The active player chooses the order in which to resolve them; resolving one may change the situation but never removes another from the pool or removes the requirement to resolve it. If effects can't be resolved, the whole action has to be taken back (except the standard exceptions).  
  "As soon as an effect is triggered, it goes into a 'pool' of effects waiting to be resolved." ([Jacob, 2023-08-29](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3144214/article/42871880#42871880))

### Paying with steel and titanium

- **When overspending with steel or titanium, do you get change back, and can cubes be exchanged?** You can overspend with steel and titanium (e.g. 6 steel for an 11 MC card) but the surplus value is lost, so it is usually more economical to pay 5 steel and 1 MC. The cubes only represent numbers of resources and can be exchanged with the bank at any time.  
  "The rule says that you can overspend with steel and titanium, but then surplus value is lost." ([Enoch, 2016-09-05](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1632920/article/23620900#23620900))
- **Can steel or titanium be used to pay for an action (e.g. Space Mirrors) if the card has the matching tag?** No. You can pay with steel or titanium only if the action shows a steel/titanium symbol left of the red arrow; only Water Import From Europa and Aquifer Pumping allow it. Actions don't have tags, cards do, and when doing an action you pay for the action, not the card.  
  "there are only two cards which allows steel and titanium for action payment instead of M€, Water Import from Europa and Aquifer Pumping." ([Jonathan, 2016-09-12](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1636687/article/23675875#23675875))
- **Can plants pay for plant-tag cards or energy for power-tag cards like steel/titanium do?** No. Only steel and titanium have the special rule for paying for cards with building and space tags; plants and energy do not.  
  "a lot of people think you can pay with plants for plant tag cards, or energy for power tag cards. But those resources do not have that extra rule." ([Jonathan, 2016-10-31](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1663612/article/24087480#24087480))
- **Can you spend steel when building a city with the standard project?** No. Standard projects have no tags, so steel cannot be used. Helion never converts heat to money, but it may use heat as if it were money.  
  "Standard projects don't have tags, so no, you cannot use steel in this case." ([Enoch, 2017-03-10](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1705209/article/25307939#25307939))
- **Can you pay with plant or energy resources when playing a plant-tagged or energy-tagged card?** No. The only building materials are steel (for cards with a building tag) and titanium (for cards with a space tag). Apart from those, cards can only be paid for with M€.  
  "The only building material in the game are steel (for cards with building tag) and titanium (for cards with space tag). There are no other way to pay for playing cards - except MegaCredits." ([Enoch, 2017-03-15](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1745432/article/25351088#25351088))

### Placing resources on cards

- **If a card adds resources you cannot collect (no card to hold microbes), can you still play it, and must you give them to opponents?** Yes, you can still play the card and skip the part adding resources you can't collect; you are not forced to give resources to opponents.  
  "Correct." ([Jacob, 2016-09-09](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1635174/article/23656067#23656067))
- **Can resources be placed on any card?** Resources can only be placed on a card that defines what you can do with them. Decomposers can collect microbes (1 VP per 3 microbes); Viral Enhancers cannot since no function is attributed to microbes there. This avoids mixing resource types on one card.  
  "you can only place microbes on a card that defines what you can do with those microbes." ([Jacob, 2016-09-09](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1635174/article/23656067#23656067))
- **Which cards can collect resources such as microbes or animals?** Only cards that explicitly can use the resources on them (spend them in an action or score VP for them) may collect them. Viral Enhancers cannot, so the resource triggered by its microbe tag is a plant resource as stated on the card.  
  "Only cards that explicitly can use the resources on them may collect such resources." ([Jacob, 2017-01-10](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1705209/article/24723217#24723217))
- **If you play a card that puts animal tokens on another card but you have no card that can hold animals, can you still play it?** Yes. You may play the card; if you have no project that can take care of the animals, they are lost. The post does not say whether the resources must or may go onto an opponent's card.  
  "You may also bring convoys with animals to Mars. But of course, if you don't have any project that can take care of your animals, they will die and you lose those resources." ([Enoch, 2017-04-01](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1755105/article/25509849#25509849))
- **Do you need a card that can hold a resource to play a card that adds that resource to your own cards?** No. Placing resources on your own cards is optional: you may play a card that gives animals even with no animal card in play, ignore the resource, and perform the rest of the card effect as usual.  
  "Placing resources on your own cards are also optional - the rules says that you can play cards that give you animals, for example, even if you have no animal cards." ([Enoch, 2017-05-17](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1780966/article/25901031#25901031))
- **Can a card without a red border place resources on another player's card?** No. You can never place resources on other players' cards unless the effect is red-bordered; resources and production without a red border only apply to the player himself.  
  "...and you cannot ever place resources on other player's cards. Rules clearly states that resources and production without red-borders only apply to the player himself..." ([Enoch, 2017-05-17](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1780966/article/25901044#25901044))
- **On which cards can a microbe resource be placed?** Only cards that define a use for microbes can store microbe resources. These are often blue, but not all blue microbe cards can collect microbes (Viral Enhancers is an example that cannot).  
  "Only cards that define a use for microbes can store microbe resources." ([Jacob, 2017-08-05](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1682744/article/26560324#26560324))
- **Can you play a card that tells you to add a microbe to another card if you have no card that can hold microbes?** Yes. This is one of the 3 allowed exceptions where you do not have to be able to do everything the card says.  
  "If you don't have such a card you can still play your card because this is one of the 3 allowed exceptions where you don't have to be able to do everything the card says." ([Jacob, 2017-08-05](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1682744/article/26560324#26560324))
- **Can resources be placed on other players' cards when a card says to add them to another card?** No. You do not place resources on other players' cards unless the icon has a red border that allows targeting other players.  
  "You do not place resources on other players' cards, because you don't have the red border onthe icon that allows you to target other players' stuff." ([Jacob, 2017-08-05](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1682744/article/26560324#26560324))
- **Can you play a card that adds resources if you have nowhere to put them?** Yes. Placing resources on your own cards is not mandatory in order to play the card; if there is nowhere to put them, simply ignore them.  
  "Placing resources on your own cards is not mandatory in order to play the card. So you can play the card even without anywhere to put them." ([Jacob, 2017-09-25](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1854030/article/27003500#27003500))
- **Can a card with a microbe tag collect microbes?** No. Only cards that define a use for a certain resource can collect it; for example Viral Enhancers cannot collect microbes even though it is a microbe card.  
  "Only cards that define a use for certain resources can collect them. So Viral Enhancers for example, can't collect microbes even though it is a microbe card." ([Jacob, 2017-09-25](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1854030/article/27003500#27003500))
- **Is adding microbes to another card optional for cards like Imported Nitrogen?** No. All effects must be performed; adding microbes is only omitted when they cannot be added to any card.  
  "No, you have to perform all effects. Adding microbes is not optional, it is only omitted when you can't add them to any card." ([Jacob, 2018-06-10](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2007312/article/29279818#29279818))
- **When a card says to add X resources to another/any card, can the resources be divided among multiple cards?** No. All resources from one effect go to a single card, and the same applies to resources gained from trading in Colonies. For Imported Nitrogen you choose one microbe card (for all 3 microbes) and one animal card (for both animals).  
  "You can not divide the resources to different cards, and the same is true when getting resources from trading in the Colonies expansion." ([Jacob, 2018-07-10](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2022389/article/29488585#29488585))

### Production decreases

- **Can you play a card that decreases your production by 2 or more steps if you have less production of that resource?** No. All production effects must be performed to play the card, so you need that production first. If the decrease targets opposing production (red border on the resource icon), someone must have that production to reduce, and you may choose to target yourself.  
  "All production effects MUST be performed in order to play the card, so yes, you need that production first." ([Jacob, 2014-11-12](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1268377/article/17443336#17443336)) `prerelease`
- **Why must a production decrease be possible before a card can be played?** A production decrease on a card usually represents the card's project converting that resource into another (e.g. energy into oxygen or steel, plants into animals, your money into someone else's money for Hackers). The decrease must be met because it simplifies doing the conversion manually every turn.  
  "So the production decrease must be met because it is a simplification from doing the conversion manually every turn." ([Jacob, 2014-11-12](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1268377/article/17444856#17444856)) `prerelease`
- **Can you play a card whose immediate effect would lower production below zero (or an already-zero track)?** No. You must fulfil the requirement and be able to perform the card's effects (rulebook pages 9 and 7). A card cannot be played if its production reduction would take your production below zero (below -5 for MC).  
  "No, you cannot play a card that lower production below zero (or below -5 for MC)." ([Isaac, 2016-08-18](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1623938/article/23482718#23482718)) `prerelease`
- **What if a card reduces any player's production (red-bordered production) and the opponent has none?** A red-bordered production reduction must be performed, so if the opponent does not have that production you must lower your own production or not play the card at all (rulebook page 15).  
  "A card effect that reduces production for any player (red-bordered production of a resource) must be performed, so if your opponent doesn’t have that production, then you must lower your own production or not play the card at all." ([Isaac, 2016-08-18](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1623938/article/23482718#23482718)) `prerelease`
- **Is the summary 'production reduction is mandatory, red-bordered or not; red-bordered resource reduction is optional' correct?** Yes, a very good summary. Basically all effects of a card need to be performed; the exception is that you can still play asteroids and comets even if there are no plants to hit.  
  "Basically, all effects of a card needs to be performed. The exception is that you can still smash asteroids and comets to the surface even if you cannot find any plants to land them on." ([Enoch, 2016-08-26](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1623938/article/23544490#23544490)) `prerelease`
- **Can you play a card that decreases a production you don't have?** No. You must perform all effects on a card to play it (except the exceptions in the rules), so if the card lowers a production you can't lower, you can't play it.  
  "You always need to perform all effects on a card to be able to play it." ([Enoch, 2016-09-06](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1633538/article/23626890#23626890))
- **Can you play a card that makes you lose more production than you have (e.g. -2 energy production with 1)?** No.  
  "Haha, well, an easy question deserves an easy answer :)" ([Jonathan, 2016-10-22](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1658831/article/24019112#24019112)) `inferred`
- **Is reducing production, or one's own resources, on a card mandatory?** Yes. Reducing production (your own or a red-bordered target) is always mandatory, and if you cannot, you cannot play the card. Reducing your own (non red-bordered) resources is also mandatory. This is endorsed as correct.  
  "The basic rule is that you have to perform everything on the card" ([Enoch, 2016-11-03](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1665700/article/24119589#24119589))
- **If a card would decrease a production you have at 0 (e.g. energy), can it be played?** No.  
  "No" ([Enoch, 2017-03-27](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1705209/article/25459654#25459654))
- **Why must production decreases be performed (even red-bordered ones) while removing resources from opponents is optional?** A production decrease simulates a cost for the project and must be performed; a 'consortium' effect means someone else loses market share. One-time resource losses that can target opponents are not costs but optional effects, performed in full, in part, or not at all.  
  "If the production decrease simulates a cost for the project, it must of course be performed for the project to work." ([Jacob, 2019-11-29](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2239751/article/33477009#33477009))

### Red border icons

- **Do permanent effects on blue (active) cards affect everyone or only the owner?** An icon with a red border can target other players (here: trigger when other players act) as well as yourself. Example: Rover Construction gives YOU 2 M€ (no red border on the M€ icon) whenever ANYONE, including you, places a city tile (red border on the city icon).  
  "If an icon has a red border, it can target other players (in this case trigger when other players do stuff) as well as yourself." ([Jacob, 2017-03-08](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1740523/article/25282212#25282212))
- **When a card adds animals/microbes, must you add them to other players' cards, and which icons can target other players?** An icon with a red border may target other players, but an icon without a red border always targets yourself. (In reply to the question, resources are placed only on cards you own.)  
  "An icon with a red border may target other players, but an icon without red border always targets yourself." ([Isaac, 2017-03-14](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1745065/article/25343753#25343753))
- **What does the red border on an icon mean (optional effect, reduction, target another player)?** The red border does not mean the effect is optional, does not mean something is reduced, and does not mean you must target another player. It simply means the effect can target any player (you or an opponent). Production decreases of red-bordered resources must still be performed, so you lower your own if no opponent has it, or do not play the card.  
  "The red border simply means that the effect can target ANY player." ([Jonathan, 2018-03-14](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1957397/article/28542533#28542533))
- **What does a red border around an effect icon mean?** A red border means the effect has a greater scope than only your own play area, i.e. it can target other players and their cards. It does not mean the effect is negative.  
  "The iconography used to show that an effect can target other players and their cards is to put a red border around the icon." ([Jonathan, 2018-08-27](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1708007/article/29865470#29865470))
- **Can an icon without a red border target another player's cards (e.g. Extreme Cold Fungus adding microbes)?** No. The absence of red borders always means you only target yourself; if the text says 'any card' but the icon has no red border it means any of your cards. A red border opens it up to other players and the text becomes more important.  
  "The absence of red borders always means that you only target yourself." ([Jacob, 2026-07-11](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1635174/article/47914513#47914513))

### Removing and stealing resources

- **Can a negative effect that removes resources be split between several players?** No. For simplicity each negative effect can only target one player. Iconography usually shows separate icons for up to three resources and a single icon with a number for higher values (exceptions for M€ and crowded cards).  
  "for simplicity, each negative effect can only target one player." ([Jonathan, 2016-08-11](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1619561/article/23418750#23418750)) `prerelease`
- **Do removed resources go back to the supply or are they stolen?** They return to the supply unless the card says 'steal' (as Hired Raiders does). Remove means no one gets them.  
  "Yes, unless it says "steal", as it does on for example "Hired Raiders". Remove means that no-one gets them." ([Jonathan, 2016-08-18](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1619561/article/23480375#23480375)) `prerelease`
- **Must attacking effects (removing resources, reducing production) be performed to play the card?** Effects that remove resources need not be performed; you may play the card and skip them (balance assumes that). Effects that reduce other players' production must be performed, though you may reduce your own production instead.  
  "You don't have to perform this effect in order to play the card. ... they have to be performed. You may reduce your own production instead" ([Jonathan, 2016-09-12](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1636330/article/23680577#23680577))
- **Can a card that removes other players' resources target several players?** There is a special rule: cards that affect other players' resources can only target one player, yet any player, not just yourself.  
  "There is a special rule concerning cards that affect other players' resources. Such cards can only target one player, yet still any player, not just yourself." ([Jonathan, 2016-10-05](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1649248/article/23881532#23881532))
- **Is removing red-bordered (anyone's) resources mandatory?** No: removing red-bordered resources is optional; you may remove a smaller amount or none, but if you remove any you must target a single player and cannot split the effect. This exception exists for thematic reasons (e.g. asteroids hitting plants).  
  "Therefore, we had to make an exception for removing red-bordered resources (usually asteroids killing plants)." ([Enoch, 2016-11-03](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1665700/article/24119589#24119589))
- **Which resource removal effects are mandatory and which are optional?** Effects that remove your own resources are mandatory. Effects that remove resources from any player (red border around the icon) are voluntary and may be performed in full, in part or not at all. Only one player or card may be targeted at a time with the effects of a card. Removing production is always mandatory.  
  "Effects that remove your own resources are mandatory, while effects that remove resources from any player (marked by a red border around the resource icon) are voluntary" ([Jacob, 2017-01-10](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1705209/article/24723217#24723217))
- **A card that removes plants from an opponent shows two symbols: is it two effects, and can the targets differ?** Destroying plants is a single effect and specifically targets one opponent, so you cannot split the removal between opponents.  
  "Destroying plants is one effect and it specifically states that you target one opponent." ([Jacob, 2017-01-24](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1713999/article/24860984#24860984))
- **Are take-that effects optional?** Removing resources from other players is always optional. Removing production is mandatory, but you may choose to remove your own production, so all take-that cards are technically optional.  
  "removing resources from other players is always optional, but removing production is mandatory. However, you could remove your own production" ([Jonathan, 2017-03-05](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1738869/article/25254470#25254470))
- **When playing a card, may you ignore removing red-bordered resources (for example when no one has any to remove)?** Effects that happen when you play a card must be performed in order to play it, but the rules state exceptions: you may ignore removing red-bordered resources.  
  "You may ignore removing red-bordered resources." ([Enoch, 2017-04-01](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1755105/article/25509849#25509849))
- **When a card says to take resources from another player, do you gain them or do they go to the supply?** The only time you take resources from another player and add them to your own is when the card specifically says STEAL. If it is just a red-bordered icon, the taken resources go to the supply. Predators and Ants are technically not stealing since the card also tells you to gain a resource; Asteroids do not steal plants, only destroy them.  
  "The ONLY time you take resources from another player and add them to your own is when the card specifically says 'STEAL'." ([Jacob, 2017-08-30](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1839646/article/26782822#26782822))
- **Does protection against removing resources (e.g. plants) also block stealing them?** Yes. Stealing involves removing, so it is blocked by protection. Stealing is also a package deal: you cannot remove fewer resources and still gain the full amount, nor steal less than the stated amount.  
  "Stealing involves removing and is therefore blocked." ([Jacob, 2024-12-01](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3415045/article/45269699#45269699)) `inferred`

### Requirements

- **When a card requires tiles, production or tags, do only your own count or everyone's?** Temperature, oxygen and ocean are global and owned by no one. For any other tile, production or tag requirement only YOUR tiles/production/tags count unless the icon has a red border, in which case everything in play counts. Both Interstellar Colony Ship and Lightning Harvest count only your own science tags.  
  "global parameters are the same for everyone, normal black-bordered icons counts only your stuff and red-bordered icons count all player's stuff." ([Jacob, 2015-03-19](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1332971/article/18694297#18694297)) `prerelease`
- **Can a card use its own tags to meet its own tag requirement?** No. A card cannot use its own tags to meet its requirements.  
  "A card cannot use its own tags to meet its requirements." ([Jacob, 2015-03-19](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1332971/article/18694297#18694297)) `prerelease`
- **How are minimum and maximum requirements shown on cards?** All maximum requirements have 'max' in the requirement box and a red background. All other requirements are minimum requirements and have a yellow background in the requirement box; 'min' is not printed because most requirements are minimum.  
  "All maximum requirements have a 'max' in the requirement box and have a red background color in the box. All other requirements are minimum requirements" ([Jacob, 2016-08-29](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1268377/article/23563150#23563150)) `prerelease`
- **Does a requirement like '3 oceans' mean exactly 3 or at least 3?** All requirements are minimum unless explicitly written otherwise. Tag requirements are the same. Temperature uses the warmer and colder wording to avoid confusion with negative numbers.  
  "All requirements are minimum unless explicitly written otherwise." ([Jonathan, 2016-10-17](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1653775/article/23967800#23967800))
- **Does a card with a Max requirement stop working once the parameter passes that value?** No. Requirements are only applied when a card is played; the card does not deactivate later (an early prototype did, but it was dropped as too fiddly).  
  "Requirements are only applied when a card is played." ([Jonathan, 2016-10-27](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1661827/article/24062792#24062792))
- **Can an already played card with a requirement (e.g. Search for Life's max oxygen) still be used after the parameter passes the requirement?** Yes. Requirements apply only when the card is played from hand; once the card is in play, ignore its requirements just as you ignore its cost.  
  "The rules state that the requirements (upper-left on the cards) only applies when you play the card from your hand." ([Enoch, 2016-11-17](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1657424/article/24244464#24244464))
- **Is a requirement without 'max' a minimum or an exact requirement?** A requirement box that doesn't explicitly say 'max' is a minimum requirement; e.g. 'requires 3 oceans' means at least 3 oceans. There are no exact requirements.  
  "There are no ‘exact requirements’." ([Jacob, 2017-01-10](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1705209/article/24723217#24723217))
- **Can you still use a card (e.g. a technology card) after its requirement has been exceeded?** Yes. Requirements only apply when you play the card from your hand; once the card is in play it stays in play.  
  "The requirements only apply when you play the card from your hand. Once the card is in play, it stays in play..." ([Enoch, 2017-03-02](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1705209/article/25228994#25228994))
- **If a card says 'requires X' without a max/min qualifier, can it only be played at exactly that parameter value?** No: there are no cards with exact requirements; all are either minimum or maximum requirements.  
  "So there are no cards in the game with exact requirements - all are either minimum or maximum requirements." ([Enoch, 2017-03-21](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1705209/article/25412602#25412602))
- **Are requirements written as 'Requires 7% oxygen' exact values, unlike 'must be -18C or colder' or '7% or less'?** There are no exact requirements. If 'max' is not stated it is a minimum. On temperature, 'or colder' / 'or warmer' is added in parentheses because negative numbers make 'more' ambiguous.  
  "there are no exact requirements. If "max" is not stated, it is minimum." ([Jonathan, 2018-01-09](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1915789/article/27911990#27911990)) `inferred`
- **Is a printed global requirement (e.g. 2% oxygen, 4 oceans) a minimum or an exact value?** Per the rulebook, any requirement must be met to play the card: a global parameter must have reached the stated level, while cards marked as maximum (e.g. oxygen 9% or lower) can only be played while the parameter is still low. It is not an exact value.  
  "Any requirement must be met in order to play the card. Some cards require a global parameter to have reached a certain level, while others can only be played while the parameter is still low" ([Jonathan, 2018-03-22](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1962292/article/28623817#28623817)) `inferred`
- **Can tags played during the same action meet a card's requirements, and must all effects be doable before the action?** Requirements must be met before you begin the action, so tags played during the same action cannot satisfy them. This differs from effects: you do not need to be able to perform all effects before the action, but you must find a way to do them during the action (compare placing a tile on the south pole on Hellas).  
  "Requirements work a little differently, because it requires a certain condition to be met before you even begin the action, and so tags being played during the same action can't be used to meet requirements." ([Jacob, 2018-11-15](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2096075/article/30501757#30501757))
- **Do tag requirements on cards count only your own tags, or all players' tags?** Requirements count only your own tags (and anything you own). For a card to count other players' tags or anything they own, its icons have a red border; without the red border only your own are counted.  
  "These cards only count your own tags. For a card to count other people's tags , or indeed anything they own, the icons would have a red border." ([Jacob, 2025-09-27](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3583075/article/46665948#46665948))

- **Can non-ocean tiles (e.g. Mining Rights) be placed on areas reserved for oceans, and what if all are used?** Only oceans may be placed on areas reserved for ocean; only three cards specifically allow an exception to this rule (not named in the post). Mining Rights may not be placed on an ocean-reserved area.  
  "The rules say only oceans may be placed on areas reserved for ocean, and only three cards specifically allow exception for this rule (as mentioned above)." ([Jacob, 2016-10-02](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1647322/article/23853277#23853277))
- **How many areas are reserved and what can be placed there?** There are 12 blue areas reserved for oceans and 3 areas reserved for specific cities. No other tiles may be placed there unless specifically stated.

### Shortening the game

- **Can the game length be shortened by lowering global parameter targets?** House rules are allowed, but changing global parameters upsets card balance since card costs account for requirements. The standard game with starting production is designed as an epic 2-hour game for any player count; if 2-player Corporate Era drags, play the standard game.  
  "Of course you can houserule anything you want, but you should know that messing with global parameters also messes with the balance of the card requirement." ([Enoch, 2016-09-20](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1639607/article/23748161#23748161)) `intent` `house-rule-ok`
- **Is there an official variant that shortens the game?** Suggested variant: at the end of each generation, before the new generation begins and before player order shifts, the current first player chooses 1 global parameter (ocean/oxygen/temperature) that is increased. The first player gets no TR or placement bonuses for this, and any bonus steps on the tracks are ignored.  
  "At the end of each generation, the current first player chooses 1 global parameter (ocean/oxygen/temp) that will be increased. This happens before the new generation begins and before player order shifts." ([Enoch, 2017-03-02](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1737666/article/25230535#25230535)) `variant`
- **Was the end-of-generation parameter-increase variant meant for the base game or for an expansion, and can it be used with Corporate Era?** The variant was intended for a later expansion, to compensate for the added game length expansions bring. There is no reason it cannot be used in a Corporate Era game if it feels too long.  
  "Actually, this variant was intended for an expansion later on. Expansions tend to add time to the game and so we came up with this mechanism to compensate for that." ([Enoch, 2017-03-03](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1737666/article/25243467#25243467)) `variant`
- **What is the official way to shorten a game?** The official shorter game is the basic game: start without the Corporate Era cards and with +1 on every production. Prelude was designed to roughly replace that production boost but can be added on top for a really short game.  
  "There is the official way to speed up the game, called the basic game: start without the corporate era cards and with +1 on every production." ([Jacob, 2021-09-20](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2727902/article/38491087#38491087))
- **Which official modular options shorten game time?** Any combination of: Prelude (fewer generations, about 15 min shorter), WGT / solar phase (fewer generations), Basic game (remove Corporate Era cards and start at +1 production of everything; about 1 h shorter), and not drafting (saves about 30 min). Solar phase was added to offset Venus lengthening play but can be used in any setting.  
  "Basic game (remove Corporate Era cards and start at +1 production of everything): fewer generations, ca 1 h shorter." ([Jacob, 2023-03-30](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3056436/article/42004296#42004296)) `variant`

### Spending or discarding zero

- **Can you discard 0 cards for an effect that lets you discard cards?** No. It is impossible to discard 0 cards: if no card leaves your hand to the discard pile, you are not discarding.  
  "it is impossible to discard 0 cards. If no card leaves your hand to the discard pile, you're not discarding." ([Jonathan, 2016-10-17](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1653775/article/23967800#23967800))
- **Is there a difference between 'pay' and 'spend', and can you pay or spend zero?** There is no in-game difference between 'pay' and 'spend'. Paying or spending zero is not paying or spending; thematically you cannot pay zero. Allowed exceptions like spending 8 heat at maxed temperature or using Symbiotic Fungus without taking the microbe are accepted despite this.  
  "There is no in-game difference between 'pay' and 'spend'." ([Jacob, 2020-12-16](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2559952/article/36521772#36521772))
- **Can 'X' in 'spend any number of' actions (e.g. Power Infrastructure) be zero?** No. X is always a positive number. A determined effect with a count (such as 'pay 1 per Z', Insects without plant tags, or Turmoil payouts) may evaluate to zero.  
  "So, to summarize, 'X' is always a positive number." ([Jacob, 2020-12-16](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2559952/article/36521772#36521772))

- **Where does the +1 MC production of the City standard project come from?** Page 10 of the rulebook: for 25 M€ the City standard project places a city tile (collecting placement bonus, with a player marker) and increases M€ production 1 step; the icon is on the game board's standard projects panel.  
  "6) City: For 25 M€ you get to place a city tile (collect any placement bonus for the tile, and place a player marker on it). You also get to increase your M€ production 1 step." ([Jonathan, 2016-09-05](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1619813/article/23619962#23619962))
- **Do standard projects share a generic city effect with cards that place cities?** No. Standard projects are effects of their own, like simple cards you can play over and over, and do not affect other card effects. What all city tiles share is that they can't be adjacent to other cities (unless a card says otherwise) and score 1 VP per adjacent greenery.  
  "You should treat the standard projects as if they were simple cards that you can play over and over again. They do not affect other card effects in any way." ([Jonathan, 2016-09-05](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1619813/article/23621046#23621046))
- **Does placing a city tile from a card increase M€ production by 1 like the City standard project does?** No. You get no extra M€ production for placing a city tile; the +1 M€ production comes from performing the 'City' standard project. The 'see page 5' reference in the rulebook is misplaced.  
  "You do not get any extra M€ production for placing a city tile - you get it from performing the 'standard action city'." ([Isaac, 2016-10-02](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1647359/article/23852133#23852133))
- **Can standard projects printed on the board be used several times per generation?** Yes, standard projects printed on the board may be used several times. The post cites spending 8 heat to raise temperature as an example, but the same author's next post says standard projects are only the actions printed on the board.  
  "Standard projects (printed on the board) may be used several times, for example spending 8 heat to raise temperature if possible (and get TR)." ([Enoch, 2016-11-08](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1668655/article/24160065#24160065)) `reversed`
- **Do cities placed by cards give the extra MC production that the City standard project gives?** No. The extra MC production is only for building a city as the standard project (action), not for cities from cards.  
  "The extra MC-production for doing the standard action city is only for building a city as a standard action. You DONT get extra MC-production for building cities from cards." ([Enoch, 2016-11-08](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1668655/article/24161389#24161389))
- **Can MC be spent on the City standard project when there are no legal placements left?** No. The City standard project cannot be used when there is no legal space to place the city.  
  "Not if there are no more legal placements." ([Jonathan, 2016-11-20](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1674504/article/24266767#24266767))
- **Can you use the City standard project for the income if you cannot place the tile?** No. You cannot do a standard city project if you cannot place the tile.  
  "You can not do a standard city to get the income if you can't place the tile." ([Jacob, 2016-12-03](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1682744/article/24387139#24387139))
- **Can you pay for the Aquifer standard project when all oceans are placed?** Yes, you can pay 18 for the standard Aquifer even if oceans are maxed, but you get nothing.  
  "You can pay 18 for a standard Aquifer even if ocean is maxed, but you get nothing." ([Jacob, 2016-12-03](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1682744/article/24387139#24387139))
- **Do cards that place a city also give the +1 M€ production the city standard project gives?** No. The +1 M€ production is not a general rule for placing cities; it only happens when you use the standard project to place a city. For a card that places a city, read the card effect.  
  "Getting +1 MC production is not a general rule for placing cities - it's just an effect that happens when you use the STANDARD PROJECT to place a city!" ([Enoch, 2017-07-23](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1705209/article/26446871#26446871)) `inferred`
- **Can you pay for the city standard project just to raise M€ production when no city can be placed?** No. Effects on cards, standard projects, card actions etc. are mandatory except maxed global parameters, resources you can't collect, and opposing resources. There is no exception for tiles other than maxed ocean tiles.  
  "Effects on cards, standard Projects, card actions etc. are mandatory, except for * maxed global parameters, * resources you can't collect, and * opposing resources. No exception for tiles other than maxed ocean tiles." ([Jacob, 2018-01-08](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1915682/article/27901395#27901395))
- **When are standard project effects resolved, and when is a standard project considered played (relevant to Thorgate's discount)?** Standard projects work like every other action: check requirements, pay the cost, then perform the effects and triggered effects in any order. The same applies to converting plants, claiming a milestone, or using a blue card action. A standard project, like a card, is not considered played until paid for.  
  "all actions work the same way: Check the requirements, pay the cost (if any), and perform the effects and triggered effects in any order." ([Jacob, 2019-02-25](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2157051/article/31336207#31336207))

### Tags

- **When do tags (including those on corporations) count?** Tags count for card effects based on existing tags (examples given: Satellites, Medical Lab, Cartel, Miranda Resort), for card tag requirements (usually science), and for the Builder milestone and Scientist award.  
  "Some cards have effects based on existing tags. That's when they count. Examples: satellites, medical lab, cartel, Miranda resort." ([Jonathan, 2016-08-10](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1619256/article/23414208#23414208)) `prerelease` `inferred`
- **If you play Advanced Ecosystems (animal, plant and microbe tags) with Decomposers in play, do you add 3 microbes?** Yes. Each tag is counted separately: effects trigger on playing a tag, not a card, and one card with several tags plays each tag.  
  "Each tag is indeed counted separately, that is why refer to playing a tag instead of playing a card" ([Jonathan, 2016-10-06](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1648861/article/23884554#23884554))
- **Do tags have rules of their own, and are there steel or titanium tags?** Tags (round icons except oxygen) are keywords with no rules of their own; they only matter when referenced by other effects. There are no steel or titanium tags: the building tag lets steel pay for part or all of a card (each steel substitutes 2 MC) and the space tag likewise for titanium. Resources are square icons, tags round, tiles hexagon, red-bordered resources can affect any player, and a brown box means production.  
  "Tags have NO RULES applied to them. They do nothing on their own. They are only referenced by other effects." ([Jonathan, 2016-10-31](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1663612/article/24087480#24087480))
- **What does a card with a Microbe tag let you do; do tags have rules?** Tags have no rules applied to them; they are thematic keywords that other cards or effects (or the player board, e.g. steel for building tags) may reference. A Microbe tag does not let you pay with microbes.  
  "Tags have no rules at all applied to them. That's why you won't find anything about them in the rulebook." ([Jonathan, 2017-03-15](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1745432/article/25350055#25350055))
- **When a card has several matching tags (Advanced Ecosystems with GMO Contract; Research with Valley Trust), does each tag trigger the effect?** Yes. Each tag triggers the effect and effects are generally cumulative: Valley Trust plays Research for 4 MC less, and Advanced Ecosystems with Viral Enhancers in play gives 3 plants (so GMO Contract would give 2 MC per tag).  
  "Each tag triggers the effect, and effects are generally cumulative, so that Valley Trust can play Research for 4 mc less." ([Jacob, 2020-05-03](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2420703/article/34719990#34719990)) `inferred`
- **Can a wild tag be designated as an event tag?** Yes. A wild tag can be an event tag or any other valid tag, including Venus if playing with Venus Next. A tag is only valid if at least 1 other card in the deck has it.  
  "Yes, it can be an event tag. Or any other valid tag, including Venus if you play with that. At least 1 ither card in the deck needs to have the tag for it to be valid." ([Jacob, 2024-05-02](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3293976/article/44205387#44205387))
- **Do Media Group and Media Archives work with a wild tag set to the event tag?** Media Group does not trigger since the tag is not played. Media Archives counts cards in the event pile, not tags, so a wild tag doesn't help it; the event icon was only reused for simplicity.  
  "Media Group doesn't trigger since the tag is not played. Media Archives counts cards in the event pile, not tags since those tags are not in play." ([Jacob, 2024-05-02](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3293976/article/44205759#44205759))
- **Can a wild tag be chosen to be a wild tag, and what is its state if no choice is made?** No: a wild tag is not itself a tag, so it can't be chosen as a wild tag. If you don't choose anything it counts as no tag, which is also the state it reverts to at the end of each action.  
  "A wild tag is not itself a tag so you can't choose a wild tag to be a wild tag, and if you don't choose anything for it, it counts as no tag." ([Jacob, 2024-05-02](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3293976/article/44205759#44205759))

### Tags on event cards

- **Do tags on event cards trigger effects?** Yes, all tags on all events can trigger effects when the event is played: building and space tags allow paying with steel/titanium (no building events exist), microbe tags trigger Decomposers and Viral Enhancers, science tags trigger Olympus Conference and Mars University, Earth tags get a discount from Earth Office and Teractor.  
  "All tags on all events can trigger some effect." ([Jonathan, 2016-09-01](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1631324/article/23596645#23596645))
- **Do the science tags on red event cards count for the end-of-game award for most science tags?** No. Tags on events only matter when the event is played (combos, discounts, effect triggers); afterwards they do not count.  
  "Tags on events only allow for combos when they are played, discounts and effect triggers." ([Jonathan, 2016-09-30](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1646338/article/23837331#23837331))
- **Do science tags on a played event count for the Scientist award or science requirements, or trigger Mars University and Olympus Conference?** Events are turned face down after being played and their tags only apply when being played. They do not count for the Scientist award or science tag requirements, but they do trigger Mars University and Olympus Conference.  
  "So they would not count for the Scientist Award or science tag requirements, but they do trigger Mars University and Olympus Conference." ([Jacob, 2016-10-02](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1647456/article/23853310#23853310))
- **Does playing an event with a Jovian tag count as putting a Jovian tag into play for Saturn Systems?** Tags on event cards do not enter play; they are only triggered while the card is being played. Event science tags therefore do not count for requirements or the Scientist award, though they trigger Mars University and Olympus Conference when played. (There are no Jovian events.)  
  "Tags on event cards do not enter play, but are only triggered while playing the card." ([Jacob, 2016-10-03](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1648217/article/23864008#23864008))
- **Do tags on played event cards count for tag requirements and end-game scoring?** No. Tags on events are played, triggering effects at that moment (science tags trigger Olympus Conference and Mars University), but the events never really enter play, so they do not count towards tag requirements or end-game scoring; e.g. Invention Contest's science tag does not count toward the Scientist award.  
  "Tags on events do NOT count for end-game scoring." ([Jonathan, 2016-10-22](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1658813/article/24019107#24019107))
- **Do tags on event cards count after the event is played?** Tags on event cards are only active when you play it, not after you have placed it face down, and not even during scoring when potential VPs are considered.  
  "The tags on event cards (red cards) are only active when you play it, not after you've placed it face down. Not even during scoring when their potential VPs are considered." ([Jacob, 2016-12-03](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1682744/article/24387139#24387139))
- **Do tags on event cards count?** They are active and may trigger effects while the event is being played, but are not counted afterwards, neither during the game nor at end game scoring (they may still give end game VP if they have a VP icon).  
  "They are active and may trigger effects while being played, but are not counted after this" ([Jacob, 2017-01-10](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1705209/article/24723217#24723217))
- **Do tags on event cards count after the event has been played, for example for end-game VP cards such as Jovian-tag scoring?** Event cards only apply their tags while being played, then are turned face down. Afterwards events are only considered for their own additional VP (or negative VP) in end-game scoring, nothing else, so their tags do not count for tag-based scoring.  
  "Eventcards only apply their tags while being played. Then they are turned face down." ([Enoch, 2017-04-19](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1751741/article/25662113#25662113))
- **Do tags on event cards count at final scoring (e.g. for cards giving VP per Jovian tag, or the Scientist award)?** No. Tags on event cards do not count during final scoring; e.g. science tags on events do not count towards the Scientist award. There are no Jovian events, and all Jovian cards also have VPs.  
  "tags on event cards do not count during final scoring. Science tags on events (those do exist) do not count towards the Scientist award, for example." ([Jacob, 2017-11-19](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1886676/article/27477602#27477602))
- **Do the tags of face-down (played) red Event cards count for requirements and end-game scoring?** No. Event cards are turned face down because they represent something that happened and is not a continuing part of your corporation (its tags, productions and abilities); their tags therefore do not count. The face-down convention has a thematic, not mechanical, origin.  
  "They are face down because they represent something that happened and is not a continuing part of your corporation (represented by its tags, productions, and abilities). Their tags don't count for the exact same reason" ([Jacob, 2019-11-25](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2319085/article/33450989#33450989))
- **Is the event symbol a tag, e.g. for Sagitta and Spire counting tags?** Yes. The event tag is just like other tags; it happens to be on red cards that go face down in your event pile after being played. Their tags count while the card is being played. Fryxen calls the older ruling bad, at least the part about a wild tag not being able to be an event tag.  
  "The event tag is just like other tags, they just happen to be on red cards that rule that they are placed face down in your event pile after they have been played. Their tags count while the card is being played." ([Jacob, 2024-05-02](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3293976/article/44207247#44207247))
- **Do tags on played event cards count for requirements such as 'you need 2 science tags'?** Event cards keep their tags, but those tags are only relevant while the event is being played. Events represent things that happen and are done, unlike ongoing projects. (The answer to later requirement checks is implied: they don't count.)  
  "When you play an event, it is still relevant whether it involves space, microbes, science etc, so they can have those tags, but they are only relevant while playing the card." ([Jacob, 2025-12-31](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3636228/article/47087464#47087464)) `hedged`

### Thermalist (award)

- **Why does Thermalist count heat resources rather than heat production?** Design intent: heat production depends on the cards drawn (tactics and luck), while heat resources reflect long-term choices, and for a 5 VP award they prefer the winner to be decided by strategy.  
  "heat production is only based on the cards you get (tactical choices and luck), while heat resources are affected by your long-term choices (strategy)." ([Jonathan, 2016-11-22](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1674504/article/24291041#24291041)) `intent`

### Tile placement

- **Do cards always follow the normal tile placement rules?** No. Many cards disregard normal placement rules, e.g. Moholes places a tile that must be placed on an area reserved for ocean, and Urbanized Area places a city tile that has to be adjacent to 2 other cities.  
  "many cards disregard normal placement rules to allow for a more thematic gameplay, such as the card Moholes, which places a tile that must be placed on an area reserved for ocean, or Urbanized Area, which places a city tile which has to be adjacent to 2 other cities." ([Jonathan, 2016-02-23](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1530933/article/21905316#21905316)) `prerelease` `inferred`
- **Can you play a card that places a tile when there is no legal placement for it?** No. Errata to the rulebook: any tiles placed by the card must have legal placements, or you cannot play the card. The exception is ocean tiles after they run out, since effects raising the ocean parameter can be ignored when it is maxed.  
  "ERRATA: Any tiles placed by the card must have legal placements, or you can't play the card." ([Jacob, 2016-09-01](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1268377/article/23595125#23595125)) `erratum`
- **Do special tiles act like greenery or have functions of their own?** Special tiles are completely without function or special rules (except Capital counts as a city). They are not cities, oceans or greeneries, have no placement restrictions, give no VP, TR or other bonuses, only the placement bonuses of the space; special rules are on the card that placed them.  
  "Special tiles are tiles completely without function or special rules (except Capital counts as a city). They are not cities, oceans or greeneries." ([Jacob, 2016-09-01](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1628076/article/23595527#23595527))
- **Can you play a card that places a tile when you cannot place the tile?** No. You cannot play a card that places a tile if you actually cannot place the tile.  
  "you can't play a card that places a tile if you actually can't place the tile." ([Jacob, 2016-12-03](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1682744/article/24387139#24387139)) `erratum`
- **Does a card need to be able to place its stated tile to be played (rulebook p. 10 1st edition)?** Erratum: the rulebook p. 10 exception that you don't need to be able to place a stated tile is wrong. Any tile placed by the card must be placed, unless it is an ocean tile and all oceans are already placed.  
  "Page 10 in the rulebook states another exception; that you don’t need to be able to place a stated tile. This is wrong" ([Jacob, 2017-01-10](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1705209/article/24723217#24723217)) `erratum`
- **Can a tile be placed on top of another tile?** No. A tile can never be placed on top of another tile.  
  "A tile can never be placed on top of another tile." ([Jacob, 2017-01-10](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1705209/article/24723217#24723217))
- **When a card lets you place two tiles (e.g. two oceans), are they placed one after another or simultaneously, and can you see a drawn card before placing the second?** Every icon is a separate effect. The tiles are placed one after the other, so the second ocean can be adjacent to the first and score its adjacency bonus, and you may see cards drawn from the first tile's placement bonus before placing the second.  
  "All icons are separate effects, so you Place the first ocean tile, then the next and so forth." ([Jacob, 2017-01-24](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1713999/article/24860984#24860984))
- **What are the basic tile placement rules for cities, greeneries and oceans?** Cities cannot be placed next to other cities. Greeneries must be placed next to another tile you own, if possible. Oceans must be placed on areas reserved for oceans, and non-ocean tiles may not be placed there. Noctis City, Ganymede Colony and Phobos Space Haven have their own appointed spaces where only those tiles may be placed.  
  "Greeneries must be placed next to another tile that you own, if possible." ([Enoch, 2017-08-25](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1705209/article/26739887#26739887))
- **Can a card be played when some of its tiles cannot be placed (Artificial Lake with all oceans placed, Space Port with no city space)?** Fryxen says an earlier rule that allowed plays placing tiles that couldn't be placed (for thematic reasons) was removed, because it was unnecessary and created unwanted rules questions, especially for Mining Rights. The post does not state a verdict for Artificial Lake or Space Port specifically.  
  "But we removed the rule for 2 reasons. First, it was an unnecessary rule. More importantly, it created unwanted rules questions, especially for Mining Rights and the like." ([Jacob, 2023-09-07](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3146994/article/42917504#42917504)) `intent` `hedged`
- **If all city and greenery tiles are used, is placing more cities or greeneries (or playing Phobos/Ganymede cards) illegal?** The tiles are not supposed to run out; use replacements if you need.  
  "The tiles are not supposed to run out. Use replacements if you need." ([Jacob, 2024-07-18](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1705209/article/44578429#44578429))

### Triggered effects

- **Are triggered card effects, such as Ecological Zone adding an animal when you play a plant or animal tag, mandatory?** Intent: effects should always trigger when possible, and any player may invoke your triggers, so they should be considered mandatory, but only if someone remembers them. Mars University is optional ("may"). Cards generally have only positive effects so it is in your interest to trigger.  
  "But I would say that any player may invoke your triggers whenever applicable, so they should be considered mandatory, but only if someone remembers them (and nice people should help each other remember)." ([Jacob, 2018-08-16](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2043277/article/29787201#29787201)) `reversed`
- **Are triggered effects mandatory, and is forgetting one a rules violation?** Triggers are not mandatory but may be invoked by anyone, so forgetting one is not a violation and cannot invalidate a game; since it is always in someone's interest they should normally be performed and players should help each other remember.  
  "Triggers are not mandatory, but may be invoked by anyone. Thus there can be no cheating or invalidating." ([Jacob, 2018-10-10](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2043277/article/30190894#30190894)) `superseded` by [a later post](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2852387/article/39909297#39909297)
- **Are triggered effects of blue cards mandatory, and must other players remind someone who forgets one?** All triggers are automatic. Most are beneficial and the rare harmful ones are usually emphasised with 'must'. Officially any player can enforce any effect by reminding the owner and nobody has a formal duty to do so. Forgetting a trigger is a rules mistake; players are encouraged to help each other remember (rulebook page 12), even retroactively if it can still be done.  
  "Basically, all triggers are automatic. [...] the official procedure is that any player can enforce any effect by reminding each other (no-one has any official 'duty')." ([Jacob, 2022-04-19](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2852387/article/39909297#39909297))
- **May a player apply a forgotten effect retroactively if that hurts another player's plan, for example a milestone claim?** Fryxen says players are free to decide for themselves, but he would allow the retroactive effect: it was a bonus the player should have had and others could have reminded them earlier. A player cannot be 100 percent sure nobody can get extra MC, so holding off on a milestone is risky.  
  "You're of course free to do as you wish, but I'd allow it. It was a bonus they should have had, and you were free to remind them of it earlier." ([Jacob, 2022-04-20](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2852387/article/39913098#39913098)) `house-rule-ok`
- **Are takebacks allowed after a player's turn is over?** Retroactive bonuses and takebacks are different things. The only takeback Fryxen would allow beyond the player's own turn is when they passed while still having obvious actions to take, such as adding an animal or converting heat.  
  "The only takeback I would allow beyond the player's turn is if they passed while still having obvious actions to take, like adding an animal or maybe converting heat." ([Jacob, 2022-04-20](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2852387/article/39913108#39913108))
- **How should a forgotten triggered bonus (such as Tharsis Republic production) be handled after the fact?** In casual play the rulebook says players should help each other remember triggered bonuses, and Fryxen often lets a player take what they missed as far back as all players agree. There is no strict casual rule.  
  "I often even let player get resources they should have had from that production as far as all the players can agree how long it has been." ([Jacob, 2025-03-26](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3485170/article/45861790#45861790)) `variant` `house-rule-ok`
- **What is the strict rule for forgotten triggers in tournament play?** In tournament play any player may point out and enforce any trigger, but only during the same turn.  
  "The strict rule is that any player may point out and enforce any trigger but only during the same turn." ([Jacob, 2025-03-26](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3485170/article/45861790#45861790))

### Turn structure and actions

- **What happens when you pass during the action phase?** If you pass, you are out of the round (you do not get to act again that generation).  
  "If you pass, you're out of the round." ([Jacob, 2014-12-10](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1274610/article/17693384#17693384)) `prerelease`
- **What can a player do during the play phase of their turn?** During the play phase you can do everything except research and production (which have their own simultaneous phases): play a card, convert plants into a greenery tile, convert heat into a temperature increase, discard a card for 1 MC, use a standard project, claim a milestone, fund an award, or use an action on a card in play, as many times as you can afford and in any order. Card actions can only be done once per round.  
  "During the play phase you can do Everything except research and production, which still have their own phases that you perform simultaneously." ([Jacob, 2014-12-10](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1274610/article/17693384#17693384)) `prerelease`
- **How many production phases happen in a generation with two players, and when do you produce?** Players go round and round taking one or two actions until everyone passes (takes no action), and only then is production performed. You usually spend most resources before passing, then get new resources once all have passed.  
  "go round and round, taking one or two actions intil everyone PASS (take no action). This means that you usually spend all or most of your resources before you pass, THEN you get new resources" ([Jonathan, 2016-08-08](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1618001/article/23391706#23391706)) `prerelease`
- **Is there a limit on the number of actions in a generation?** No. In one generation you may essentially perform any number of actions, but only 1 or 2 at a time before the next player.  
  "In one generation you may essentially perform any number of actions, but only 1 or 2 at a time." ([Jonathan, 2016-08-08](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1618001/article/23391724#23391724)) `prerelease`
- **Is the number of actions limited per generation in solo?** No. In both solo and multiplayer the number of actions is unlimited; resources are the limit.  
  "In both solo and multiplayer, the amount of actions is unlimited, but the resources are what limits you." ([Jonathan, 2016-08-11](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1619637/article/23418789#23418789)) `prerelease`
- **Do players draw cards each turn, and how many turns can a player take per generation?** Per rulebook page 8: you take 1 or 2 actions each turn, or pass; play proceeds clockwise until all players have passed, so you get as many turns as you like per generation as long as you do not pass. Cards are bought only at the start of each generation.  
  "Page 8 in the rulebook says that "you take 1 or 2 actions each turn, or pass. play proceeds clockise around the table until all players have passed"" ([Isaac, 2016-08-24](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1626665/article/23525654#23525654)) `prerelease`
- **How many turns do you get in the action phase when you hold a lot of M€?** You get as many turns as you like during the action phase, with 1 or 2 actions per turn, so spend your M€; only afterwards comes the production phase that pays new income.  
  "you get as many turns as you like during the action phase, with 1 or 2 actions per turn." ([Isaac, 2016-09-29](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1646049/article/23831323#23831323))
- **Is there a limit to the number of actions a player can take per generation, and when does the generation end?** The turn goes around the table repeatedly until everyone has passed; each turn is 1 or 2 actions. As long as you do at least 1 action it will be your turn again after the others. If all others have passed you can keep taking turns until you pass too. The total number of actions per generation is unlimited.  
  "The total number of actions per generation is unlimited." ([Enoch, 2016-10-04](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1642571/article/23866951#23866951))
- **In the Action phase, does each player take only one turn of 1-2 actions per generation?** No. Players take turns doing 1 or 2 actions, going around the table repeatedly until everyone has passed; taking 2 actions and then passing is a misreading. The rulebook wording was to be clarified in the next print.  
  "I agree with you all :) and we will have it much clearer in the next print." ([Jacob, 2016-10-19](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1656716/article/23989925#23989925)) `erratum`
- **Is a player limited to 2 actions per generation?** No. The rules allow 1-2 actions per turn but any number of turns per generation; limiting to 2 actions per generation is a misconception that adds game time.  
  "The rules say you can play 1-2 actions per turn, but any number of turns per generation." ([Jonathan, 2016-10-24](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1659079/article/24027372#24027372))
- **How many actions can a player take in a generation?** Actions per generation are unlimited. The rules (to be rephrased in the next reprint) mean players take turns doing a limited number of actions at a time and keep going until they pass.  
  "Yes, we will refrase and emphasize that you have unlimited actions in the next reprint." ([Enoch, 2016-11-07](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1667605/article/24149064#24149064))
- **How can a player perform more than two actions per generation?** Go around the table doing 1-2 actions each time until every player has passed; you can do as many actions as you like in a generation, but only 1-2 at a time.  
  "You go around the table doing 1-2 actions until every player has passed." ([Enoch, 2016-11-10](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1666399/article/24179769#24179769))
- **How many actions can a player take per generation, and what is limited to 1-2?** The number of actions per generation is unlimited. The only limit is 1-2 actions at a time before the next player's turn; players go around the table until they choose to pass.  
  "The number of actions per generation is unlimited! The only limitation is that can only do 1-2 actions at a time before it is the next players turn." ([Enoch, 2016-11-13](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1670806/article/24206063#24206063))
- **How many actions can a player take?** 1-2 actions per turn, with unlimited actions per generation.  
  "1-2 actions per turn, unlimited actions per generation." ([Jacob, 2016-12-03](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1682744/article/24387139#24387139))
- **How many actions does a player get in the action phase of a generation?** Players have an unlimited number of actions each generation. They go around the table taking turns of 1-2 actions until everyone has passed.  
  "Did you understand that all players have unlimited number of actions every generation? [...] You go around and around and around the table doing 1-2 actions at a time until everyone has passed" ([Enoch, 2016-12-15](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1690101/article/24499246#24499246))
- **Was the rule about actions per generation reworded?** Yes, the rulebook text was re-formulated to make clear you have unlimited actions each generation (1-2 actions per turn, many turns).  
  "Yes, we have re-formulated this part of the rules to make it clear that you have unlimited actions each generation." ([Enoch, 2016-12-16](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1690101/article/24511203#24511203)) `erratum`
- **How many actions does a player take in a generation and when are they out of the action phase?** You have an unlimited number of actions each generation but may only take 1 or 2 at a time (your turn). After the other players' turns you get a new turn. Only if you passed (did no action on your turn) are you out for the rest of that action phase.  
  "You have an unlimited number of actions each generation, but may only take 1 or 2 at a time (your turn)." ([Jacob, 2017-01-10](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1705209/article/24723217#24723217))
- **Is there a limit to how many cards a player can play in a generation?** No. You have an unlimited number of actions each generation, so you can play as many cards as your resources allow.  
  "This means that you can play as many cards as your resources allow." ([Enoch, 2017-03-06](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1737666/article/25260257#25260257))
- **After all other players pass, does the remaining player have unlimited actions?** Yes. The number of turns is unlimited: if you are the only player who has not passed, you keep taking turns until you choose to pass.  
  "Yes, the number of turns are unlimited. If you are the only one that have not passed on your turn, you can continue to take turns until you choose to pass." ([Enoch, 2017-04-06](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1758310/article/25554123#25554123))
- **How do turns and generations relate in the action phase?** Every player has as many turns as they like each generation until they pass on their turn, but only 1-2 actions each turn; the turn then proceeds around the table until it is your turn again. A turn is not a generation.  
  "Every player has as many turns as they like each generation until they choose to pass on their turn. BUT - only 1-2 actions each turn" ([Enoch, 2017-05-27](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1690206/article/25989396#25989396))
- **How many turns can a player take in a generation?** You may take as many turns as you like in a generation until you pass. The second printing's rulebook and reference cards state this more clearly.  
  "the rulebook and reference cards are a little more clear on your right to take as many turns as you like in a generation, until you pass" ([Isaac, 2017-07-28](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1819646/article/26487981#26487981))
- **Why is there no player-influenced turn order (e.g. first to pass goes first next generation)?** Design intent: turn order is fixed (rotating first player) so it is fair, predictable and easy; a turn order track was rejected as it complicates play, makes the state harder to overview, and risks a static turn order where one player goes first almost every generation.  
  "So we opted for a turn order that would be fair, predictable and easy." ([Jacob, 2017-11-20](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1887368/article/27489545#27489545)) `intent`
- **Can an attack card be played against a player who has already passed for the generation?** Yes. There is no rule protecting passed players; a player who passes only forfeits the ability to respond.  
  "No such rule. If your buddy passes, he forfeits his ability to respond. You can still play the card to affect him." ([Jacob, 2018-02-20](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1944112/article/28327573#28327573))
- **How does the action phase proceed and when does it end?** The first player takes 1 or 2 actions (some corporations have a fixed first action, after which they may take a second action or not). Turns pass clockwise with 1 or 2 actions at a time until everyone has passed (taken no action on their turn), so the turn can go several laps and you get another turn as long as you take at least 1 action on your turn.  
  "The turn passes clockwise with players taking 1 or 2 actions at a time until everyone has passed (=taken no action on their turn)." ([Jacob, 2019-12-31](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2338674/article/33682050#33682050))

### Variants and house rules

- **Can Terraforming Mars be played with 6 players?** Nothing technically prevents it (answer 'Technically, no' to 'is there any reason we can't play with 6'). Balance issues may arise, especially for effects affecting all players, and the Terraformer milestone should probably be lowered to about 30 TR instead of 35 to stand a chance of being taken.  
  "Technically, no. Some balance issues might arise, especially for effects affecting all players. Also, the Terraformer milestone should probably be lowered to about 30 TR instead of 35 to stand a chance of being taken." ([Jonathan, 2016-12-10](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1687208/article/24452379#24452379)) `variant`
- **Does EagleEye80 recommend a sixth player?** The game works at 1 to 5 players. He would not recommend adding a 6th player (they have tried it): downtime, needing a very large table, no chance to follow opponents.  
  "I think the game works on all player counts from 1-5 [...] I would not recommend adding a 6th player. (yes, we have tried it)" ([Enoch, 2016-12-15](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1687208/article/24499228#24499228)) `variant`
- **Is a card-market variant (face-up market replacing the starting deal/draft) workable, and what market size is suggested?** Lord_Aethan encouraged trying it and suggested a fixed-size market of 3*2 or 4*2 cards regardless of player count, rather than a size scaling with player count.  
  "I do believe it would be better to have a fixed-sized market, though. I would suggest 3*2 or 4*2 cards regardless of player count." ([Jonathan, 2017-01-03](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1699470/article/24643970#24643970)) `variant` `house-rule-ok` `hedged`
- **Should players be allowed to trade resources with each other as a house rule?** The designers advise against it: trading would make the game much longer, would undermine the importance of the resources and productions you choose to build, and would favour players good at negotiating. Having too much steel, heat or titanium is part of the game; use the Miner and Thermalist awards.  
  "It would take away the importance of which resources and productions you choose to build. If you play poorly you may end up with more resourses than you need, yes, and that's part of the game." ([Enoch, 2017-02-27](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1732739/article/25197866#25197866)) `intent`
- **Should there be a standard action to buy cards from the deck for M€ (house rule)?** The designers advise against it: allowing cards to be bought for money makes money too powerful, so the game would always be about who has the most money, whereas now lacking cards limits what money can do.  
  "If you can buy cards for money, then that will make money too powerful, and the game would essentially always be about who has the most money." ([Jonathan, 2017-08-31](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1839385/article/26800067#26800067)) `intent`
- **How could a variant where players have two corporations be balanced?** Fryxen suggests costing the extra corporation at 65 (corporation baseline value) minus its starting MC, e.g. Teractor 5 MC, PhoboLog 41 MC (giving 10 titanium and the titanium boost); alternatively discarded corporations could form a first-come-first-served market set up before the first action phase. Not tested.  
  "The most straight-forward way to implement it would be cost them at 65 (Corporation base line value) minus their starting Money." ([Jacob, 2018-09-18](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2061394/article/30021122#30021122)) `variant` `hedged` `house-rule-ok`
- **What does FryxGames ask of people making and sharing fan-made cards?** Fan-made cards must be marked 'fan made' to distinguish them from official content, should avoid copyrighted artwork, and may not be used commercially. Cards are game content while cubes, tiles and similar items are alternative accessories.  
  "please mark all cards 'fan made' so there is a clear distinction between official and non-official content." ([Jacob, 2020-05-22](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2433226/article/34902171#34902171)) `intent`
- **Is the Moon expansion an authorized FryxGames product?** There is no official Moon expansion; it is fan made and FryxGames is not involved, so using it is not authorized, though players can play any way they like.  
  "There is no official Moon expansion. It is fan made and FryxGames is not involved in any way." ([Jacob, 2022-09-01](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2926719/article/40727740#40727740)) `intent` `house-rule-ok`
- **Is advising or negotiating about attack targets (e.g. who has plants to remove) part of the game?** Fryxen likes keeping negotiation about attack targets in his games; helping players see their options is fine especially when teaching, but beyond some point it stops being good. It depends on the group; no formal rule.  
  "Negotiating targets of an attack is a spice I'd rather keep in my games. :)" ([Jacob, 2023-07-19](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3120314/article/42644408#42644408)) `intent` `house-rule-ok`
- **How can engine building be toned down while keeping the game interesting?** Fryxen suggests removing all card-drawing cards: engine builders need access to many cards, so less access makes the strategy harder. The base game itself was designed with higher baseline production and fewer engine cards.  
  "If you want to nerf engine building a little while still keeping it interesting, try removing all card-drawing cards." ([Jacob, 2025-08-01](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3550894/article/46409473#46409473)) `variant`
- **Is there an official team mode?** An official 2v2 expansion for Terraforming Mars released on the Gamefound campaign for The Legacy Of Mars: two teams of two, sending cards and resources to your team-mate, with new milestones and awards adjusted for it.  
  "In the 2v2 version, you team up against each other, send cards and resources to your team-mate, and enjoy a combo-filled night with new milestones and awards adjusted just for this experience." ([Jacob, 2026-05-11](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3706792/article/47663326#47663326)) `variant`
- **Does the 2v2 product add new project cards or corporations?** No. It is a very simple product with the 2v2 rules and 2 sets of milestones and awards; some of those are mechanically linked to team play. It adds no new content applicable to normal play.  
  "No, it's a very simple product with 2v2 rules and 2 sets of milestones and awards adjusted to a gameplay where it will be easier to go bananas with your strategies" ([Jacob, 2026-05-11](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3706792/article/47664455#47664455)) `variant`

### Ants

- **Can you use Ants if there is no microbe to take?** No, because removing a microbe is the action's cost. You could, however, remove an ant to gain an ant.  
  "You can not use Ants if there is no microbe to take because that is the cost for the action." ([Jacob, 2016-12-03](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1682744/article/24387139#24387139))
- **In solo play, where you can steal from the neutral opponent, can you use Ants and Predators?** Yes.  
  "Yes you can. :)" ([Jacob, 2017-03-22](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1705209/article/25417910#25417910))
- **Can you use an action like Ants or Predators (remove a resource from any card to add one to this card) when no card holds a suitable resource to remove?** No. Actions on blue cards must always be performed in full: you pay the cost (if any) and get the reward, without exceptions. So Ants or Predators can only be activated if some card (yours or another player's, because of the red border) holds the resource to remove.  
  "Actions on blue cards must always be performed in full, you perform the cost (if any) and you get the reward. No exeptions ever!" ([Enoch, 2017-04-01](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1755105/article/25509849#25509849))
- **Can the Ants action be used when there is no microbe on any card to remove?** No. Actions (upper panel of a card) must always be performed in full with no exceptions, so Ants cannot be activated unless there is a microbe resource on one of your or your opponents' cards to remove. Removing red-bordered resources as a lower-panel effect is optional, but an action is not.  
  "Removing red-bordered resources as an EFFECT on a card (lower panel) is optional - but ACTIONS (upper panel) must always be performed in full with no exceptions." ([Enoch, 2017-05-17](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1780966/article/25901031#25901031))
- **Can Predators and Ants add a resource to themselves without having a resource to remove?** Official answer: the cost must be paid. The removal part of the action is a cost and cannot be skipped to gain the resource. Whether a card may pay the cost from its own resources is left open in the post.  
  "Official answer: the cost must be paid." ([Jonathan, 2017-10-25](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1870118/article/27262675#27262675)) `hedged`
- **Can the Predators or Ants action be used when there is no animal/microbe on another card to remove, given that red-bordered resources are optional?** No. The animal (or microbe) removal before the arrow is a cost and must be done in full to get the effect after the arrow, so the action cannot be used if no resource is available to remove.  
  "Örjan is correct - the animal removal before the arrow is a cost, and must therefore be done in full in order to get the effect after the arrow." ([Jacob, 2020-02-22](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2374854/article/34114400#34114400))
- **Is removal of red-bordered resources optional, as the rulebook says?** Per the user Örjan, endorsed with 'Örjan is correct': optionality of removing red-bordered resources applies when you play a card from your hand (red-bordered production removal is mandatory); the red border means any player including yourself, and the action on Predators and Ants removes a resource from any one card (any player) and adds it to itself.  
  "Örjan is correct" ([Jacob, 2020-02-22](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2374854/article/34114400#34114400)) `inferred`

### Asteroid Mining Consortium

- **Is Mining Consortium's titanium production requirement there because it forces you to reduce someone's production?** No. The requirement is thematic: if you have no production of a resource it is hard to be part of a consortium on it. Other cards that lower any player's production do not necessarily have such a requirement.  
  "the requirement is a thematic one, that if you don't have any production of a resource, it is thematically hard to be part of a consortium on that resource." ([Jonathan, 2018-03-14](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1957397/article/28542554#28542554)) `intent`

### Viral Enhancers

- **Does each tag played trigger effects such as Viral Enhancers, and who chooses the trigger order?** Yes: each tag played can trigger effects, and per the rules you choose the order in which they trigger.  
  "Yes. Each tag played can trigger effects, and per the rules, you choose the order in which they trigger." ([Jonathan, 2016-08-15](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1621865/article/23447745#23447745)) `prerelease` `inferred`
- **If I play a microbe-tag card with Viral Enhancers in play, can I take a plant resource, and does it apply when playing Viral Enhancers itself?** Yes to both: you are not forced to match the tag. Microbe cards that collect microbes can add a microbe to the card or gain 1 plant instead; microbe cards that do not collect microbes always give the plant. It also triggers when you play Viral Enhancers itself.  
  "Yes, and that includes when you play Viral Enhancers itself." ([Jacob, 2016-08-15](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1621865/article/23452515#23452515)) `prerelease`
- **With Viral Enhancers, when you play a plant, microbe or animal tag, what can you take?** You choose: put a resource cube on the card you just played (if it can hold one) or take a plant resource on your player board. Each tag counts, so Advanced Ecosystems gives 3 plant resources (it cannot hold resources itself).  
  "When you play a card with plant/microbe/animal tags, you choose: - put a resource cube on the card you just played (if possible) - or take a plant resource on your player board" ([Enoch, 2016-10-09](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1650981/article/23907366#23907366))
- **If you have Viral Enhancers and no plants, can you play a plant-tagged card that requires paying a plant, using the plant Viral Enhancers gives you?** No. Viral Enhancers gives a plant when you play a plant-tagged card, but you cannot play a card that requires you to pay a plant if you do not already have a plant before you play it.  
  "Viral enhancers gives you a plant if you play a card with a plant tag - but you can not play that card you described if you do not have a plant before you play it." ([Isaac, 2017-03-01](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1736927/article/25218268#25218268))
- **How do Viral Enhancers' triggers work when a card has multiple matching tags, e.g. Decomposers (plant and microbe)?** Each tag is counted when you play a card, so Viral Enhancers triggers per matching tag: Decomposers gives 2 microbes on itself, or 1 microbe plus a plant. Explanation text may differ from the icons, but icon meaning is used unless there is an asterisk.  
  "Viral Enhaners then Decomposers will give 2 microbes on Decomposers, or 1 microbe plus a plant." ([Jacob, 2018-07-02](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2018322/article/29433070#29433070))
- **Can you take a plant with Viral Enhancers if the played card can hold animal or microbe resources?** Yes. When Viral Enhancers triggers you always have the option to take a plant for the triggering tag(s).  
  "When Viral Enhancers triggers, you always have the option to take a plant for the triggering tag(s)." ([Jacob, 2018-08-19](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1650981/article/29809404#29809404))

### Viron

- **Can Viron's effect interact with other players' action cards?** No. The only way you can ever interact with other players via card effects is if the icons have a red border; Viron's effect has no red border, so it cannot use other players' cards.  
  "The ONLY way you can EVER interact with other players via card effects is if the icons have a RED BORDER." ([Jacob, 2018-06-16](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1705209/article/29322736#29322736))
- **When Viron repeats an action card with an 'OR' between options, can it use either option, and both?** Viron can use either option on the action card regardless of which option was chosen with the normal use of that card, but never both: 'OR' means 'either X or Y'.  
  "Viron can indeed use either option on an action card, regardless of which option you chose with the normal use of that card. But in neither case do you get to do both options." ([Jacob, 2025-01-17](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3436260/article/45501546#45501546))

### Water Splitting Plant

- **Can you use Water Splitting when oxygen is maxed?** Yes, you can spend 3 energy to get nothing.  
  "You can even use Water Splitting when O2 is maxed, spending 3 energy to get nothing." ([Jacob, 2016-12-03](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1682744/article/24387139#24387139)) `inferred`
