# Colonies: official BGG rulings

48 rulings posted on BoardGameGeek by FryxGames, Stronghold Games and the Automa designers. [README.md](README.md) says who is who, how this was built, and what the tags mean. Under each heading the rulings run in date order, so a later one can overrule an earlier one.


## Rules


### Beginner corporations

- **How do Beginner Corporations work in any setup?** In any game setup, a first-time player can be dealt 1 Beginner Corporation and 10 free cards instead of choosing from 2 corporations and buying cards. Beginner Corporations are not dealt to all players unless they are all beginners.  
  "In ANY game setup and combination of variants and expansions, a 1st time player can join and be dealt 1 Beginner Corporation and 10 free cards" ([Jacob, 2018-11-21](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2099289/article/30545995#30545995))
- **How do Beginner Corporations work together with Prelude?** A Beginner Corporation player gets the 10 free cards and then chooses 2 out of 4 Prelude cards just like the others.  
  "if you play Beginner Coroporation with Prelude, you are supposed to get your 10 free cards and then choose 2 out of 4 Prelude cards just like the others." ([Jacob, 2018-11-21](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2099289/article/30549330#30549330))

### Colony bonuses and trade income

- **May floaters from a colony effect be split among several floater cards?** No. Each effect adds only to one card, like Large Convoy. Colony bonuses are separate effects and can be directed to other cards (e.g. placement bonus on one card, trade income on another, each colony's bonus on different cards).  
  "Each effect should only add to one card, just like similar card effects like Large Convoy." ([Jacob, 2018-11-04](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2088736/article/30399380#30399380))
- **If you have a colony on a tile and trade with it, do you get both trade income and the colony bonus?** Yes, but they are separate effects, so for Titan the trade income floaters can go on one card and the colony bonus floater on another.  
  "Yes, but they are separate effects, so in the case of Titan you can place the trade income floaters on one card and the colony bonus floater on another card if you like." ([Jacob, 2018-11-17](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2097370/article/30515731#30515731))
- **Can resources gained from a colony be split between several cards?** The trade income must go to only one card, but colonies there have their trading bonus (and placement bonus) which can go to different cards since those are separate effects.  
  "The trade income must go to only one card, but if you have colonies there, their trading bonus (and placement bonus) can go to different cards since those are separate effects." ([Jacob, 2019-01-22](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2136182/article/31043199#31043199))
- **Is there a required order for collecting trade income and colony bonuses?** No fixed order: treat them as independent effects and the active player chooses the order.  
  "Number 2) is right. The active player chooses order." ([Jacob, 2019-09-24](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2281271/article/33002123#33002123))
- **Are colony bonuses mandatory or optional (e.g. a microbe that an opponent with Ants would eat)?** All effects are generally mandatory. Three exceptions: microbes (card resources) must be taken if possible but can be ignored if you can't take them; parameter raises may be ignored if maxed; red-bordered resource destruction may be ignored wholly or partly. If you can't perform all effects, you can't take the action.  
  "All effects are generally mandatory, regardless of if you play a card, use a standard action, use a blue card action or something else." ([Jacob, 2020-02-22](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2283213/article/34114364#34114364)) `inferred`
- **Can a player choose not to take their colony bonus (e.g. microbes that an opponent with Ants would eat)?** Official answer: No. Effects are generally mandatory, with exceptions: card resources must be taken if possible but may be ignored if you can't take them; parameter raises may be ignored if maxed; red-bordered resource destruction may be ignored. So if you trade to get microbes, or get them as colony bonuses, you must take them if you can.  
  "So if you trade to get microbes, or get them as colony bonuses, you must take them if you can." ([Jacob, 2020-02-22](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2374233/article/34114388#34114388)) `inferred`

### Placing colonies

- **If a card requires placing a colony but there is no valid position, can the card still be played?** No. A colony is not a resource and the colony tile is not a card, so the exception for resources and tiles that cannot be placed does not apply; you must place the colony to play the card.  
  "A colony is not a resource, and the colony tile is not a card, so that exception does not apply. You have to place the colony in order to play the card." ([Jacob, 2018-09-11](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2057930/article/29972040#29972040))
- **Can you build a colony on a colony tile whose resources you cannot store (to block it or collect the bonus)?** Yes, for the same reasons: card-resources you cannot store can be ignored and the rest of the effect applied.  
  "Yes, for the same reasons as above." ([Jacob, 2018-11-10](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2085437/article/30453783#30453783))
- **Can you place a colony on a tile (e.g. Titan) when you have no card that can collect the placement bonus resource?** Yes. It raises the level the marker drops back to after trades, and later your resource cards can receive resources from colony bonus and trade income once you have cards that can collect them. Normally not a good play.  
  "You can place a colony on Titan without having a floater cards to put the placement bonus on." ([Jacob, 2019-11-26](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1705209/article/33460467#33460467))

### Setup and deck composition

- **When playing the standard game plus Colonies without Corporate Era cards, does everyone start with +1 production of each resource?** Yes. Exclude the Corporate Era cards, add the Colonies cards and colony tiles, and everyone starts with +1 production of each resource. Prelude can be added too (2 Prelude cards per player) and +1 production is still kept.  
  "If you want to play standard game + Colonies, you exclude the CE cards, add the Colonies cards and colony tiles, and everyone starts with +1 production of each resource." ([Jacob, 2018-11-21](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2099289/article/30545995#30545995))
- **May both the promo copies and the Colonies copies of Lunar Exports, Atmo Collectors and Jovian Lanterns be put in the deck?** No. Each card exists in only one copy in the project deck (they are patents); the promos are pre-releases of the Colonies cards, so only 1 of each card is used.  
  "Each card is only supposed to exist in 1 copy in the deck, as they are thematically called "patents"." ([Jonathan, 2018-11-29](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2011396/article/30611231#30611231))
- **In a solo game, when do you select which colony tiles to keep, and is the extra Aridor colony tile random?** In solo, you choose colony tiles at the same time as choosing cards and corporation; in multiplayer, the tiles are drawn before choosing cards and corporation. Aridor can choose the discarded colony tile in a solo game; it is not random, and Aridor should choose a tile that goes well with the starting hand.  
  "In solo, you choose colony tiles at the same time as choosing cards and corp. For multiplayer, the tiles are drawn before choosing cards and corp." ([Jacob, 2019-02-07](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2146233/article/31188090#31188090))

### Solo game

- **Can Colonies be played solo?** Yes, you can definitely play solo with Colonies.  
  "Yes,you can definitely solo with Colonies." ([Jacob, 2018-06-29](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2016993/article/29417410#29417410))
- **In solo with Colonies, is the starting TR lowered?** No. Colonies lowers the starting MC production by 2 steps, not the TR; this is relevant for TR solo.  
  "Colonies lowers the MC production 2 steps, not the TR. Relevant for TR solo." ([Jacob, 2019-06-19](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2223324/article/32265774#32265774))

### Trading

- **How does the white colony marker on a colony tile move, so the board state can be recreated after a bump?** The white colony marker goes down as far as it can whenever the colony tile is traded with, and goes up one step in the solar phase. If there is a trade fleet there, the white marker should be at the bottom of the track. Building colonies blocks spaces at the bottom so the white marker doesn't go down as far.  
  "the white colony marker goes down as far as it can whenever the colony tile is traded with, and goes up one step in the solar phase." ([Jacob, 2018-07-08](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2018821/article/29472037#29472037))
- **When you trade with a colony tile whose resources you cannot store, does the scale still reset?** The scale resets as normal. Taking card-resources when you don't have anywhere to store them can be ignored and the rest of the effect applied, as per normal rules for playing cards. It also does not stop colonies on that tile from collecting their colony bonus.  
  "The scale resets as normal." ([Jacob, 2018-10-30](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2085437/article/30355581#30355581))
- **Can the trade cost be paid with a mix of MC, energy and titanium (e.g. 6 MC and 1 titanium)?** No, you cannot mix payment types for trading.  
  "You can not mix, no." ([Jacob, 2018-11-14](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2095383/article/30487889#30487889))
- **Can you trade with multiple colonies in one generation?** Yes, if you have more trade fleets. Trade fleets move from the trade fleets tile and only return in the solar phase, so each trade fleet can trade only once per generation.  
  "The trade fleets always move from the trade fleets tile, and only go back there in the solar phase, so each trade fleet can only trade once during the generation." ([Jacob, 2018-12-10](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2110664/article/30699496#30699496))
- **Can you trade with Miranda, Titan or Enceladus if you have no cards that can collect animals, floaters or microbes?** Yes. Every action works along the same lines as playing a card, with the same exceptions, so you can trade and just ignore the part of the effect you can't collect.  
  "Every action works along the same lines as playing a card, and with the same exceptions." ([Jacob, 2019-03-22](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1705209/article/31552135#31552135))
- **Can you trade with a colony tile that has no colonies on it?** Yes, you can trade without any player colonies; there are 7 independent colonies in the background producing stuff to trade.  
  "You can trade without any player colonies." ([Jacob, 2019-08-17](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1705209/article/32732873#32732873))

### Wild tag

- **Does a card with a wild tag count towards the number of cards with no tags?** A wild tag is no tag at all until you choose it to be one of the defined tags during an action, so a card with a wild tag counts as a card with no tag outside that moment.  
  "It is no tag at all until you choose it to be one of the ones defined in the rules during an action." ([Jacob, 2018-07-25](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2031069/article/29614168#29614168))
- **Confirming that a wild tag counts as no tag for Community Services but not as a tag for Aridor, what else is a wild tag useful for?** Right: a wild tag counts as no tag for Community Services and does not count as a tag for Aridor.  
  "Right." ([Jacob, 2018-10-31](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2031069/article/30365855#30365855))
- **Does a wild tag boost cards that give something per tag when played?** Yes, a wild tag in play enhances cards like Satellites and Insects that give something per tag when you play them (not when you play the wild tag card itself).  
  "Another use for the wild tag is of course to enhance cards like Satellites, Insects etc. that give something per tag when you play them (not when you play the wild tag)." ([Jacob, 2018-10-31](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2031069/article/30365855#30365855))
- **For Aridor and Community Services, is the wild tag a tag?** The wild tag is NOT a tag for Aridor (no production bump) but would give Community Services a bump. The wild tag is only ever an actual tag during one of your actions when you decide it to be a certain tag.  
  "The wild tag is only ever an actual tag during one of your actions when you decide for it to be a certain tag." ([Jacob, 2018-10-31](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2085683/article/30365843#30365843))
- **What can a wild tag be used for?** Allowed: satisfying tag requirements for playing cards (Gene Repair, Fusion Power), fulfilling milestone requirements (Diversifier, Builder), boosting cards that count tags when played (Insects, Worms, Terraforming Ganymede). Forbidden: triggers when you play the wild tag (Space Station, Aerobraking), Jovian scoring (Ganymede Colony), award competition (Scientist), opponents targeting it (Toll Station), and Aridor.  
  "Satisfying tag requirements for playing cards (Gene Repair, Fusion Power)" ([Jacob, 2018-12-06](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2108421/article/30670343#30670343)) `inferred`
- **Does a wild tag count for Community Services, and does it trigger Aridor?** Yes, it counts for Community Services and does not trigger Aridor. The wild tag is only the tag you want it to be during one of your actions; at all other times it is no tag. Anyone is free to houserule.  
  "Yes, it counts for Community Services, and will not trigger Aridor." ([Jacob, 2018-12-12](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2108421/article/30725437#30725437)) `house-rule-ok`
- **Why is a wild tag considered no tag while events count as having an event tag?** Design intent: the wild tag is not a tag when played; events are thematically one-time and thus have a meaningful tag.  
  "Events are thematically one time eventuellt and thus have a meaningful tag." ([Jacob, 2023-10-29](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2031069/article/43197108#43197108)) `intent`

## Cards


### Aridor

- **Do tags on event cards, multiple tags on a card, or a wild tag trigger Aridor's new-tag production bonus?** Tags on event cards do not count for Aridor, not even the event tag, because events are not put in play. A card with two new tags (e.g. Vesta Shipyard) might give 2 bumps if you had neither before. A wild tag does not count: it is not a tag when put into play and is not 'put into play' when applied during an action.  
  "Tags on event cards do not count for Aridor, even the even tag itself." ([Jacob, 2018-10-30](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2085683/article/30358641#30358641)) `hedged`
- **Playing Sister Planet Support with a wild tag set as Venus to meet its requirement, does that trigger Aridor?** The wild tag can satisfy the Venus requirement but does not trigger Aridor (it is not a new permanent tag). Playing Sister Planet Support does trigger Aridor because it brings its own permanent Venus tag into play. Outside your own action and while being played the wild tag is no tag.  
  "The wild tag can be used to fulfill the venus tag requirement for playing Sister Planet Support, but it will not trigger Aridor (because it's not a new permanent tag)." ([Jacob, 2018-12-06](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2108421/article/30670343#30670343))
- **Why can't the wild tag trigger Aridor?** Aridor triggers on permanent tags and the wild tag is not a permanent tag; 'wild' is not a tag type but a rule/effect. The wild tag counts as no tag at all when undecided, to remove rules ambiguities.  
  "Aridor triggers on permanent tags, and the wild tag is not a permanent tag; it changes." ([Jacob, 2018-12-12](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2108421/article/30723777#30723777)) `intent`
- **Does setting a wild tag to a building tag while playing a building prevent an Aridor trigger, versus Sister Planet Support triggering Aridor anyway?** Aridor should trigger on Sister Planet Support as previously said, because it brings a new permanent tag type; the wild tag is not permanent. He withdraws the idea that a wild tag can prevent your own Aridor trigger, and says he might change his mind about the wild tag.  
  "Aridor should trigger on SPS like I said because it does bring a new permanent tag type. The wild tag is not permanent." ([Jacob, 2018-12-13](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2108421/article/30728549#30728549)) `hedged` `inferred`
- **Does Aridor get its bonus from playing event cards with new tags?** No. Aridor doesn't get its bonus from event cards because event tags don't stay in play.  
  "Aridor doesn't get its bonus from event cards, because those tags don't stay in play." ([Jacob, 2019-08-17](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1705209/article/32732873#32732873))

### Community Services

- **Does Community Services count the corporation and Preludes, and event cards?** Green and blue cards count, as well as the corporation and Preludes. Events do not count because they are face down (not in play).  
  "Green and blue cards count, as well as corporation and preludes. Events do not count because they are face down (not in play)." ([Jacob, 2018-10-29](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2084477/article/30353016#30353016))

### Cryo-Sleep

- **Does Cryo-Sleep's 'pay 1 less resource' trading discount apply when paying with M€?** Yes: M€ is a resource, just like energy or titanium, so paying with M€ costs 1 less (8 M€).  
  "M€ is a resource, just like energy or titanium." ([Jonathan, 2018-10-13](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2075257/article/30219065#30219065)) `inferred`

### Factorum

- **Can Factorum's action (pay 3 M€ to draw a building card) be taken if no building-tag card is left in the deck and discard?** No: not finding the right card is not one of the 3 exceptions, so the action is cancelled. This is one of the few cases where an action is started and then cancelled, because the search revealed information. No errata exists; a suggested house rule is to discard cards from the deck until it runs out or a building card is found, then take it.  
  "Not finding the right card is not one of the 3 exceptions. If the action tells you to get a certain card and you can't, then the action is cancelled." ([Jacob, 2021-05-18](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1705209/article/37732543#37732543)) `house-rule-ok`

### Jovian Lanterns

- **Why does the Colonies printing of the Lanterns card have no space tag compared to the promo?** Intentional: tags were removed from cards where unnecessary because space and Jovian strategies were already boosted in Colonies. Thematically the Lanterns are located in the atmosphere, not in space.  
  "Thematically, the Lanterns are located in the atmosphere, not in space." ([Jacob, 2018-10-30](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2084653/article/30353779#30353779)) `intent` `inferred`
- **Which version of Jovian Lanterns is correct, the promo with a space tag (cost 21) or the Colonies card without it (cost 20)?** The space tag was removed, and the cost reduced accordingly, because Jovian Lanterns is situated in Jupiter's atmosphere, not in space; the version without the space tag is the intended one.  
  "The reason we removed the space tag (and therefore reduced the cost) is that Jovian Lanterns is a project situated in Jupiter's atmosphere, not in space." ([Jacob, 2023-09-21](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3156635/article/42987358#42987358)) `erratum`

### Jupiter Floating Station

- **Is the maximum of 4 on the Jupiter Floating Station a limit on floaters on the card or on the MC gained?** There is no limit to adding floaters; the limit applies only to how many credits you can get in that action. Alternatively it can be read as how many of your floaters you may count, which amounts to the same thing.  
  "There is no limit to adding resources, only in how many credits you can get in that action." ([Jacob, 2018-10-05](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2016315/article/30157213#30157213)) `inferred`
- **May Jupiter Floating Station hold more than 4 floaters, or is 4 only the cap on MC gained?** As many floaters as you like; the 'max 4' only restricts the second action of getting money for floaters.  
  "As many as you like. The "max 4" is clearly a restriction only for the second possible action of getting money for floaters." ([Jacob, 2018-11-12](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2094344/article/30474925#30474925)) `hedged` `inferred`

### Lunar Mining

- **Does Lunar Mining count its own Earth tag even though the card lacks 'including this'?** Yes. The rule is that every card triggers off its own tags; the 'including this' explanation text only reflects it. Unless a card has an asterisk and explicit instruction not to count itself, it counts its own tags.  
  "The rule is that every card triggers off its own tags." ([Jacob, 2024-06-16](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3318759/article/44429639#44429639))

### Pluto

- **Is Pluto's colony bonus draw a card and then discard a card, in that order?** Yes: the effect is specifically a draw then a discard, so the active player decides only who draws/discards first and can take trade income before their own colony bonuses.  
  "That's how it works, yes." ([Jacob, 2018-11-12](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2094344/article/30474925#30474925))
- **If several players have a colony on Pluto, in which order do they get their colony bonuses when someone trades there?** The active player determines the order of effects.  
  "The active player determines the order of effects." ([Jacob, 2018-11-21](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2099543/article/30545361#30545361))

### Saturn Systems

- **If a corporation goes first, does its Jovian tag trigger a later Saturn Systems, and how does Prelude change that?** With Prelude, preludes are played after all corporations, so a Jovian prelude of the first player's Stormcraft would trigger Saturn Systems even though Stormcraft goes first. Corporation tags alone depend on reveal order.  
  "If you use Prelude, though, they are all played after all corporations. So if Stormcraft has a jovian prelude, it would trigger Saturn Systems even though Stormcraft goes first." ([Jacob, 2019-08-21](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2261045/article/32760926#32760926))

### Stormcraft Incorporated

- **Can Stormcraft's floaters (usable as heat) count as heat for the Thermalist award?** No. They are still floaters, not heat, and the award doesn't use heat, it only counts it.  
  "No. Because they are still floaters, not heat. And the award doesn't use heat, it only counts it." ([Jacob, 2018-12-06](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2108715/article/30672335#30672335)) `inferred`
- **What does the action of Stormcraft Incorporated do?** The action adds a floater to any of your cards that define a use for those floaters, and can be used once per generation.  
  "An action to add a floater to any of your cards that define a use for those floaters. This action can be used once per generation." ([Jacob, 2021-12-09](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2772171/article/39005315#39005315)) `inferred`
- **Is the Stormcraft Incorporated effect only triggered when its action is taken?** No. The effect is separate from the action: floaters on Stormcraft can always be spent as if they were 2 heat each, whenever you would spend heat (analogous to Helion).  
  "An effect that allows you to spend floaters from Stormcraft as if they were 2 heat each. This effect is always active and allows you to use these floaters whenever you would spend heat." ([Jacob, 2021-12-09](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2772171/article/39005315#39005315)) `inferred`
