# Hellas & Elysium: official BGG rulings

27 rulings posted on BoardGameGeek by FryxGames, Stronghold Games and the Automa designers. [README.md](README.md) says who is who, how this was built, and what the tags mean. Under each heading the rulings run in date order, so a later one can overrule an earlier one.


## Rules


### Board typos

- **Is 'Claimer's Price' on the Hellas/Elysium board a typo, and is 'Excentric' a typo?** Isaac Fryxelius confirmed 'Claimer's Price' is a typo that carried over from the main game board to both Hellas and Elysium. 'Excentric' is not a typo.  
  "yes, definitly a typo (that really came as chock when we realized it, and it actually survived from the main game to both Hellas and Elysium :cry:)" ([Isaac, 2017-11-15](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1882729/article/27443330#27443330))

### Ecologist (milestone)

- **The Elysium Ecologist milestone shows 4 on the map but 6 in the (French) rules; which is right?** Fryxen answered only that the iconography on the board can be trusted because it is not changed in translation, which points to the number shown on the map (4); he did not state the number explicitly.  
  "you can trust the iconography because that is not changed in translation." ([Jacob, 2017-08-24](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1835986/article/26732613#26732613)) `hedged`
- **Does the Ecologist milestone count cards or tags, and must all of plant, animal and microbe types be present?** It counts 4 tags, not 4 cards, so a card with both a plant tag and an animal tag counts as two of the four.  
  "It is 4 tags, not 4 cards. If you have a card with both plant tag and animal tag, you're halfway there :)" ([Jonathan, 2018-03-26](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1835986/article/28647328#28647328))

### Event cards and awards

- **Do cards in the discard pile (events) count towards the Space Baron and Celebrity awards?** No. Event cards do not contribute to these awards.  
  "Not. The rules clearly state that event cards do not contribute to these Awards." ([Jacob, 2017-08-28](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1838326/article/26761449#26761449))
- **Are played event cards still 'in play' for the Hellas & Elysium awards that count cards in play?** No. Event cards are not in play after you have played them.  
  "Event cards are not in play after you've played them." ([Jacob, 2017-08-28](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1838326/article/26761621#26761621))

### Excentric (award)

- **Do resources on cards placed on Self-Replicating Robots count toward the Excentric award on Hellas?** No. The cards on SRR are not yet in play, so resources on them do not count towards Excentric. The resources are still resources, and other cards can add resources to it as long as they don't specify cards in play (which Excentric does).  
  "The cards on SRR are not yet in play, and so the resources on them do not contribute towards the Excentric Award on the Hellas map." ([Jacob, 2017-01-10](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1705209/article/24723217#24723217))
- **Do resources on cards placed on Self-Replicating Robots count towards the Excentric award?** No. The Excentric award counts resources on cards in play, including corporations. Cards held on Self-Replicating Robots are not in play, so their resources do not count.  
  "Errata: The Excentric Award Counts Resources on cards in play (including Corporations)." ([Jacob, 2017-09-11](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1846557/article/26880799#26880799)) `erratum`
- **Do microbes, animals and other special resources on cards count as 'resources' for the Excentric award?** Yes. All square icons are resources, shown by metallic cubes. The six basic resources are on the player board, and there are several non-standard resources such as microbes, animals and science.  
  "In essence, all square icons are always resources, and they are represented by the metallic cubes." ([Jonathan, 2017-09-18](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1846557/article/26940919#26940919))

### Generalist (milestone)

- **What exactly does the Generalist milestone require?** Official answer: in a standard game, 2 production of each of the 6 standard resources; in a Corporate Era game, 1 production of each.  
  "Official answer: Standard game: 2 production of each the 6 standard resources. Corporate Era game: 1 production of each." ([Jacob, 2017-08-28](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1838190/article/26760872#26760872))
- **Do production bonuses from Prelude cards count towards the Elysium Generalist milestone?** Yes. Prelude production and tags count just like those from corporations and project cards. The 'extra' production in the Elysium rule refers only to the +1 on all production in the standard game.  
  "The prelude production and tags count, just as corporations and project cards. The 'extra' production refers to the +1 on all production in the standard game, nothing else." ([Jacob, 2018-07-02](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2018281/article/29432153#29432153))

### Hellas South Pole

- **Can the 6 M€ for placing on the Hellas South Pole be paid with M€ gained from other placement effects of the same action?** Yes. The 6 M€ at the South Pole is part of the effect, not the card's cost, so you can perform it in your preferred order and use M€ you gain from other effects (like Moss with Viral Enhancers). An actual cost must be paid first and cannot be paid from resources gained by the effects.  
  "The same goes for the south pole - it requires you to lose 6MC, but it is actually part of the effect, not the cost, so you can perform it in your preferred order and get away with it!" ([Jacob, 2018-02-27](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1705209/article/28391249#28391249))
- **Can the -6 M€ on the Hellas South Pole be paid later in the action, like the plants on Moss?** Yes. It can be paid later during the action, as with the -1/-2 plants on the Moss cards, as long as you are able to finish the complete action with all its effects.  
  "The -6 MC on the South Pole can be payed for later during the action, just as the -1/-2 plants on the Moss cards, as long as you are able to finish the complete action with all its effects." ([Jacob, 2018-06-10](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1705209/article/29279781#29279781))
- **On the Hellas South Pole, is the 6 M€ still required if there are no oceans left to place?** Yes. Placing the ocean is ignored if oceans are maxed, but losing 6 M€ is not ignored and must be performed for the action to be taken.  
  "In the same way, placing an ocean tile is ignored if maxed, while losing 6 MC is not, and so has to be performed in order for the action to be taken." ([Jacob, 2018-09-15](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1705209/article/30001005#30001005))

### Map contents and solo play

- **Can the Hellas & Elysium maps be used for solo play, and are their milestones and awards used?** The Hellas & Elysium maps are for 1-5 players. In solo play the maps' new placement bonuses and ocean configuration apply, but the new milestones and awards are not used.  
  "The new milestones and awards are not used in solo play though." ([Jacob, 2017-01-15](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1708188/article/24768420#24768420))
- **What does the Hellas & Elysium expansion add to play?** It is only a double-sided map with no new cards. Each side has 5 new milestones, 5 new awards, a new ocean placement configuration and many new placement bonuses; otherwise play is the same.  
  "Each side contains 5 new milestones and 5 new awards, a new configuration of ocean placements and a lot of new placement bonuses. However, the play is the same, just varied." ([Jonathan, 2017-01-16](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1708725/article/24774126#24774126)) `intent`

### Milestones and awards sets

- **Why do milestones and awards come in fixed sets per map rather than being randomized?** Sets can be designed for specific maps, are easier to balance, avoid overlapping milestones/awards, and are hard to expand with new balanced ones. Randomized milestones and awards give variation but do not improve the game; this is the designers' stated reason, not a ban on the variant.  
  "Using randomized M&As provides interesting variation, but it does not improve the game." ([Jacob, 2017-08-09](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1826897/article/26593156#26593156)) `intent`

## Cards


### Lava Flows

- **Where can Lava Flows be placed on the Elysium and Hellas maps?** On the Elysium map Lava Flows has four new target sites. On the Hellas map it is playable without its printed placement restrictions, so it can be placed 'anywhere'. The restrictions are removed so players need not take cards out when switching maps.  
  "Lava Flows has four new target sites on the Elysium map. On the Hellas map, it will instead be playable without its printed restrictions, so it can be placed 'anywhere'." ([Jacob, 2017-01-16](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1708725/article/24776113#24776113))
- **On which of the new map sides does Lava Flows have designated target spaces?** On one of the two new sides the allowed Lava Flows spaces are marked in bold, as on the main board. (Per the other Hellas rulings, on the other side it can go anywhere.)  
  "One of the sides has marked in bold where it can go, just as on the main board" ([Jonathan, 2017-07-27](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1819678/article/26485489#26485489))

### Noctis City

- **Where may Noctis City be placed on the Hellas and Elysium maps, which lack the Noctis Labyrinthus spot?** On the new maps Noctis City can be placed anywhere (it loses its old reserved spot but is fully playable). Fryxen later clarified in another thread that it is placed like a normal city, see the 2017-08-24 ruling.  
  "Therefore, Noctis City can be placed anywhere, and will miss its old sweet spot, but be fully playable." ([Jacob, 2017-01-16](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1708725/article/24776113#24776113)) `superseded` by [a later post](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1820335/article/26735937#26735937)
- **On the Hellas/Elysium maps (where the rules insert says Noctis City may be placed on any non-ocean area), does the normal city restriction of not being adjacent to another city apply?** Yes. The map rules override Noctis City's exception, so it is placed just like a normal city: not on ocean areas and not beside other cities.  
  "The rules override the Noctis City exception, so it is placed just like a normal city (i.e. not on ocean areas, and not beside other cities)." ([Jacob, 2017-08-24](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1820335/article/26735937#26735937))
- **Where can Noctis City (and Lava Flows) be placed on the Hellas and Elysium maps; is the usual city-adjacency restriction dropped?** On the Hellas map, Lava Flows and Noctis City may be placed on any non-ocean area. On the Elysium map, Lava Flows must be placed on one of the 4 volcanoes (names in bold on the board) and Noctis City can be placed anywhere.  
  "On the Hellas map you may place Lava Flows and Noctic City on any non-ocean area. On the Elysium map you must place the Lava Flows on any of the 4 volcanos" ([Enoch, 2017-08-28](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1836544/article/26761061#26761061))
- **Why do Noctis City and Lava Flows have no special locations on the Hellas & Elysium maps?** Thematic reasons: neither map has the Noctis region, and only Elysium has volcanos. Rather than requiring removal of those cards from the deck, the placement restrictions were removed for these maps.  
  "neither map has the Noctis region, and only Elysium has volcanos. Rather than demanding their removal from the deck we thought it simpler to remove their restrictions." ([Jacob, 2017-11-26](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1890843/article/27536823#27536823)) `intent` `inferred`
- **Why not officially ban Noctis City, Lava Flows (140) and Lava Tube Settlement (P37) on Hellas instead of removing their placement restrictions?** The official map rules remove the placement restrictions of these cards rather than banning them, because sorting them out of the deck when changing maps is more effort and the rule makes switching maps easy.  
  "Because it is a bigger effort to sort them out from the huge deck than to just remove their restrictions. Makes it easy to switch maps as you like." ([Jacob, 2019-11-14](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2312348/article/33373658#33373658)) `intent`
- **Why are volcanic-area cards restricted on Elysium but unrestricted on Hellas, and could the cards be banned on Hellas instead?** Hellas has no volcanic sites, so the cards are unrestricted there by official rule. Fryxen agrees disallowing them would be more thematic and consistent, but the official rule stays as is for convenience; players who don't mind sorting the cards out may do so.  
  "BUT it a nuisance to try to sort those cards out when changing maps. That is why we chose the official rule. If you don't mind going through all the cards to sort them out, I think that is a more thematic solution." ([Jacob, 2019-11-26](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2312348/article/33460402#33460402)) `intent` `house-rule-ok`
- **How were placement restrictions handled for earlier maps when the restricted feature was unavailable?** When Hellas & Elysium was released, Noctis City lost its placement restriction because it made the card unplayable, and on Hellas all cards restricted to volcanic areas lost that restriction so they remain playable. This avoids removing cards from the deck.  
  "Noctis City lost its placement restriction since it made the card unplayable, and on Hellas all cards restricted to volcanic areas lost that restriction to make them playable." ([Jacob, 2024-01-29](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3234520/article/43694248#43694248)) `intent`

### Phobos Space Haven

- **Does Phobos Space Haven keep its reserved placement area on the Hellas and Elysium maps?** Yes. Phobos Space Haven still has its reserved area in space; it is not freed to be placed anywhere on the new maps.  
  "Phobos Space Haven still has its reserved area in space, though." ([Jonathan, 2017-07-27](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1819678/article/26485489#26485489))

### Poseidon

- **How should Poseidon's mandatory first-action colony be resolved when it cannot legally be placed on any colony tile?** No fixed official rule; use a house ruling that does not disadvantage the player too much. For the case where only Miranda is unavailable he would allow the colony on Miranda, since the alternatives (doubling up, fizzling, a 4th colony) are more intrusive or give an unfair advantage. An official ruling was not given.  
  "If you come across an edge case like this, do a house ruling that doesn't disadvantage the player too much (in this case I would allow a colony on Miranda" ([Jacob, 2024-07-29](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3341272/article/44630207#44630207)) `intent` `house-rule-ok`
