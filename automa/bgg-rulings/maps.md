# Maps (Amazonis, Vastitas, Utopia, Cimmeria): official BGG rulings

9 rulings posted on BoardGameGeek by FryxGames, Stronghold Games and the Automa designers. [README.md](README.md) says who is who, how this was built, and what the tags mean. Under each heading the rulings run in date order, so a later one can overrule an earlier one.


## Rules


### Amazonis Planitia

- **What is the solo player's starting TR on the Amazonis Planitia board (13 generations with Prelude, 15 without)?** Starting TR is the normal 14.  
  "Starting TR is the normal 14." ([Jacob, 2024-07-25](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3339110/article/44612726#44612726))
- **Is the Amazonis Planitia map playable with the MarsBot automa?** No. The automa does not support Amazonis Planitia: it was tuned for the pace of the standard-sized maps and can only simulate a 2 player game.  
  "No, the automa doesn't support that map, as it would have required massive changes to how it operated" ([Nick, 2024-11-18](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3407536/article/45199783#45199783))

### Cardboard maps and neoprene mats

- **Why do the milestones and awards differ between the neoprene maps, the cardboard maps and the Milestones & Awards tiles, and which are official?** The neoprene versions of the fan-made maps are the original fan-made versions; the cardboard maps are the official, FryxGames-rebalanced versions (the older maps and Amazonis are identical in both). Milestones and awards printed on a map are balanced only for that map, while the M&A tiles were balanced against each other, which caused a few changes; none were big enough to change the official maps.  
  "So when comparing the neoprene to the cardboard versions, the neoprene are the original fan-made ones, and the cardboard maps are the official ones." ([Jacob, 2024-08-04](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3344244/article/44660810#44660810)) `intent`
- **Why do the cardboard maps differ from the neoprene mats (names of milestones/awards, placement bonuses, region names, milestone balance)?** The cardboard maps are official and were changed from the fan-made neoprene mats: milestone/award names were changed to avoid duplicates with existing ones, the Mars projection differs so a couple of placement bonuses moved one step and some region names shifted, and milestones/awards were rebalanced so sets can be randomized. The neoprene mats were left unchanged as the fan community creation; they are not updated to the cardboard versions.  
  "We have one set of milestones and awards specifically balanced to be randomized, each map has its own set of milestones and award to balanced against each other, and the neoprene mats are the result of our wonderful fan community." ([Jonathan, 2024-08-19](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3352893/article/44737861#44737861))

### MSL Curiosity

- **If Colonies is not used, how is the Terra Cimmeria MSL Curiosity placement bonus handled (ignore it, or pay the 5 M€)?** There is no official way to play without Colonies, but the intent is for products to be modular. When a map gives a bonus that does not apply to your setup, skip that entire bonus: the whole area loses both the colony bonus and the -5 M€ placement cost. (The statement that some awards and milestones may be removed was followed by a later post saying such awards and milestones are used normally.)  
  "when a map gives you a bonus or other feature that is not applicable to your setup, just skip that entire bonus. So some awards and milestones may be removed, and some placement bonuses may be blank." ([Jacob, 2024-01-29](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3234520/article/43694248#43694248)) `reversed`
- **With Colonies, if you have no valid spot to place a colony, can you still place a tile on the Terra Cimmeria MSL Curiosity space and pay 5 M€ for nothing?** No. If you cannot perform the placement effect (place a colony), you cannot place a tile on that space.  
  "If you can't perform the effect, you can't place a tile there." ([Jacob, 2024-02-08](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3242862/article/43755578#43755578))

### Playing without Colonies or Venus

- **If playing Amazonis without Colonies, what happens to the Constructor award (cities and colonies), and likewise the Terra Cimmeria Planetologist milestone without Venus?** In both cases the number of colonies / Venus tags is 0 and the award or milestone is used normally; it is not removed. The Milestones & Awards product can be used to randomise goals and exclude the ones that do not fit.  
  "In both cases the number of colonies / Venus tags is 0 and the award / milestone can be used normally." ([Jacob, 2024-02-08](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3242831/article/43755615#43755615))

## Cards


### Mining Rights

- **If Mining Rights is placed on a space that gives both steel and titanium, do you get both production types?** No, you choose one of them.  
  "No, you choose one." ([Jacob, 2025-01-07](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3436260/article/45442601#45442601))
- **Is the 'or' between steel and titanium on a Mining Rights space inclusive, so both can be claimed?** No. Since you do not get 2 production for placing on a 2-steel area, it would be unreasonable to get both resources when they are different.  
  "Since you don't get 2 production for placing on a 2 steel area, it would be quite unreasonable to get both if they were different." ([Jacob, 2025-01-09](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3436260/article/45455886#45455886))
