# Milestones & Awards: official BGG rulings

9 rulings posted on BoardGameGeek by FryxGames, Stronghold Games and the Automa designers. [README.md](README.md) says who is who, how this was built, and what the tags mean. Under each heading the rulings run in date order, so a later one can overrule an earlier one.


## Rules


### 2v2 milestones and awards

- **How do the 2v2 milestones and awards work, and can they be combined with the modular Milestones/Awards expansion?** They come on two double-sided tiles, one with 5 milestones per side and one with 5 awards per side, giving 4 combinations. They are balanced for 2v2 with higher demands and some are tailored to team play, so they cannot be combined with the modular Milestones/Awards.  
  "The milestones are balanced specifically for 2v2 with higher demands since it is easier to specialize in 2v2, and some of the milestones and awards are uniquely tailored to teamplay, so no, they can't be combined with the modular Milestones/Awards." ([Jacob, 2026-05-12](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3706792/article/47670699#47670699)) `variant`

### Briber (milestone)

- **If a corporation makes newly revealed milestones cost 0 to claim, does the Briber milestone still cost its extra 12 M€?** Yes, the Briber milestone's extra 12 M€ is still paid on top of the (reduced) claim cost. The corporation referred to is not named in the post.  
  "Yes." ([Jacob, 2023-09-13](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3148485/article/42948134#42948134)) `inferred`

### Map-specific milestones and awards

- **Why are some map-printed milestones and awards, such as Polar Explorer, not included as tiles in the Milestones & Awards product?** Milestones and awards tied to a specific map feature (e.g. Polar Explorer and the south pole) were left out because they would be unthematic on other maps; a few may also have been too similar to others. New ones were added instead.  
  "Some milestones and awards are tied to features of their specific map, such as Polar Explorer being tied to the south pole. It would be unthematic on other maps, which is why it wasn't included." ([Jacob, 2024-07-28](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3340768/article/44627123#44627123)) `intent`
- **Can the normal milestones and awards printed on a map (such as Polar Explorer) be used together with the Milestones & Awards tiles?** Yes, the pre-printed competitions can be combined with the M&A tiles. Fryxen describes a game where the M&A tiles were randomized, a couple that clashed were removed, and a couple of the pre-printed competitions on the board were used instead. The M&A tiles are balanced against each other so that competitions are equally attractive.  
  "Sure you can use normal competitions in conjunction with the M&A product." ([Jacob, 2024-09-05](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3340768/article/44819335#44819335)) `variant` `house-rule-ok`

### Milestone requirements

- **Why do some milestones in the new products have different requirements (Builder 7 tags, Terraformer 29 TR) than the original ones?** The new milestones were deliberately rebalanced against each other across maps (some maps had earlier milestones than others), with some extra general tweaks guided by data and feedback. The post does not say whether the originals must be replaced on existing maps.  
  "They were rebalanced to each other; some maps had generally earlier milestones than other maps etc, but these should all be competitive with each other." ([Jacob, 2024-07-25](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3339652/article/44616222#44616222)) `intent` `inferred`

### Politician (award)

- **If a player holds the leader of the dominant party at game end, does that leader count 1 or 2 toward the Politician award (most Party Leaders and influence combined)?** It counts 2: one for being a party leader and one for the influence the dominant party leader grants.  
  "Yes, the dominant party leader would contribute 2 to the total." ([Jacob, 2024-07-28](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3340698/article/44624835#44624835))
- **Is a new chairman appointed at the end of the game before the Politician award is counted?** No. The game ends before the Turmoil step, so no new chairman is appointed at the end of the game.  
  "Game end is before the Turmoil step, so no new chairman is appointed at the end of the game (just clarifying)." ([Jacob, 2024-07-28](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3340698/article/44624835#44624835))

### Space tag milestone

- **Do space events count toward the space-tag milestone in Milestones & Awards?** No: space events do not count for the space tag milestone.  
  "For the space tag milestone, remember that space events don't count." ([Jacob, 2023-09-18](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3155551/article/42970691#42970691))

### Venus milestone and award

- **Why are there no Hoverlord and Venuphile tiles in Milestones & Awards, and when are they used?** Venus needs those goals to be relevant enough, so the Venus award and milestone are always included when you use Venus Next.  
  "Yes, Venus requires those goals to make it relevant enough. That's why they're always in if you use Venus Next." ([Jacob, 2023-09-18](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3155551/article/42970691#42970691))
