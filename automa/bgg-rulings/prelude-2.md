## Rules


### Choosing from more corporations and preludes

- **Can you let players choose from more than 2 corporations?** The official rule is 2 corporations, to make people try weaker corps. There is a common house rule to choose from 3, which Fryxen thinks is fine, especially with the many corporations now. He thinks 4 is probably too many.  
  "there is a common house rule to choose from 3, and I think that's fine too" ([Jacob, 2023-09-25](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3158988/article/43007350#43007350)) `variant` `house-rule-ok` `hedged` `superseded` by [a later post](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3080317/article/44413299#44413299)
- **What new optional rule does Prelude 2 add for selecting preludes and corporations?** Prelude 2 has a new optional rule to choose from 6 preludes (and 3 corporations). The exact dealing procedure is not described in this post. He also says there are new ways to get extra preludes and that some of the new preludes are expansion dependent.  
  "there is a new optional rule to choose from 6 preludes (and 3 corps)." ([Jacob, 2024-06-13](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3080317/article/44413299#44413299)) `variant`
- **What is the intended starting selection with the Prelude 2 optional rule?** He considers 10 project cards, 6 preludes and 3 corporations a suitable selection for the current state of the game; 4 corps would be too much choice.  
  "I think 10 + 6 + 3 is a suitable selection for the current state of TM." ([Jacob, 2024-06-16](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3158988/article/44429671#44429671)) `variant`
- **What optional rule does Prelude 2 add for the starting choice of corporations and preludes, and can it combine with the Focus variant?** Prelude 2 adds an optional rule to choose from 3 corporations (and 6 preludes). It can be combined with the Focus variant: draw 1 corporation from the select pool and 2 from the general pool. The development team advises against more than 3 corporations.  
  "With Prelude 2, there will be a new optional rule to choose from 3 corps (and 6 preludes). It can be combined with the Focus variant to draw 1 from a select pool and 2 from the general pool of corps." ([Jacob, 2024-06-23](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3322401/article/44461444#44461444)) `variant`
- **What is the new optional rule in Prelude 2 about the starting selection?** A new optional rule allows you to choose from 6 preludes and 3 corporations, making it easier to find good situations for situational preludes.  
  "the new optional rule allowing you to choose from 6 preludes and 3 corporations" ([Jacob, 2024-07-16](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3334481/article/44570971#44570971)) `variant`
- **How does the Prelude 2 'choose from more' option apply to the solo game?** Optionally choose your corporation from 3 instead of 2, and choose your 2 preludes (never 3) from 6 instead of 4. This makes solo a little easier and is suggested when using many extra cards.  
  "Prelude 2 added an option to choose your corp from 3 instead of 2, and choosing your 2 (never 3!) preludes from 6 instead of from 4." ([Jacob, 2025-01-20](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3444381/article/45515542#45515542)) `variant`
- **What is the new optional setup rule from Prelude 2?** Optional rule: during setup choose from 3 corporations and 6 preludes (rather than 2 corporations and 4 preludes), to make it easier to find a corporation and 2 preludes that suit your starting hand.  
  "This is also the main reason for the new optional rule from Prelude 2: Choose from 3 corps and 6 preludes during setup." ([Jacob, 2025-10-18](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3594343/article/46756580#46756580)) `variant`
- **Can the 3 corporations / 6 preludes setup be used without Prelude 2?** Yes, it can be used, but Fryxen would not use it with only base + Prelude, since it is designed for a larger card and corporation pool; choosing from 2 was meant to push players out of their comfort zone.  
  "Sure you can use it. But if you only play base + prelude, I wouldn't. It's designed to fit a larger card pool (and corp pool)." ([Jacob, 2025-10-18](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3594343/article/46756977#46756977)) `variant` `house-rule-ok`

### Counting a card's own tag

- **Does a Prelude 2 card whose effect counts tags 'including this card' count its own tag?** Fryxen answered 'Mostly correct' to a list in which this was answered yes: a card always includes its own tag (and possibly tags of other cards played in the same action) even when the text does not remind you. His reply only comments on points 3 to 7. The specific card is not named in the post (image).  
  "Mostly correct." ([Jacob, 2024-07-15](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3334230/article/44565901#44565901)) `hedged` `inferred`

### Maps and expansion-coupled bonuses

- **Do expansion-specific placement bonuses and restrictions apply on maps when you play without that expansion?** No. Bonuses and restrictions such as colonies, delegates and volcanic areas only matter in games with the corresponding expansions and maps; without them you can ignore them. The same goes for Noctis City: you can place it normally if there is no reserved area for it.  
  "If you play without those, you can ignore these bonuses / restrictions. Same with Noctis City; you can place it normally if there isn't a reserved area for it." ([Jacob, 2024-05-15](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3260575/article/44267734#44267734))
- **Can a map's placement bonus tied to an expansion be ignored without that expansion?** Yes. If you play on a map with placement bonuses coupled to certain expansions, you can ignore the whole bonus if you do not play with that expansion.  
  "If you play on a map with placement bonuses coupled to certain expansions, you can ignore that (whole) bonus if you don't play with that expansion." ([Jacob, 2024-12-03](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3354996/article/45278974#45278974))

### Order of effects

- **After receiving the Venus track 1-card bonus, can you discard that card for an effect such as 'Discard 1 card to terraform Venus 1 step'?** Yes ('Mostly correct'): the two icons are separate and the text does not require the discard to come before the Venus increase.  
  "Mostly correct." ([Jacob, 2024-07-15](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3334230/article/44565901#44565901)) `hedged`
- **Does 'Discard 1 card to terraform Venus 1 step' require discarding first?** No: without an asterisk the icons take precedence over the wording and the order of effects is up to you.  
  "Good point, but without an asterisk, the icons take precedence and the order of effects is up to you." ([Jacob, 2024-07-15](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3334230/article/44565919#44565919))

### Prelude 2 requirements and contents

- **Is the original Prelude required to play Prelude 2?** Officially yes, Prelude is required, because Prelude 2 alone does not provide enough preludes with 5 players and no other expansion. In most cases it would work without.  
  "Officially, Prelude is required, because it will not be enough with 5 players and no other expansion. In most cases it would work though." ([Jacob, 2023-05-11](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3080317/article/42261457#42261457)) `intent`
- **How many Prelude 2 corporations, preludes and project cards are usable depending on which expansions are in play?** Prelude 2 has 54 cards. With Venus, Colonies and Turmoil all in play: 5 corporations, 25 preludes, 24 project cards. Venus only: 5 corporations, 19 preludes, 7 project cards. Colonies only: 5, 19, 3. Turmoil only: 5, 19, 8. None of those expansions: 5 corporations, 16 preludes, 0 project cards. Six project cards require two of the expansions. He notes the numbers may shift slightly in final card adjustments.  
  "If you don't use any of those expansions: 5 corporations, 16 preludes, and 0 project cards." ([Jacob, 2023-05-15](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3083671/article/42277478#42277478)) `hedged`
- **Can any Prelude 2 project cards be used without Venus, Colonies or Turmoil, and are preludes tied to expansions?** None of the Prelude 2 project cards are suitable for play without expansions, so the number of usable project cards is zero. Each expansion has 3 preludes tied to its mechanics; some neutral preludes also reference them (for example one referencing planet tags and one using floaters) but not enough to upset balance.  
  "None of the project cards in Prelude 2 are suitable for play without expansions." ([Jacob, 2023-05-15](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3083671/article/42277821#42277821))
- **If you play with all expansions except Turmoil, which Prelude 2 cards must be removed?** Remove the 3 Turmoil-tied preludes and the 8 Turmoil project cards, plus a few cards that require Turmoil and another expansion. All cards are marked with any expansion they require.  
  "Yes, plus a few that require Turmoil and another expansion. All cards are marked with any expansion they require, so they will be easy to sort out." ([Jacob, 2023-05-15](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3083671/article/42280215#42280215))
- **Are Prelude 2 corporations tied to expansions?** No. All the Prelude 2 corporations are independent and can be played with or without any expansions.  
  "All the corporations are independent and can be played with/without any expansions." ([Jacob, 2023-05-15](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3083671/article/42280245#42280245))
- **Can Prelude 2 be played without the original Prelude?** Omitting the original preludes means lacking all the ordinary preludes, so they are meant to be played together. You can try Prelude 2 alone, but the designer does not want to be responsible for problems that might arise.  
  "But you can certainly try with only Prelude 2 if you want. I just don't want to be responsible for problems that might arise." ([Jacob, 2024-06-13](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3080317/article/44413299#44413299)) `intent`
- **Can Prelude 2 be used with only the base game?** Officially it requires the base game and Prelude. In theory you could use the 5 corporations and 15 of the 25 preludes (and none of the 24 project cards), but you really need Prelude 1 for it to be any good except for the corps; having at least one of Venus/Colonies/Turmoil is recommended, preferably two. Using the cards not dependent on those expansions does no harm.  
  "You really need Prelude 1 for it to be any good (except for the corps)." ([Jacob, 2026-01-30](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3652654/article/47230601#47230601))

### Prelude fizzle rule

- **Does the prelude refund (reveal and discard an unplayable prelude for 15 MC) apply only when Prelude 2 is included?** No. The rule is universal for preludes. They should have had it before Prelude 2, but the need was extremely rare.  
  "The rule is universal for preludes. We should have had it before Prelude 2, but the need was extremely rare." ([Jacob, 2024-07-15](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3334230/article/44565901#44565901))
- **When can the prelude fizzle rule apply, e.g. for secret prelude choices?** The fizzle rule is for mistakes and mishaps. Whenever you make a secret choice of preludes, one can be or become unplayable and then the fizzle rule applies. The WG Project action falls in this category since opponents are not shown your options.  
  "Basically, the fizzle rule is for mistakes and mishaps." ([Jacob, 2024-07-16](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3334230/article/44570844#44570844))

### What counts as an action

- **Does Suitable Infrastructure trigger during the prelude phase / setup?** Not quite as framed: playing your corporation and preludes during setup also count as actions outside the action phase, so effects tied to actions can apply there.  
  "Playing your corp and preludes during setup also count as actions I think, outside the action phase." ([Jacob, 2024-07-17](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3335155/article/44576777#44576777)) `hedged`
- **What counts as an action in the rules, e.g. for preludes, Head Start and final greenery conversion?** Everything that uses action mechanics counts as an action: playing your corporation and each prelude, and any card that lets you play another card creates a new action that suspends the original. Head Start creates new actions while its action is suspended. Even final greenery conversions count since they use a normal action mechanic. He frames this as a provisional ruling ('we'll see how long this holds').  
  "Everything that uses action mechanics are counted as actions." ([Jacob, 2024-11-01](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3395635/article/45111443#45111443)) `hedged`
- **What does 'as your first action' mean now that preludes count as actions?** Effects like 'as your first action' must now mean 'as your first action in the action phase', so as not to interfere with the playing of preludes; this matches the original intent.  
  "effects like 'as your first action' must now mean 'as your first action in the action phase' in order to not mess with the playing of preludes." ([Jacob, 2024-11-04](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3395635/article/45128719#45128719))

### Wild tag

- **Can wild tags work outside the action phase, e.g. in preludes or final scoring?** Wild tags can work outside the action phase in quite rare cases, but not for final scoring. This applies to other effects that refer to actions as well.  
  "So wild tags can work outside the action phase, but the cases are quite rare, and it doesn't apply to final scoring." ([Jacob, 2024-11-01](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3395635/article/45111443#45111443)) `hedged`
- **Is the earlier ruling that wild tags only work in the action phase still valid?** No. That ruling was meant to exclude the solar phase and end-game scoring. Because cards now create actions outside the action phase, limiting wild tags to the action phase is no longer valid, though uses during setup are expected to be very rare. Things that behave like actions should be considered actions unless there is a special reason.  
  "Now it just seems to me that things that behave like actions should also be considered actions, unless there is a special reason for it not to." ([Jacob, 2024-11-04](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3395635/article/45128719#45128719))
- **Does a card with a wild tag count as a tag type or as no tag for awards such as Administrator (most cards with no tag)?** Fryxen says a wild tag is not a tag type but a reminder of its function, being able to count as any tag type. He does not state directly how it counts for Administrator or Highlander; he agrees with the poster that it does not count as an actual tag during award counting.  
  "Snake is right, a wild tag is not a tag type but a reminder of its function - tobe able to count as any tag type." ([Jacob, 2024-12-29](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3430089/article/45396534#45396534)) `intent` `hedged`

## Cards


### Applied Science

- **If Applied Science is your only card with a resource and has exactly 1, is the option to move a resource to another card invalid?** Correct: after paying the action cost Applied Science has 0 resources and there is no valid target, so the only choice is to gain a standard resource. Fryxen adds that trying to put the resource back on Applied Science would be a very fringe use.  
  "Correct. Although trying to put is back to Applied Science would be a very fringe use." ([Jacob, 2023-09-06](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3148765/article/42907957#42907957))
- **Can Applied Science add a resource to Board of Directors, which is then used to draw a prelude in the same generation?** Yes. The Board of Directors resources are resources, so the combo works.  
  "Correct. That is a very fun combo indeed." ([Jacob, 2025-07-28](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3549528/article/46389060#46389060))

### Board of Directors

- **If you pay 12 MC for the Board of Directors action and cannot play the prelude, do you discard it and get 15 MC?** No. If you do not actually play the prelude, the option to play it for 12 MC is invalid.  
  "No. If you don't actually play the prelude, then the option to play it for 12 MC is invalid." ([Jacob, 2024-07-15](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3334230/article/44565901#44565901)) `inferred` `superseded` by [a later post](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3478406/article/45816668#45816668)
- **Can you try to play an unplayable prelude with Board of Directors and fizzle it?** His current ruling is that you cannot try to play a prelude that is unplayable, but he calls it a tricky rules situation. It is rarely relevant since you would waste a director resource for just 3 MC.  
  "my current ruling is you can't try and play a prelude that is unplayable, but it is indeed a tricky rules situation." ([Jacob, 2024-07-16](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3334230/article/44570844#44570844)) `hedged` `superseded` by [a later post](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3478406/article/45816668#45816668)
- **When using Board of Directors to play a drawn prelude, when are the 12 MC and the director resource committed, and what if the prelude (e.g. Merger) then fails?** You can change your mind about playing the prelude until you have new information. Once you play it (e.g. Merger) you are committed; if it then fails the prelude fizzles and you get 15 MC for the 12 MC and director resource already paid.  
  "I would say you can change your mind about playing the prelude until you have new information. Once you play Merger, you're committed, and you can choose any of the corps." ([Jacob, 2025-03-17](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3478406/article/45816668#45816668)) `hedged`
- **May a player use Board of Directors to play a prelude they know will fizzle?** Yes, you can choose to play a prelude you know will fizzle.  
  "You can even choose to play a prelude you know will fizzle." ([Jacob, 2025-03-17](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3478406/article/45816668#45816668))
- **Can a player be forced not to play a prelude with Board of Directors because it will not be possible to resolve it?** No. The game does not know what the player knows, and preludes fizzle (the player gets compensation) instead of bouncing back like other actions that cannot be resolved. Fryxen notes this is a tricky area with edge cases.  
  "Well, the game doesn't know what you know. And preludes don't bounce like other actions when they don't work out, they fizzle." ([Jacob, 2025-03-17](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3478406/article/45817562#45817562))
- **How many preludes can you take with Board of Directors per generation?** Only 1 prelude each generation, and taking it costs 12 MC.  
  "you only get 1 prelude each generation, and if you take it, it costs 12 MC." ([Jacob, 2025-07-28](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3549528/article/46389071#46389071))

### Double Down

- **If Double Down copies Board of Directors, do you get 4 more director resources?** No. Double Down copies only the direct effect, which would put 4 directors on Double Down itself; since Double Down defines no use for them they cannot be collected, so you get none.  
  "Double Down copies only the direct effect, which means it tries to put 4 directors on ITSELF." ([Jacob, 2024-07-14](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3331165/article/44559570#44559570))
- **Can Double Down copy Preservation Program?** Yes: he saw it used at FryxCon to copy Preservation Program and get 5 TR without that prelude's negative ability.  
  "One cool use of Double Down I saw at FryxCon was when someone copied Preservation Program to get 5 TR without that prelude's negative ability" ([Jacob, 2024-07-14](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3331165/article/44559570#44559570)) `intent`
- **Can Double Down copy a prelude's ongoing (blue) ability such as Terraforming Deal's?** No. Double Down only copies the direct effect, not any 'blue' ability.  
  "Double Down only copies the direct effect, not any 'blue' ability." ([Jacob, 2024-07-23](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3334481/article/44601727#44601727)) `inferred`
- **If you play Double Down to copy Merger and Merger cannot be resolved, what happens?** Double Down fizzles and you gain 15 MC instead. You cannot change the target once you have new information.  
  "If you Double Down on Merger and it fizzles, then Double Down fizzles and you gain 15 MC instead. You can not change target once you have new information." ([Jacob, 2025-03-17](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3478406/article/45816668#45816668))

### Early Colonization

- **If you cannot raise all colony tracks 2 steps, can you not play that prelude?** Correct: you need to be able to increase the colony tracks. Colony tiles that have not been activated yet (for example Miranda without any animal cards) count as having no tracks and do not block you from playing Early Colonization.  
  "Caveat mentioned already that colony tiles that have not been activated yet (e.g. Miranda without any animal cards) count as having no tracks, and so they don't block you from playing Early Colonization." ([Jacob, 2024-07-15](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3334230/article/44565901#44565901))

### Industrial Complex

- **Can Cyberia Systems or Robotic Workforce copy Industrial Complex's production box, and what does it do?** Yes with both. You do not pay the 18 MC again, since it is not part of the production box. Copying it repeats the production effect: increase every production lower than 1 to 1 (not +1 in total). The production box includes any special rules attached to that production.  
  "Correct. The production box is not only the icons, but including any special rules attached to that production." ([Jacob, 2024-12-29](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3430226/article/45396575#45396575))

### Kaguya Tech

- **When Kaguya Tech places a city, does Philares trigger?** Yes, Philares triggers since a tile is placed (it can be a big payout). The card was not yet published at the time and the wording may change.  
  "1) Yes, Philares triggers since you place a tile (and it can actually be a quite big payout!)." ([Jacob, 2023-09-17](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3154812/article/42964868#42964868)) `hedged`
- **If you play Kaguya Tech as Philares, do you get your Philares adjacency bonuses a second time?** Yes, you get your adjacency bonuses a second time. To get maximum use you would need a greenery tile completely surrounded by opponents' tiles, though 3 adjacencies is easily reached.  
  "Yep." ([Jacob, 2024-01-24](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3232914/article/43667544#43667544))

### Mars Nomads

- **Can the Nomads cube move back to an area it has already been in, or stay in its current area?** It can move back to an area it was in before, but it cannot 'move' to the area it currently stands in.  
  "2) Yes, you can move back to a place you've already been, but you can't 'move' to the same area you're already standing in." ([Jacob, 2023-09-17](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3154812/article/42964868#42964868))
- **Do the Nomads get ocean adjacency bonuses?** Yes, they get ocean adjacency bonuses as well as the placement bonus.  
  "3) Yes, ocean adjacency too." ([Jacob, 2023-09-17](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3154812/article/42964868#42964868))
- **Does the Nomads cube interact with Arcadian Communities markers (can the AC action be used where the Nomads are)?** The Nomads do not block Arcadian Communities placement. Since Arcadian Communities reserves areas, the Nomads cannot move there; the Nomads do not reserve their area, they only block tile placement there. The card was unpublished, so wording could change.  
  "Edit: Since AC reserves areas, the Nomads can not go there. In contrast, the Nomads doesn't 'reserve' its area, just blocks placement there." ([Jacob, 2023-09-17](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3154812/article/42964868#42964868)) `hedged`
- **Do Land Claim and Arcadian Communities reservations block the Nomads?** Yes. The Nomads can only move to unreserved areas, so areas reserved by Arcadian Communities or Land Claim block them (with the wording at the time).  
  "Since they can only move to unreserved areas, both AC and Land Claim block it. With the current wording anyway." ([Jacob, 2023-09-17](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3154812/article/42966383#42966383)) `hedged`
- **Do the Nomads get the placement bonus when the cube is first placed?** No, not when first placed. But the cube can be moved in the same generation to gain the bonus.  
  "And no, they don't get the bonus when first placing the cube. But they can move it to gain the bonus on the same generation." ([Jacob, 2023-09-17](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3154812/article/42966383#42966383))
- **As Arcadian Communities, may you move the Nomads onto a hex with your own marker, or put multiple markers on one area?** No. Arcadian Communities reserves areas, so the Nomads cannot go there even if reserved for you, and Arcadian Communities cannot place multiple markers on the same area since the first one reserves it.  
  "No. AC reserves areas, so the Nomads can't go there, even if they are reserved for you." ([Jacob, 2023-09-17](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3154812/article/42966562#42966562))
- **Which tile-placement triggers fire off the Nomads moving?** Mining Guild triggers off placement bonuses, not tile placement, so it triggers off the Nomads. Philares, Mars First and similar cards trigger off tile placement, which the Nomads do not do.  
  "Mining Guild triggers off placement bonuses, not tile placement (unless we changed the wording and I don't remember doing that), so Mining Guild does trigger off the Nomads." ([Jacob, 2023-09-19](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3154812/article/42978389#42978389))
- **Does the answer for Mining Guild depend on the card's printed wording (older text referred to steel/titanium from placement bonuses)?** Fryxen concedes the Mining Guild text was changed in the third edition, so the outcome depends on which edition's wording a player has. No further resolution is stated.  
  "So then it depends on which edition you're playing with" ([Jacob, 2023-09-19](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3154812/article/42978617#42978617)) `intent` `hedged`
- **Can the Nomads be moved onto a space whose placement bonus you cannot fulfil (cannot pay the MC cost, no delegate or colony to place)?** Confirmed as correct: if you still cannot pay after gaining MC from other trigger effects you cannot move there. Delegates and colonies are not covered by the three exceptions (only global parameters at max, resources you cannot collect, and removing resources from any player), so if you cannot place a delegate or colony you cannot place on or move to a hex with that bonus. Oceans, temperature and oxygen at max do not block.  
  "Correct. With the caveat that expansion-specific bonuses like colonies, delegates, and volcanic areas (bold names) are only relevant in games with the corresponding expansions and maps." ([Jacob, 2024-05-15](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3260575/article/44267734#44267734))
- **What counts as an empty area for placing Mars Nomads: not having a tile, or also no placement bonus?** An empty area means not having a tile. Fryxen confirmed an answer to this ('Correct').
