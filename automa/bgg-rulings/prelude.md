## Rules


### Choosing preludes

- **When are Prelude cards chosen: in step 6 with corporations and project cards, or when preludes are played?** Step 7 starts with everyone examining their starting options (corporations, projects, preludes, colony tiles in solo), then players make all choices and discard unchosen cards (1 corporation, x project cards and 2 preludes). Then corporations are revealed and projects paid for, then preludes are processed. Preludes cannot be chosen during the play-your-preludes round; the Prelude rules should have said preludes are discarded together with the other cards.  
  "Then players make all their choices and discard their unchosen cards (1 corp, x projects and 2 preludes). Then corps are revealed and projects paid for, Then Preludes are processed." ([Jacob, 2022-06-08](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2880044/article/40231068#40231068)) `erratum`
- **When do you choose your preludes: before any corporations or preludes are played, or in step 7b?** You choose your preludes (and other cards) before any corporations or preludes are played. The rulebook's discard wording did not foresee the fizzle rule; a more correct text would discard unchosen preludes at the same time as unchosen project cards and corporations.  
  "You still choose your preludes (and other cards) before any corps or preludes are played." ([Jacob, 2024-09-06](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2993276/article/44821774#44821774))
- **Can players keep their options open and decide which preludes to play only after seeing others' choices?** No. The choice is made before revealing so it is not influenced by what others choose; no 'wiggle room' (e.g. against Recession). A fizzled prelude still gives 15 MC.  
  "The point of choosing before revealing is that the choice should NOT be influenced by what the others choose." ([Jacob, 2024-09-07](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2993276/article/44826021#44826021))
- **Which step do the rules say the prelude choice is made in?** Step 6 is where you choose your cards, including preludes; step 7b says to play your PICKED preludes and discard the others, so the choice is made in step 6, not 7b.  
  "Step 6: Choose your cards, including preludes. Step 7b: Play your PICKED preludes and discard the others." ([Jacob, 2024-09-09](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2993276/article/44835015#44835015))
- **If the discard in step 7b leaves room to change your choice unnoticed, is that allowed?** No. In 7b you play the 2 preludes that you picked in step 6, even if no one can tell whether you cheated. The discard should have been in step 6.  
  "The rule is that in 7b you play the 2 preludes that you picked (in step 6)." ([Jacob, 2024-09-09](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2993276/article/44837434#44837434))
- **Why is the prelude choice made in step 6, and may groups change it?** The reason is to make setup smooth and leave reacting to the action phase; the first player advantage is not considered significant. Groups can use a house rule if they prefer.  
  "But that is a matter of taste and you can go with a house rule if you prefer that." ([Jacob, 2024-09-09](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2993276/article/44838242#44838242)) `intent` `house-rule-ok`

### Combining Prelude with other rules

- **With Prelude and Venus Next, is the Solar Phase still needed?** The expansions are completely modular and can be used together, but the World Government Terraforming step can optionally be skipped.  
  "Completely modular, so yes, they can be used together. But the World Government Terraforming step can optionally be skipped." ([Jacob, 2018-04-01](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1968149/article/28702713#28702713))
- **Is the starting TR reduced with Prelude, including in solo?** No. The Prelude bonuses are a replacement for the +1 production of each resource in the standard game; starting TR is unchanged. Solo play with Prelude lowers the limit to 12 generations, but you still start at 14 TR.  
  "Playing solo with Prelude lowers the time limit to 12 generations, but you still start at 14 TR." ([Jacob, 2018-05-21](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1996592/article/29123544#29123544))
- **Should the basic game (no Corporate Era) be combined with Prelude?** Not recommended since Prelude in part simulates the basic game's fast start, but combining is allowed.  
  "Note that the basic game is in part also simulated by the Prelude expansion, so you shouldn't combine those (though you can)." ([Jacob, 2018-06-24](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2014175/article/29379556#29379556)) `intent`
- **Does Prelude with Corporate Era make the game as long as a standard game, and what was Prelude meant to replace?** Prelude was meant as a replacement for the +1 production of each resource in the standard (non-Corporate-Era) game, but it doesn't focus the game on terraforming like removing the Corporate Era cards does, so play time is reduced less (about 15 minutes).  
  "it was meant as a replacement for the +1 production in the standard game, but it still doesn't focus the game on terraforming like removing the CE cards does." ([Jacob, 2018-08-28](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2039696/article/29875831#29875831)) `intent`

### Prelude cards as project cards

- **Are prelude cards project cards, can they get discounts, and do their tags trigger effects?** Preludes work like ordinary projects except for how you choose them and when you play them. They have no cost, so discounts don't help, but their tags can trigger other effects (e.g. a Jovian tag with Saturn Systems). You must be able to perform the effect in order to choose and play the prelude.  
  "Preludes work like ordinary projects except for how you choose them and when you play them. They have no cost, so you can't benefit fromm any discount" ([Jacob, 2018-07-12](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1705209/article/29511131#29511131))
- **Do Prelude cards that add oceans or raise oxygen/temperature increase TR as normal?** Yes, all other rules apply to Prelude cards, so they raise TR as normal.  
  "Yes, all other rules do apply." ([Stronghold, 2018-08-08](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2038699/article/29721161#29721161))
- **Do prelude cards count for the Tycoon milestone (15 blue and green cards)?** No. Even though they work similarly to green cards, preludes are not considered green.  
  "No, it's not counted for Tycoon. Even if they work similarly to green cards, they are not considered to be green." ([Enoch, 2018-08-27](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1705209/article/29864720#29864720))

### Prelude fizzle rule

- **What happens if a prelude card turns out to be unplayable?** You must prove it to your opponents, discard it back to the box, and gain 15 M€.  
  "If you try to play a prelude card and it turns out to be unplayable for any reason, you must prove this to your opponents, discard it back to the box, and gain 15 MC." ([Jacob, 2017-01-10](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1705209/article/24723217#24723217))
- **What happens if a prelude cannot be played (fizzles)?** When playing a prelude, if it fizzles it is discarded back to the box and you gain 15 MC. You must show/prove to the other players that you indeed cannot play it, so this is not optional. The rule will enter future Prelude rulebooks and the FAQ.  
  "When playing a prelude, if it fizzles, it is discarded back to the box instead and you gain 15 MC. You must show/prove to the other players that you indeed cannot play the prelude, so this is not an optional thing." ([Jacob, 2023-01-17](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2993276/article/41533232#41533232))
- **How does the prelude fizzle rule apply to Ecological Experts, Merger, Business Empire and Donation?** Ecological Experts must still be played if you can afford any card in hand; only by showing a hand of unplayable cards can you discard it and take 15 MC. Merger is safer: if you can't play any drawn corporation (shown as proof) you get 15 MC. Business Empire with too little money fizzles for 15 MC. You can intentionally fizzle a first prelude and play Donation second; you still choose which prelude to play first.  
  "Only by showing the other players a hand full of cards that are too expensive or otherwise unplayable, only in that case can you avoid playing Ecological Experts, discard it, and get 15 MC." ([Jacob, 2023-01-17](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2993276/article/41533232#41533232))
- **When proving a prelude can't be played, must you show that another prelude could pay for it first?** No. You still choose when to play a prelude, so you only need to prove it can't be played at that time; other preludes are irrelevant. The rule does not affect your ability to choose preludes or their order.  
  "You still choose when to play a prelude, so you only need to prove it can't be played AT THAT TIME. No need to show the other preludes since they are irrelevant." ([Jacob, 2023-06-14](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2993276/article/42449048#42449048))

### Prelude setup and order

- **What is the Prelude setup order?** 1) Normal setup. 2) Choose starting projects, preludes and corporation, discarding the rest. 3) Play corporations in turn order and pay for the starting hand. 4) Play your 2 preludes in any order, then the next player, and so on. 5) Start the action phase; some corporations have a fixed first action. A prelude that draws cards may be worth playing before Eccentric Sponsor.  
  "Play your 2 preludes in any order, then next player and so on." ([Jacob, 2018-06-30](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1986303/article/29423619#29423619))
- **How are corporations and Prelude cards dealt and chosen, and can the draft be adapted?** As stated in the rules: deal 2 corporations and 4 Prelude cards to each player; players select the 1 corporation and 2 Prelude cards they want. Groups may adapt it to their style.  
  "Deal 2 Corps and 4 Prelude cards to each player. Players select the 1 Corp and 2 Prelude cards they want." ([Stronghold, 2018-07-29](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2033138/article/29640121#29640121))
- **What is the exact order for Prelude setup: choosing, revealing, paying and playing preludes?** Everyone picks corporation, cards and preludes simultaneously; unchosen corporations, preludes and projects are discarded face down (corporations and preludes to the box, projects to the common discard pile). In turn order players reveal their corporation and pay for cards bought, resolving corporation effects that trigger on themselves (e.g. Splice). Then in turn order each player plays both preludes, one at a time, following all normal rules; tags on preludes apply when played and stay in play. Then generation 1 starts with the first player taking the first action.  
  "Player 1 plays both preludes, one at a time. Then next player goes and so on, following all normal rules for playing cards. Any tag on the preludes apply as normal when played, and then stay in play." ([Jacob, 2018-10-05](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2070878/article/30157182#30157182))
- **In what order should corporations and Preludes be revealed at the start?** Reveal all corporations before playing any Preludes.  
  "It is also important to reveal all corporations before playing preludes." ([Jacob, 2018-11-04](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2088822/article/30399783#30399783))
- **When playing Prelude, should Prelude corporations be shuffled in with the others, or should each player get one base/promo and one Prelude corporation?** Nick would say it's up to you: he shuffles all corporations together and deals, and says seeding one Prelude corporation into the deal per player would also be perfectly valid.  
  "I would say it's up to you really. I just shuffle the lot together and deal out. But seeding one prelude corp into the deal per person would also be perfectly valid." ([Nick, 2024-11-20](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3408714/article/45211256#45211256)) `house-rule-ok` `hedged`

### Solo game

- **How many generations does a solo game with Prelude last?** Solo play with Prelude involves fewer generations: 12.  
  "Solo play indeed involves fewer generagions. 12 to be specific." ([Jacob, 2018-03-03](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1944165/article/28442696#28442696))
- **Can Preludes be used in solo play, and does the generation limit change?** Preludes can be used in solo, but then you only play 12 generations, not 14.  
  "Preludes can be used in solo but then you only play 12 generations, not 14." ([Jacob, 2018-04-01](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1968148/article/28702698#28702698))
- **How does Prelude change the solo game, and what is the TR Solo variant?** With Prelude in solo you get 2 Prelude cards but only 12 generations instead of 14. TR Solo variant: the goal becomes reaching a TR of at least 63 in the allotted time, all other solo rules (e.g. Corporate Era) unchanged.  
  "Prelude gives you 2 powerful Prelude cards to jumpstart the game, but the allotted time is now 12 generations instead of 14." ([Jacob, 2018-05-07](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1983367/article/29020516#29020516))
- **What do Prelude and the TR solo variant change in solo play, and can they be combined?** Using Prelude cards lowers the time limit to 12 generations. Using the TR solo version changes the goal to 63 TR. Both modules can be combined with other solo constellations and with each other.  
  "Using prelude cards will lower the time to 12 generations. Using the TR solo version changes the goal to 63 TR." ([Jacob, 2018-07-31](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2034537/article/29658079#29658079))
- **Is solo play without Prelude cards legitimate?** Yes: solo without the Prelude cards is fine, with the standard 14 generations.  
  "Playing solo without the prelude cards is totally legit, just go 14 generations." ([Jacob, 2021-12-05](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2770400/article/38982753#38982753))

### Solo TR63 mode

- **Does TR Solo have a way to raise TR directly?** Yes. TR Solo includes a standard project that gives 1 TR for 16 megacredits.  
  "Maybe I should clarify that the TR solo includes a standard project to get 1 TR for 16 megacredits." ([Jacob, 2018-05-17](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1983367/article/29099943#29099943))
- **In solo with the 63 TR win option, if I already have 63 TR in the final round, do further actions such as placing a greenery still raise oxygen and TR?** Yes. Placing a greenery still raises oxygen (if not maxed) and TR, and its cascading bonuses still apply; the extra TR and board points still count for the score.  
  "An extreme case woul be placing a greenery increasing oxygen, gaining plants from placement, oxygen triggering the temperature bonus, which in turn triggers the bonus ocean, which gives even more plants from placement and triggering Arctic Algae." ([Jacob, 2018-08-14](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2042802/article/29775592#29775592))
- **In the 63 TR solo variant with Prelude, when is the win checked and does end-of-game greenery placement count?** Directly after production is the solar phase, and at the start of it you check whether you have won with 63 TR. In final scoring you may place greeneries as normal; any oxygen increase and TR from those counts only towards your final score, not towards the win condition.  
  "those TR steps are not counted towards winning, only towards your final score." ([Jacob, 2018-09-05](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2054524/article/29929752#29929752))
- **Why does Prelude solo use a TR goal instead of a VP goal?** Intent: the TR goal keeps the tension between terraforming and reaching a good score, opening new strategies and making more cards interesting.  
  "To keep the tension between terraforming and reaching a good score." ([Jacob, 2020-12-06](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2554653/article/36449306#36449306)) `intent`
- **In the final plant conversion of a TR Solo game, do greeneries raise oxygen (and TR)?** Not as the solo rules are written: greeneries placed in the final plant conversion do not give oxygen, because the rules were written for the terraform-all scenario where parameters are maxed; the designers did not want to complicate TR solo.  
  "we let it stand as it was, meaning that greeneries don't give oxygen in the final plant conversion." ([Jacob, 2021-02-28](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2611160/article/37129636#37129636))
- **Is it fine to let the final conversion in TR Solo work normally (oxygen, TR, and heat conversion)?** The designer says an earlier comment saying otherwise was mistaken. He would logically have the final conversion work normally (greeneries giving oxygen and TR when possible, and heat conversion too), but this is his unofficial preference, not the rule as printed.  
  "Yeah, that was a mistaken comment in that I hadn't recently read the rules." ([Jacob, 2021-03-01](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2611160/article/37132579#37132579)) `intent` `house-rule-ok`
- **Can the Prelude solo TR 63 goal be used without the Prelude expansion?** Yes; the TR 63 solo from Prelude can easily be played without Prelude as well.  
  "TR63 solo from Prelude (can easily be played without Prelude as well) makes quite a few cards more relevant (which was the point of designing it)." ([Jacob, 2021-11-16](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2759818/article/38860794#38860794)) `intent`
- **Can the TR63 mode be used without Prelude?** Yes; the TR63 mode can be used whether or not you own Prelude. Its rules are not given in the post.  
  "the TR63 mode (which you can use whether you get Prelude or not)" ([Jacob, 2023-03-27](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3053305/article/41985552#41985552)) `variant`

### Variants and game balance

- **Is there an endorsed beginner analog to the Beginner Corporation for Prelude cards, such as +1 to all productions?** The designer says +1 production on all resources sounds good as a beginner substitute for preludes (slightly more worth than 2 preludes, offsetting less synergy potential). It may be used even with the Generalist milestone, since preludes could let another player claim it on the first turn anyway.  
  "+1 on all sounds good. It's only slightly more worth than 2 preludes, offsetting the fact that you get less potential for synergy." ([Jacob, 2019-02-18](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2153185/article/31282813#31282813)) `variant` `hedged`
- **What official suggestions exist for reducing runaway leaders in 2-player games?** Suggestions: draft; limit game length (use normal WGT terraforming, or start with more preludes, e.g. draw 6 preludes and take 4); or try a merger variant where each draws 4-5 corporations and plays with 2.  
  "So how about drawing 6 preludes and taking 4 of them?" ([Jacob, 2023-02-06](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3022765/article/41669445#41669445)) `variant` `house-rule-ok`

### Wild tag

- **How does the wild tag work: is its type set once or does it change by action?** At any point during an action you may choose one tag type for the wild tag; it keeps that type for the whole action, and afterwards it has no type again. You can do this only once per action. Outside this time the wild tag has no type (counts as no tag). It can be used for milestones, tag requirements and cards that count tags.  
  "At any point during an action, you may choose a tag type (one, not two, and certainly not 3) for the wild tag. The wild tag continues to have that type for the whole duration of that action." ([Jacob, 2018-07-25](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2030851/article/29611733#29611733)) `inferred`
- **Does choosing a type for the wild tag count as playing that tag (e.g. triggering Olympus Conference)?** No. Choosing a tag type for the wild tag does not count as playing that tag, and does not count as getting a new tag into play; it simply 'is' there for the duration of that action.  
  "choosing a tag type for the wild tag does not count as playing that tag (so you can't trigger Olympus Conference for example), and it doesn't count as getting a new tag into play." ([Jacob, 2018-07-25](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2030851/article/29611733#29611733))
- **Does a wild tag count towards Media Archives and the Legend milestone as an event tag?** No. Event tags are only counted while being played, since afterwards they are face down and not active; choosing what the wild tag is does not count as playing the tag. Media Archives and Legend use the event tag icon for simplicity but count event cards, not event tags.  
  "Media Archives and the Legend milestone use the event tag in the iconography, but this is only for simplicity and readability. They count event cards, not event tags" ([Jacob, 2018-08-05](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2036028/article/29699309#29699309))
- **Can Toll Station or Galilean Waystation count other players' wild tags, and when does a wild tag count as a specific tag?** No. The wild tag can only be chosen by the owning player, during the owner's action, if it is already in play. At all other times it counts as no tag (and can thus trigger Community Services). It does not apply to Toll Station/Galilean Waystation for opponents, to end-game scoring (awards, jovian multipliers), or during the action in which the wild-tag card itself is played.  
  "The wild tag can only be chosen - by the owning player - during the owner's action - if it is already in play" ([Jacob, 2019-09-09](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2030851/article/32893227#32893227))
- **Can the owner use their wild tag as a Jovian tag when playing Galilean Waystation, and does playing a wild tag trigger Aridor?** Yes, if the owner plays Galilean Waystation the wild tag can be chosen as a Jovian, as it would benefit Satellites. No, Aridor does not benefit from playing a wild tag.  
  "Aridor does not benefit from playing a wild tag, no." ([Jacob, 2019-09-09](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2030851/article/32894167#32894167))
- **Does a wild tag count for the Diversifier and Ecologist milestones, and does playing a wild-tag card trigger anything?** It counts for milestones, since you claim a milestone in the action phase. Playing the card with the wild tag does not trigger anything, because the wild tag cannot be chosen while it is being played.  
  "It does count, since you claim the milestone in the action phase. Playing the card with the wild tag does not trigger anything though" ([Jacob, 2019-12-06](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2325144/article/33527062#33527062))
- **Does the wild tag (e.g. on Research Network) count as a tag of the player's choice while playing prelude cards during setup?** No. The wild tag only counts during the player's action in the action phase; during the prelude (setup) stage Research Network counts as a prelude card with no tag. It was limited to the action phase deliberately, to avoid problems with awards and Turmoil tag mechanics.  
  "Correct. Our first idea was that it would always count, but we quickly realized that this wouldn't work." ([Jacob, 2020-12-21](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2531750/article/36558438#36558438)) `superseded` by [a later post](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3395635/article/45128719#45128719)
- **Why does the wild tag not count when playing preludes, even though it would rarely matter?** Intent: the wild tag counting only in the action phase is a deliberate simplification. The designer prefers a rule that is straightforward almost always over one with a special case for preludes.  
  "I'd rather have a rule that is straightforward 99.9% of the time and counterintuitive 0.1% of the time than a rule that is intuitive 100% of the time" ([Jacob, 2020-12-21](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2531750/article/36563316#36563316)) `intent` `superseded` by [a later post](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3395635/article/45128719#45128719)
- **Can Research Network's wild tag, used when performing an action, count as an animal or plant tag to trigger Arklight's effect?** No. Using the wild tag is not the same as playing a tag. Research Network's wild tag can increase the tag count, for example for requirements and milestones or to improve cards such as Cartel and Insects, but it cannot trigger effects such as Arklight's or Viral Enhancers'.  
  "Using the wild tag is not the same as playing a tag. It can be used to increase the count of tags for example to fulfill requirements/milestones, or to improve cards such as Cartel and Insects. It can not be used to trigger effects like Arklight, Viral Enhancers etc." ([Jacob, 2021-12-20](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2777510/article/39071544#39071544))
- **If you play a different card, can your wild tag count as a plant tag and trigger Ecological Zone?** You can count your wild tag as any existing tag during an action, but you did not play that tag, so it does not trigger effects such as Ecological Zone. Only the tags on the card played that action are played. The wild tag also cannot be used while it is being played. It is good for requirements, milestones and cards that count tags (like Satellites).  
  "the wild tag can't be used while being played, so it wouldn't trigger even when it was played. The wild tags are good for requirements, milestones, and cards that count certain tags" ([Jacob, 2024-10-10](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2030851/article/44996585#44996585))

## Cards


### Arcadian Communities

- **Must Arcadian Communities' first community (placed in the Prelude phase) be adjacent to the player's tiles?** No. The first community does not have the adjacency restriction.  
  "The first community doesn't have that restriction." ([Jacob, 2018-09-16](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2060467/article/30006976#30006976)) `inferred`

### Eccentric Sponsor

- **Does Eccentric Sponsor's 25 M€ reduction stack with other discounts and corporation effects, and can it play multiple cards?** It lets you play ONE card for 25 cheaper: a card costing 25 or less is free, a more expensive one costs the difference. Corporation effects are active when preludes are played, so discounts such as Teractor's apply (Large Convoy for 8 M€: 36 - 25 - 3). You cannot play multiple cards with it.  
  "It allows you to play ONE card for 25 cheaper. You can play a card for 25 or less for free, or a more expensive one paying the difference." ([Jacob, 2018-06-24](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2014344/article/29379590#29379590))
- **Can you play your two prelude cards in any order, e.g. a production prelude before Eccentric Sponsor to play Strip Mine?** Yes, preludes can be played in any order, even one that draws cards first to look for a better card for Eccentric Sponsor.  
  "You can even play one that draws cards to see if you can find better candidates for your Eccentric Sponsor (the real name of the card)." ([Jacob, 2018-06-27](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2014344/article/29399782#29399782)) `inferred`
- **When is Eccentric Sponsor's card played: later in the game or immediately?** You play the card immediately when playing Eccentric Sponsor, as part of the prelude phase.  
  "You play it immediately when playing Eccentric Sponsor." ([Jacob, 2018-06-30](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1986303/article/29423619#29423619))
- **Must the card played with Eccentric Sponsor still satisfy its production decreases and global requirements?** Correct: the card played via Eccentric Sponsor still has to satisfy the cost of its production box (e.g. Domed Crater's -1 energy production) and its global requirements; only 25 M€ of its cost is reduced.  
  "Correct in both cases." ([Jacob, 2018-08-15](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2043238/article/29777232#29777232)) `inferred`
- **Can Eccentric Sponsor and Ecology Experts both apply to the same project card?** No. They cannot be combined to play the same card; each is played individually.  
  "They can not be combined to play the same card, no. They are played individually." ([Jacob, 2018-08-15](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2043238/article/29779398#29779398))

### Ecology Experts

- **With Ecology Experts, do you still pay for the card played while ignoring its global requirements?** Correct: Ecology Experts only ignores global requirements; the card must still be paid for. Corporations are revealed after prelude cards are chosen, so the player must gamble on the corporation's effects.

- **What does 'VP icon' mean in Vitor's effect and why 'including this'?** 'VP icon' refers to the graphic element on the card, not to its function: this includes cards whose VP is '1 per X resources' (no VP yet when played) and excludes negative VP cards. Vitor's own effect counts itself (cards trigger their own effects), just as only the top-right jovian tag of Saturn Systems counts as an actual tag so it triggers itself once; Celestic's 'floater icon' is a precedent for referring to icons.  
  "The wording 'VP icon' refers to the graphc element on the cards, not to their function, which allows us to unabigously explain how it works." ([Jacob, 2018-03-09](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1954398/article/28498242#28498242))
