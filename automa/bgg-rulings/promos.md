# Promos: official BGG rulings

100 rulings posted on BoardGameGeek by FryxGames, Stronghold Games and the Automa designers. [README.md](README.md) says who is who, how this was built, and what the tags mean. Under each heading the rulings run in date order, so a later one can overrule an earlier one.


## Rules


### First actions

- **If both corporations a player has (via Merger) have a first action, how do they work?** Both are part of your first action, just like playing a card with multiple effects.  
  "If both corporations have a '1st action', then they are both part of your first action, just like playing a card with multiple effects." ([Jacob, 2022-06-03](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2877214/article/40195927#40195927))
- **If Merger gives two corporations with a 'first action', how are they handled?** If you have two 'first actions' they are both done during your first action. If you play Merger during your first action, the new corporation's first action is also performed immediately.  
  "If you now have 2 'first actions', they are both done during your first action. If you play Merger during your first action, any 'first action' on the new corp is also immediately performed" ([Jacob, 2022-06-20](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2886401/article/40299169#40299169))
- **If a first-action effect (Valley Trust) leads to Merger and a new corporation with its own first action (Philares), is that one action or two?** The first action is still just one action; many things can happen in it, just as when playing a card with multiple effects.  
  "The first action is still just one action, but many things can happen in that action, just as when you play a card with multiple effects." ([Jacob, 2022-09-26](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2874012/article/40859020#40859020))
- **What happens when several cards give a 'first action' (e.g. two corporations via Merger, or Head Start)?** Multiple first actions are performed during the same first action. If you get more first actions through Merger, just do them before other actions.  
  "No, multiple first actions are performed during the same first action. And if you get more first actions through Merger, just do them before other actions." ([Jacob, 2023-01-04](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2993276/article/41450757#41450757))
- **How should '1st action' be read when a corporation is played after the first action has already passed (Merger via Board of Directors or a WG Project)?** Interpret '1st action' as 'next available action'. If you have two, Fryxen's ruling is that they stack up and are done together right away; the alternative is to let them occupy the first two actions.  
  "following the intended effect would be to interpret '1st action'¨as 'next available action', and, if you have 2, either have them stack up (my ruling), or let them occupy the first 2 actions." ([Jacob, 2024-09-06](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2886401/article/44823945#44823945))

### Promo cards

- **How many copies of the identical promo card from the pre-launch kit are added to the deck?** Only 1 promo card per game; all cards in the game are unique and the promo cards were meant to raise interest in the game.  
  "Only 1 promo card per game." ([Jonathan, 2016-09-21](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1641824/article/23763645#23763645)) `intent`
- **Can two copies of a promo card be mixed into the deck?** No. Every card is supposed to be unique, so the deck contains only one copy of each card.  
  "Every card is supposed to be unique, so only one copy in the deck." ([Jacob, 2017-11-13](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1882947/article/27422484#27422484))
- **Should you play with both copies of the three Colonies alt-art promo cards?** No. Each card should be unique: do not put both the promo copy and the Colonies copy in the deck.  
  "No, each card should be unique :)" ([Jonathan, 2018-08-31](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2050792/article/29903678#29903678))
- **Are the Colonies alternate-art promo cards identical in stats to the final Colonies cards?** No. They were created before final adjustments (one card lost its space tag and its cost went from 21 to 20), so they aren't really official, but you can play with them.  
  "Yeah, so the alternate art promos aren't even really official. They were created before final adjustments. But you can play with them, it's no big deal." ([Jacob, 2024-04-06](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3273626/article/44077895#44077895)) `erratum`

### Starting corporation tags

- **Do tags on a revealed corporation trigger other corporations' effects (e.g. Splice with Recyclon's microbe tag)?** Yes, corporation tags trigger normally like any card, also for Saturn Systems and Stormcraft, so strict player order matters when revealing. The same applies to Prelude cards and the first turn (e.g. Immigrant City before Tharsis Republic places its first city). All starting cards are chosen before any are revealed.  
  "Yes, it triggers normally, just like all cards. This is also true with Saturn Systems and Stormcraft. The same can be said about Prelude cards and first turn of the game. Player order matters." ([Jacob, 2018-11-04](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2088822/article/30399740#30399740))

## Cards


### Arcadian Communities

- **What happens with Arcadian Communities when the board is otherwise full?** If the board is otherwise full, only Arcadian can place tiles, since it can reserve areas in advance with its communities.  
  "If the board is otherwise full, only Arcadian can place tiles." ([Jacob, 2018-01-31](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1930389/article/28121311#28121311))
- **Can Arcadian Communities use its action in the first generation?** Yes. It can use its ability in the first generation, giving 2 communities in the first generation (including the one from its first action).  
  "Arcadian Communities can use its ability the first generation, yes, resulting in 2 communities the first generation (including the 1st action one)." ([Jacob, 2018-02-01](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1930389/article/28132490#28132490))
- **Does Arcadian Communities get 3 MC for placing a tile on an area marked by the Land Claim event?** Yes. For Arcadian Communities a Land Claim marker works like any other area marked with their player marker: they can spread from it and gain the 3 MC when placing a tile on it.  
  "For Arcadian Communities, Land Claim works just like any other area marked with their player marker. They can use the Land Claim marker to spread from, and they get 3 MC refund for placing tiles on it." ([Enoch, 2019-02-04](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2144398/article/31163534#31163534))
- **After a tile is placed on an area reserved by Arcadian Communities, does the reservation remain (e.g. 3 MC again when Kaguya Tech places a city there)?** Intent: you replace your reservation with the tile, and from then on it behaves like a normal tile/area again, so the reservation does not persist.  
  "The intent of Arcadian Communities is that you replace your reservation with the tile. So from that point, it behaves like a normal tile/area again." ([Jacob, 2023-10-29](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3180398/article/43196342#43196342)) `intent`

### Asteroid Hollowing

- **If Directed Impactors places an asteroid on Asteroid Hollowing, do you also get Asteroid Hollowing's increase of MC by 1?** No. Placing an asteroid on Asteroid Hollowing via Directed Impactors only puts an asteroid resource there (worth 1/2 VP); it does not also give the MC increase of 1 that the question asked about.  
  "No. It just puts an asteroid there (worth 1/2 VP)" ([Jacob, 2022-10-11](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2449791/article/40956119#40956119))

### Bactoviral Research

- **Can Bactoviral Research be played if you have no card that can hold microbes?** Yes. Not being able to collect resources you gain is one of the 3 exceptions to the rule that all effects must be carried out.  
  "Yes, it can. Not being able to collect resources you gain is one of the 3 exceptions to the rule that all effects must be carried out." ([Jacob, 2021-10-10](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2738624/article/38619096#38619096))

### Crash Site Cleanup

- **Does Crash Site Cleanup require that some player actually removed another player's plants this generation?** Fryxen confirms ('correct') the reading that the requirement is that any player has removed another player's plants this generation, and that at least one plant must actually have been removed (merely being able to remove some does not count).  
  "correct." ([Jacob, 2021-01-08](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2452083/article/36693419#36693419))
- **In solo, does removing plants from the invisible opponent (e.g. with Virus) enable Crash Site Cleanup?** Yes ('correct'): trashing plants from the invisible opponent counts as something actually happening, as with Mons Insurance in solo, so playing a card like Virus enables Crash Site Cleanup.  
  "correct." ([Jacob, 2021-01-08](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2452083/article/36693419#36693419))

### Diversity Support

- **Which resources count towards the 9 different resource types required by Diversity Support, and do standard resources count?** It requires 9 different types of resources, which can be MC, plants, heat, microbes, floaters etc.: the standard resources count as well as card resources.  
  "Yes, it requires 9 different types of resources, which can be MC, plants, heat, microbes, floaters etc." ([Jacob, 2020-11-21](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2456543/article/36334042#36334042))
- **Do resources placed by Self-Replicating Robots count as a type for Diversity Support or for the Eccentric milestone?** No. The resources placed by Self-Replicating Robots have no type, so they are not applicable to playing Diversity Support or counting for Eccentric. They are also on a card that is not in play, which disqualifies them for Eccentric as well.  
  "The resources placed by Self-replicating Robots have no type, and are thus not applicable to playing Diversity Support or counting for Excentric." ([Jacob, 2020-12-11](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2456543/article/36487476#36487476))

### Double Down

- **Does Double Down copy a prelude's tags, so that copying an Earth-tag prelude triggers Point Luna?** No. Double Down copies just the direct effects of the prelude card, not its tags, so it does not trigger Point Luna.  
  "No, just the direct effects of the prelude card, so no tag and therefore no trigger of Point Luna." ([Jacob, 2023-01-12](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2993276/article/41502250#41502250))

### Harvest

- **Do the 2021 Season Promo printings of Harvest ('Gasin 12 M€') and Bio Printing Facility ('Speciese') really contain typos?** Yes, the latest printing of the 2021 Season Promo Cards has these typos: Harvest should read 'Gain 12 M€' and Bio Printing Facility's flavour text 'Species'. They are only typos, not game-changing text, and the cards will not be reprinted.  
  "I can confirm that the latest printing of 2021 Season Promo Cards do have these typos, unfortunately." ([Jonathan, 2022-03-10](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2827880/article/39645531#39645531)) `erratum`

### Head Start

- **Is the first action of Head Start the first action of the game, and what does 'immediately' mean?** The first action of Head Start is indeed the first action, triggering Inventrix and the like. 'Immediately' stresses that it happens before the action phase. You still get the 2 actions even if you have a corporation-defined first action; that first action is just defined.  
  "The first action of Head Start is indeed the first action, triggering Inventrix and the like. We wrote 'immediately' to stress that this was before the action phase." ([Jacob, 2023-01-04](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2993276/article/41447529#41447529))
- **Can you combo Tharsis Republic's first-action city with Rover Construction using Head Start's actions?** No: you can't combo Tharsis with Rover Construction before the first-action city. You could do it with Excentric Sponsor or Ecological Experts, since those don't count as regular actions.  
  "This does however mean that you can't combo Tharsis with Rover Construction before the first action city. But you could do it with Excentric Sponsor or Ecological Experts, since these don't count as regular actions." ([Jacob, 2023-01-04](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2993276/article/41447529#41447529))
- **Can the effects and the two actions of Head Start be resolved in any order, and can they be separated or skipped?** Under the 'bag of effects' principle, playing Head Start leaves 4 separate effects (2 MC per hand card, gain 2 steel, take 1 action, take 1 action) to resolve in any order, including triggered effects. The two actions can be separated, but cannot be interrupted by other effects. He also said the actions may be skipped, which he retracted on 2023-01-20.  
  "you can skip these actions if you like, and also that you can't interrupt these actions with other effects since they are themselves each a separate effect." ([Jacob, 2023-01-11](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2993276/article/41494904#41494904)) `reversed` `superseded` by [a later post](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2993276/article/41555074#41555074)
- **Are the actions or card plays of Head Start, Excentric Sponsor and Ecological Experts optional?** No. None of these preludes say you can skip their action/card play, so they are all mandatory. Worst case for Head Start is having only the sell patents action available.  
  "None of the Preludes Head Start, Excentric Sponsor and Ecological Experts say that you can skip their action/card play, so they are all mandatory." ([Jacob, 2023-01-19](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2993276/article/41548927#41548927))
- **Can you skip Head Start's two actions if your only possible action is selling patents?** No, you cannot skip the actions since the card doesn't say so; the earlier statement that they could be skipped was wrong.  
  "No, you can not skip the actions since the card doesn't say so. The cited comment was wrong in this regard." ([Jacob, 2023-01-20](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2993276/article/41555074#41555074))
- **With Head Start, must you perform a corporation-specific first action (Tharsis, VITOR, Poseidon, MSI, etc.)?** Yes, you must perform it if your corporation has one. The idea is that you start 2 actions earlier, whether or not you have a fixed first action.  
  "Yes, you must if you have one. The idea is that you start 2 actions earlier, whether you have a fixed first action or not." ([Jacob, 2023-03-29](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3055761/article/41996773#41996773))

### Law Suit

- **Is the Lawsuit promo playable in solo, given the neutral player cannot attack you?** Not playable.  
  "Not playable." ([Jacob, 2019-05-23](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2191701/article/32049252#32049252))
- **If Lawsuit is drawn in solo, may you remove or redraw it?** No, there is no rule allowing that, and no rule to sort cards out of the deck for solo play. Fryxen adds that promo cards are optional material, so a group can pretend it is not there or not play with it.  
  "No, there is no rule allowing that." ([Jacob, 2019-05-23](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2191701/article/32049610#32049610)) `house-rule-ok`
- **If a player plays Mons Insurance, can another player play Law Suit against that player because of the production decrease?** No. Law Suit requires that an opponent decreased your production, but Mons Insurance has an asterisk and bold text saying you decrease your own production yourself, so no opponent decreased it.  
  "Law Suit is not applicable anyway, since it requires that an opponent decreased your production, but Mons Insurance specifies with an asterisk and bold text that you do it yourself." ([Jacob, 2021-07-22](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2695672/article/38137047#38137047))

### Merger

- **When Merger says 'Then pay 42 MC', can the new corporation's starting steel or titanium be used toward the payment as if playing a card?** No. Merger says pay 42 MC, so it must be paid in MC only; steel and titanium cannot be used. Paying first and then getting the effect matches the normal order of playing cards and actions.  
  "No, it is not a correct interpretation since it doesn't say so. It says pay 42 MC. Period." ([Jacob, 2022-06-05](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2874012/article/40205976#40205976))
- **What happens if you cannot pay the 42 MC for Merger?** Formally it is the same as any prelude you can't afford (e.g. Business Empire after spending all your money on cards): the card can't be played and is taken back, and since there is no other chance to play preludes, its benefit is lost.  
  "Formally, this is the same as when you play another prelude that you can't afford, for example Business Empire when you've spent all your money on cards: The card can't be played and is taken back." ([Jacob, 2022-06-06](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2879043/article/40213123#40213123))
- **If no drawn corporation lets you afford Merger's 42 MC, is there an official fix?** No general or house rule is given. Fryxen would improvise a temporary exception in the rare case, for example drawing corporations until you can play one, and would allow retroactively buying fewer cards if Business Empire is unaffordable.  
  "Rather than having a general rule or even house rule for this, I'd make up some temporary exception in the rare case this should happen." ([Jacob, 2022-06-06](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2879043/article/40213123#40213123)) `variant` `house-rule-ok`
- **What happens if after drawing corporations with Merger the player cannot pay the 42 MC?** Strictly speaking Merger fails and you get nothing. As a house rule Fryxen would personally continue to draw corporations until one can be played, and advises saving a little MC (about 7) so the risk is practically zero.  
  "If it should happen, strictly speaking, the Merger fails and you get nothing. If it happened in my group, I think I would personally house-rule that you continue to draw corps until you can play one." ([Jacob, 2022-06-08](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2880044/article/40225996#40225996)) `hedged` `house-rule-ok`
- **How likely is it that Merger fails for lack of MC, and what would Fryxen do in a casual game when it does?** Fryxen calculates the failure risk as very low when saving some MC (saving 7 MC gives 0 percent risk in base plus Prelude and with all corporations) and worst when the other prelude is Business Empire played after Merger. In casual play he would simply allow the player to draw more corporations until they get one they can play.  
  "If it happened in my group during casual play, I'd simply allow them to draw more corps until they get one they can play." ([Jacob, 2022-06-08](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2880044/article/40229925#40229925)) `house-rule-ok`
- **Do you really get the effects of both corporations when using Merger (e.g. Ecoline and Tharsis Republic)?** Yes, you get all the effects and resources from both corporations.  
  "Yes, you get all the effects and resources from both corporations." ([Jacob, 2022-06-20](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2886401/article/40299169#40299169))
- **Are there limitations on the timing of buying cards and paying for Merger?** Two limitations: you must buy your project cards with the cash of the first corporation before seeing and choosing your second one, and you must be able to pay the 42 MC after playing the second corporation.  
  "You must buy your project cards from the cash of the first corp, before seeing & choosing your second one. And you must be able to pay the 42 MC after playing the second corp." ([Jacob, 2022-06-20](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2886401/article/40299169#40299169))

### Mons Insurance

- **When another player plays Mons Insurance, could a player with an effect letting them decrease a different production instead (hypothetical 'weird Helion') choose that alternative?** Yes: a hypothetical corporation that lets you decrease heat production instead of M€ production would let its owner choose that when Mons Insurance is played, because each affected player performs the decrease themselves.  
  "1) yes, a 'weird Helion' would work." ([Jacob, 2021-07-23](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2695672/article/38140808#38140808)) `intent` `inferred`
- **Can Helion pay Mons Insurance payouts with heat, and what does the receiver get?** Helion may be used to pay Mons Insurance payouts, but the receiver gets MC: you pay an amount and the other player receives the same amount, and Helion only affects what you pay. The receiver cannot force you to use heat even if you lack MC, because using heat as MC is your optional ability.  
  "Helion may be used to pay for Mons Insurance payouts, but the receiver gets MC [...]. The receiver can not force you to use heat, even if you don't have enough money, since it is your optional ability, not theirs." ([Jacob, 2022-06-03](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2877214/article/40195927#40195927))
- **With Mons Insurance, Stormcraft Incorporated and Helion together, can floaters be used to pay Mons Insurance payouts?** Yes, you can use floaters for the Mons payment and the receiving player gets the corresponding MC. The active player still determines when you do the payout. (This point 6 was edited after the first version.)  
  "In a hypothetical scenario of combining Mons + Stormcraft + Helion [...] you can use floaters for Mons payment, the player receiving the corresponding MC. The active player still determines when you do the payout however." ([Jacob, 2022-06-03](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2877214/article/40195927#40195927))
- **When a Mons Insurance payout is paid with Helion heat or Stormcraft floaters, does the receiver get heat or a floater instead of MC?** Fryxen admits he got it wrong in the first version of point 6 (which had the receiver getting a floater): the receiver gets MC, and the post was corrected.  
  "Yeah, sorry, got that wrong. It is corrected now. Tricky, these cases..." ([Jacob, 2023-01-17](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2877214/article/41533263#41533263)) `inferred`
- **Mons Insurance plays Flooding with its last money: in what order do the ocean, attack and insurance payout resolve?** The ocean must be placed first (to determine the victim); its TR, adjacency and placement bonuses go into the pool. The player may resolve the attack before those; it triggers the Mons Insurance payout, which is added to the pool and may be resolved even if the player cannot afford it (allowed by that effect). Only then the ocean bonuses are resolved.  
  "The player resolves the insurance payout but has no money for it (which is ok according to that effect). Only then does the player continue with the ocean bonuses, gaining TR, MC and whatnot." ([Jacob, 2023-08-29](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3144214/article/42871880#42871880))

### Pharmacy Union

- **When does Pharmacy Union's draw of a science-tag card happen, and how does it work?** It works like previous examples (reveal cards until a science tag card is found, take it, discard the rest) but is done when the corporation is revealed, not on the first turn, just like the timing of similar preludes.  
  "Yes, it works the same as previous examples, but is done when it is revealed instead of the first turn (just like the timing of similar preludes)." ([Jacob, 2020-07-24](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2470876/article/35415932#35415932))
- **If you play a card with both a microbe tag and a science tag while Pharmacy Union has no disease, and you resolve the science tag first to flip the corporation, what happens to the microbe tag?** Both tags trigger at the same time and may be resolved in the order of your choice, but all must be resolved. If you resolve science first and flip Pharmacy Union, the microbe trigger still resolves: the disease is not added (a resource you cannot collect is ignored, one of the three exceptions) but you still lose 4 MC (or as much as you can).  
  "you can ignore effects collecting resources you can't collect (one of the famous 3 exceptions), so the microbe trigger is resolved by you losing 4 MC and NOT getting a dicease resource." ([Jacob, 2020-09-13](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2451064/article/35805086#35805086))
- **Does flipping Pharmacy Union face down remove effects of tags that already triggered but are not yet resolved?** No. Both tags trigger together and no tag takes precedence. Flipping the corporation prevents further triggerings but does not remove effects that have already been triggered and are waiting to be resolved.  
  "The corp flipping is preventing further triggerings, but does not remove effects already triggered." ([Jacob, 2020-09-15](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2451064/article/35821666#35821666))
- **When a card triggers two Pharmacy Union effects, is the triggered effect's text or its outcome fixed at trigger time?** The triggering text itself is stored until resolved, not its result or choice. Resolving the microbe tag first adds a disease and costs 4 MC, after which the science tag must remove that disease for 1 TR (the flip option is no longer available). Resolving science first with no disease flips the corporation, and the waiting microbe trigger still resolves: the disease is skipped and you lose 4 MC or as much as possible. Likewise a pending 3 TR would not fizzle because the corporation flipped.  
  "It is not the result or choice of the trigger that is stored, but the triggered text itself." ([Jacob, 2020-09-16](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2451064/article/35824269#35824269))
- **If you play Research (two science tags) while Pharmacy Union has no diseases, is the play legal?** Yes. Both science tags trigger and the first resolves normally; because Pharmacy Union's flip effect says 'may', the effect is allowed to be ignored, so the play is legal. (Fryxen edited this post after realising an earlier version of the card without 'may' was being discussed.)  
  "Because it says 'may' on the effect, this effect is allowed to be ignored, so the play is legal." ([Jacob, 2020-09-19](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2451064/article/35851657#35851657))
- **Does the iconography on Pharmacy Union's starting draw imply it happens as a first action like other corporations' draws?** No. The timing is not part of the iconography. The tag shown on the icon acts as a kind of asterisk and is explained in the text; corporations and preludes have used the same iconography with different timing before, and the procedure to get the card is always the same.  
  "the timing is not part of the iconography" ([Jacob, 2020-09-20](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2470876/article/35857049#35857049)) `intent`
- **Does removing Pharmacy Union from play (flipping it) let you fizzle a microbe trigger that is waiting to be resolved?** No. In the case of a science tag plus a microbe tag the earlier ruling stands: removing Pharmacy Union from play changes the game state, but does not allow you to fizzle effects that are waiting to be resolved.  
  "Removing PU from play does change the game state, but it does not allow you to fizzle effects that are waiting to be resolved." ([Jacob, 2020-09-22](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2451064/article/35867765#35867765))
- **In its final version, what happens to Pharmacy Union when it is flipped, and does it count as playing an event?** The final version of Pharmacy Union is put in the event pile when flipped, so it counts as a played event for Media Archive and the event milestone. It does not trigger Media Group or Interplanetary Cinematics because it does not count as PLAYING an event tag.  
  "the final version of PU actually puts PU in the event pile (to count as a played event). This is useful for Media Archive and taking the event milestone." ([Jacob, 2020-09-22](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2451064/article/35867765#35867765))
- **With one disease on Pharmacy Union, you play Research. How do the two science triggers resolve, and what does 'may' cover?** Resolving the first trigger removes the disease and gains 1 TR; resolving the second, with no disease left, you may place Pharmacy Union in the event pile and gain 3 TR. The 'may' covers the whole 'remove + 3 TR' option, so you may not gain 3 TR without removing Pharmacy Union, nor remove it without gaining 3 TR.  
  "The 'may' refers to the whole 'remove + 3 TR', so you may not gain 3 TR without removing PU, and you may not remove PU without gaining 3 TR." ([Jacob, 2020-09-22](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2451064/article/35867765#35867765))

### Self-replicating Robots

SRR resources are not real resources and the corresponding card is not in play, so they generally do not interact with other parts of the game.
