# Turmoil: official BGG rulings

62 rulings posted on BoardGameGeek by FryxGames, Stronghold Games and the Automa designers. [README.md](README.md) says who is who, how this was built, and what the tags mean. Under each heading the rulings run in date order, so a later one can overrule an earlier one.

Pristar: [Jonathan, 2021-06-05](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2669157/article/37851931#37851931), Pristar: [Jacob, 2025-12-14](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3627644/article/47022795#47022795)

## Rules


### Chairman and dominant party

- **When a party becomes ruling its delegates are removed and dominance shifts; so is the chairman usually left with no delegates in that party?** Correct. The ruling party will shift most of the time. The chairman is worth 1 TR plus 1 influence, so pursuing it can be worthwhile, and ruling parties can be anticipated.  
  "Correct. But the chairman is worth 1 TR plus 1 influence, so it can definitely be worth pursuing." ([Jacob, 2019-04-24](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2191608/article/31818639#31818639))
- **Does the Chairman still count as a delegate of a party for determining the dominant party?** No. The Chairman is not in a party anymore, so it doesn't count towards dominance.  
  "The chairman is not in a party anymore, so it doesn't count towards dominance." ([Jacob, 2019-12-15](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2331021/article/33590510#33590510))
- **Are ruling and dominant party mutually exclusive?** No. The dominant party is marked by the dominance marker and the ruling party by its policy tile being displayed. A party can be both, although it is unlikely, since the dominant party is emptied when it becomes ruling and would have to gain enough new delegates to surpass all other parties.  
  "A party CAN be both ruling and dominant at the same time, although it is unlikely" ([Jacob, 2020-01-12](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2347545/article/33772033#33772033))

### Delegates and influence

- **Can a player reach 4 influence with a card that gives +1 influence?** Yes: with the card, plus being chairman, dominant party leader and another delegate in the dominant party, a player can reach 4 influence.  
  "you can indeed reach an influence of 4 if you also have the chairman, the dominant party leader and another delegate in the dominant party." ([Jacob, 2019-08-03](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2249468/article/32615582#32615582))
- **Is each party limited to 7 delegates?** No. Each player is limited to the 7 delegate pieces in the game, but each party has unlimited space. The 6 seats behind the party leader seat are decorative; delegates can be placed anywhere in the party's area.  
  "Each player is limited to the 7 delegate pieces given in the game, but each party has unlimited space." ([Jacob, 2019-12-24](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2335426/article/33645519#33645519))
- **How do neutral delegates accumulate in Turmoil, and how often do you need an extra lobbyist?** There are 2 new neutral delegates every generation and they stack up over time; to become chairman you often need to place an extra delegate.  
  "In order to be chairman, you often need to place an extra delegate. There are 2 new neutral delegates every generation, and over time, it stacks up." ([Jacob, 2020-02-23](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2360747/article/34117634#34117634))
- **Does a delegate in the lobby count as being in the reserve for cards that place delegates from the reserve?** Yes, the lobby is considered part of the reserve for supply purposes. The different wording between cards amounts to the same. You normally never take delegates from parties.  
  "Yes, the lobby is considered part of the reserve for supply purposes." ([Jacob, 2021-01-12](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2570071/article/36719863#36719863))

### Diversity (global event)

- **In the Diversity global event (10 MC with 9 or more different tags), how does Influence count?** Each Influence counts as a different, unique, imaginary tag: 2 Influence equals 2 different tags. So 7 different tags plus 2 Influence qualifies. Event tags are not face up so they don't count.  
  "2 influence = 2 different, unique and imaginary tags." ([Jacob, 2020-01-09](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2345232/article/33743859#33743859))

### Election (global event)

- **If two players tie for first in the Election global event, does the third player get second place rewards?** No. Global events like this work like awards: if 2+ players already won, there is no 2nd place.  
  "They work like awards. If 2+ players already won, there is no 2nd place." ([Jacob, 2021-03-22](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2625639/article/37308482#37308482))

### Global events

- **Where does an ocean tile removed by a Turmoil event go?** It goes back to the pile and has to be placed again.  
  "It goes back to the pile and has to be placed again." ([Jacob, 2019-04-16](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2186983/article/31756714#31756714))
- **Do global event effects on parameters apply once or per player, and do effects on players apply to all?** Parameter changes are one-off effects as written on the card (not per player). The parts that affect players (resources, production, TR) affect all players, with the amount depending on game state and influence.  
  "Those changes are of course one-off effects, just as described on the cards. The part affecting the players (easily identified because they affect player-related stuff like resources, production and TR) are affecting all players" ([Jacob, 2019-10-31](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2300308/article/33273164#33273164))
- **In a global event, can players choose the order of effects, e.g. Helion paying for building tags with heat before Great Dust Storm removes all heat?** No. The choose-the-order rule applies on your turn, and a global event is nobody's turn. The ruling is that things are done in the written order, with triggered effects done immediately and the first player deciding ties. So players lose all heat before paying for their buildings.  
  "My current ruling is that things are done in the written order, with any triggered effects done immediately, the first player deciding any ties." ([Jacob, 2019-12-15](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2330604/article/33588052#33588052))
- **Does the player who removes an ocean tile lose TR?** No. They do nothing except decide which ocean is removed.  
  "No, they don't actually do anything, they just decide what ocean is removed." ([Jacob, 2019-12-16](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2186983/article/33591712#33591712))
- **For the global events described (resources on every card that uses resources; ranking Earth tags; ranking Building tags plus cities), do corporations with resources get one, and are ties handled so that multiple first ranks leave no second rank?** Correct on all three: resources are added even to corporations that collect resources, and when there are several first ranks they all get the first-rank penalty/reward and there is no second rank (ties are friendly).  
  "That is all correct." ([Jacob, 2019-12-21](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2334270/article/33633309#33633309)) `inferred`
- **If a global parameter is maxed, are the corresponding steps in global events like Dry Deserts and Snow Cover ignored?** Correct: if the parameter is at its maximum it may not be affected again, so the first part of Dry Deserts (remove an ocean) and Snow Cover (reduce temperature) would be ignored.  
  "Correct on all points." ([Jacob, 2019-12-21](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2334454/article/33634574#33634574)) `inferred`
- **When a global event gives a standard resource per Influence, can the resources be different?** Yes, you can choose different resources.  
  "3) You can choose different resources." ([Jacob, 2020-01-09](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2345608/article/33749503#33749503))

### Order of effects

- **In what order may triggered effects be performed?** Triggers can be performed in any chosen order but do not exist until the thing that triggers them has occurred.  
  "The triggers can be performed in any chosen order, but of course don't exist until the thing that trigger them has occurred." ([Jacob, 2019-10-31](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2298086/article/33274228#33274228))

### Reds

- **How does the Reds policy apply to actions that raise TR?** Reds require paying 3 M€ when you get TR, so you can't take any action that increases your TR unless you pay the Reds 3 M€ during that action.  
  "This means that you can't take any action that would increase your TR unless you pay the Reds 3 MC during that action." ([Jacob, 2017-01-10](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1705209/article/24723217#24723217))
- **If the Reds are ruling and you cannot pay 3 MC, can you still perform an action that raises TR?** No. The Reds' tax must be paid to raise TR; effects chain (parameter increase adds TR, TR adds the -3 MC), losing resources is not ignorable, and if you cannot pay you cannot perform the action at all. You may gain the MC during the action (e.g. via an ocean adjacency bonus) to pay.  
  "When TR is taken, Reds adds a -3 MC to the list of effects. Losing resources is not something that can be ignored, so this effect must also be performed. If you can't do it, then you can't perform the action at all." ([Jacob, 2019-05-02](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2196388/article/31885882#31885882))
- **If the Reds rule and you lack 3 MC, can you play the card/action that raises a global parameter and just not raise TR?** No. All actions follow the same principles as playing a card: pay cost, meet requirements, and be able to perform all effects and triggers (excluding maxed parameters, removing opposing resources, and taking card resources you can't collect). You cannot do the action that would raise your TR if you can't pay the Reds.  
  "For this particular case, it is a matter of how to play an action, and it follows the same rules as playing a card, so you can't do the action that would raise your TR at all if you can't pay the Reds." ([Jacob, 2019-10-31](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2298086/article/33274228#33274228))
- **With Reds ruling and not enough MC, can you play Big Asteroid (raising temperature two steps) using MC from the ocean bonus to pay the Reds TR tax?** Yes. You choose the order of effects, and as long as you can find a way to perform all effects you can play the card. Each temperature step, TR step and MC cost is a separate effect; the Reds payment is not in the pile of waiting effects until you take the TR.  
  "Summary: You choose the order of effects, and as long as you can find a way to perform all effects, you can play the card/action." ([Jacob, 2020-01-20](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2352956/article/33842537#33842537))
- **With Reds ruling and Venus maxed, can the player choose to raise Venus with Atmoscoop to avoid paying the Reds tax?** Yes, you can choose Venus; it is your only option if you can't pay the Reds for the temperature TR. Players may choose any legal option, and both are legal if you can pay the Reds.  
  "Players may choose any legal option, and both are legal if you can pay the Reds." ([Jacob, 2020-12-17](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2561134/article/36531759#36531759))
- **Under Reds ruling, can you take an action that raises TR if you cannot pay for it?** No. Reds require you to pay for your TR, and gaining TR is not one of the exceptions to performing all effects, so you cannot take actions that would raise your TR if you cannot pay the Reds. This can also block greenery placement when the greenery would raise the oxygen level.  
  "you can't take actions that would raise your TR if you can't pay the Reds." ([Jacob, 2021-03-23](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2625008/article/37317089#37317089))

### Ruling policies

- **Is the Ruling Bonus, or becoming Chairman, affected by the Ruling Policy (e.g. Reds)?** No. Ruling policies are only active during the action phase, so the Reds do not punish TR gained from the party's ruling bonus or from the new chairman.  
  "The ruling policies are only active during the action phase, so Reds will not punish the one getting its policy TR or the new chairman." ([Jacob, 2019-04-16](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2187250/article/31760384#31760384))
- **Do the Greens and Mars First policies trigger during end game scoring?** No; neither Greens nor Mars First trigger during end game scoring.  
  "Likewise, neither Greens nor Mars First will trigger during end game scoring." ([Jacob, 2019-04-16](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2187250/article/31760384#31760384))
- **Could the game start with the Scientists as ruling party instead of the Greens?** Yes, you could indeed start with Scientists if you like, but they prefer Greens and the rules are shorter without a choice. Greens were chosen for thematic reasons and because several parties (Kelvinists, Mars First, Reds, Unity) would unbalance the game start.  
  "So to conclude, you could indeed start with Scientists if you like, but we prefer Greens, and the rules are shorter if you don't have a choice. Do as you wish." ([Jacob, 2022-06-06](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2878615/article/40211398#40211398)) `variant` `house-rule-ok`

### Solar phase

- **If a global event (e.g. Volcanic Eruptions) completes the last global parameter during the Turmoil phase, does the game end that generation?** No. In the solar phase the game end check occurs before the global event triggers, so you play another generation until the next end game check.  
  "Correct. You play another generation until you make the next end game check." ([Jacob, 2019-12-17](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2331994/article/33601218#33601218))
- **What is the order of the production phase, research and solar phase in a game with expansions?** Researching new cards happens at the start of the next generation; after production comes the solar phase. The solar phase begins with the end-game check and then administers the expansions in release order: Venus Next (World Government Terraforming), Colonies (colony production and reset of trade fleets), Turmoil (several steps).  
  "Researching new cards is at the start of the next generation, after production comes the solar phase. It begins with the end game check and goes on to administer the expansions in release order" ([Jacob, 2020-02-23](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2360747/article/34117634#34117634))

### Solo game

- **How does solo play work with Turmoil?** Use the normal Turmoil rules in solo: goal is complete terraforming, or 63 TR (which adds a Buffer Gas standard project). The player loses 1 TR every generation, neutral delegates make controlling the Terraforming Committee harder, and combining with other expansions just adds their rules and solo rules.  
  "Goal: complete terraforming or 63 TR (which adds a Buffer Gas standard project). Use the normal Turmoil rules in solo." ([Jacob, 2019-05-28](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2207170/article/32089357#32089357))
- **In solo with Turmoil, must you play the TFALL (end of generation 14) objective?** No. You can also play the TR63 objective with Turmoil in solo; it is more difficult but far from impossible, and being chairman and getting the Reds in power early helps.  
  "You can play TR63 as well, and it is more difficult, but far from impossible." ([Jacob, 2020-02-23](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2360747/article/34117634#34117634)) `inferred`
- **Does the neutral player compete in global events like Revolution?** Yes, the neutral player competes (e.g. counting influence) and will take the penalty.  
  "The neutral player competes, and will take a 'hit' in this case." ([Jacob, 2021-05-24](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2664688/article/37773806#37773806))

### Sponsored Projects (global event)

- **Does the Sponsored Projects global event add a resource to Self-Replicating Robots?** Yes, confirmed with 'Correct on all points'.  
  "Correct on all points." ([Jacob, 2019-12-21](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2334454/article/33634574#33634574)) `inferred`

### TR revision

- **Can the Turmoil TR revision (lose 1 TR per generation) be dropped?** The designers tried without it and the engines went bananas with runaway leaders winning with 50+ points; players may try without it but should not expect a balanced game.  
  "Try without it if you like, but don't expect a balanced game." ([Jacob, 2019-12-03](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2180216/article/33506595#33506595)) `intent` `house-rule-ok`
- **What is the TR revision for in Turmoil?** Design intent: it is not aimed at the leader specifically but slows the overall exponential escalation caused by party bonuses, policies, chairman TR, influence and global events, giving players more time to win by other means. Reds act as the specific catch-up mechanic.  
  "So the TR revision doesn't specifically hit the leader, like you said, but it slows down the overall escalation, giving players more time to win by other means." ([Jacob, 2019-12-16](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2180216/article/33595008#33595008)) `intent`

## Cards


### Banned Delegate

- **Can Banned Delegate target your own Chairman, e.g. to avoid a Revolution penalty?** Follow the theme when in doubt: the card represents the Chairman kicking out an opponent, not a weird way of resigning, and resigning is for those who have already lost real power. By implication it should not be used to escape a Revolution.  
  "The card is obviously meant to represent the chairman (your guy) kicking out an opponent, not as a very weird way of resigning." ([Jacob, 2020-02-19](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2372250/article/34081166#34081166)) `intent` `hedged` `inferred`

### Factorum

- **Does Factorum's second step (spend 3 MC to draw a building card) involve revealing cards one by one until a building tag is revealed and kept?** Correct: it involves revealing cards until a building tag is revealed and kept, so all players know which building card was drawn.  
  "Correct on all points." ([Jacob, 2019-12-21](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2334454/article/33634574#33634574)) `inferred`
- **Why doesn't Factorum say 'reveal' like other reveal-until effects?** Design intent: Factorum's ability uses the same reveal-until mechanic, but the card avoids the long explanation, assuming players are familiar with the mechanic.  
  "Because it requires such a long explanation, and we figured players picking this up would already be familiar with the mechanic." ([Jacob, 2020-01-03](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2334454/article/33701556#33701556)) `intent`

### Freyja Biodomes

- **With only Stratospheric Birds in play, can you choose to receive microbes instead of animals with Freyja Biodomes, even though they can't be placed?** Yes, you can choose that (players may choose any legal option).  
  "Yes, you can choose that." ([Jacob, 2020-12-17](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2561134/article/36532024#36532024)) `inferred`

### Generous Funds

- **Why is the Influence icon on Generous Funds shown with a plus, and how is it counted?** Influence does not change the count but adds to it. With 28 TR and 2 Influence you have 2 sets of 5 TR over 15, not 3, then Influence is added for 4 payouts total; each Influence gives one payout (equal to a set of 5 TR). In Energy Templates, by contrast, each Influence counts like a power tag (half a payout).  
  "In these few particular cases however, the influence doesn't affect the count itself, but adds to it." ([Jacob, 2020-10-08](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2517683/article/35992714#35992714))

### Interplanetary Trade

- **Can a wild tag count as a theoretical extra tag for Interplanetary Trade when you already have all distinct tags?** No. The wild tag can only be a tag that could normally be face up in your game, so it can't be an event tag, and it can't be a Venus tag unless you play with Venus Next.  
  "The wild tag can only be a tag that could normally be face up in your game." ([Jacob, 2020-01-05](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2334454/article/33717702#33717702))
- **Does Interplanetary Trade count its own tag among the different tags?** Yes, it also counts its own tag, if you don't already have a space tag in play.  
  "It also counts its own tag, if you don't already have a space tag in play." ([Jacob, 2022-06-03](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2334454/article/40197696#40197696))

### Law Suit

- **Can Law Suit be played in a solo game?** No: in solo your neutral opponent never harms you, so you can never meet the Law Suit condition (and it has no discard pile to archive it).  
  "In solo, you have a very nice and neutral opponent, that will never do you harm. So you'll never win (play) a Law Suit against them." ([Jacob, 2020-01-03](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2341108/article/33700832#33700832))

### Mons Insurance

- **Does Mons Insurance pay each player 3 MC for its own starting -2 MC production on opponents?** No. Although it would follow the normal working of cards, it would be unthematic to compensate for charging for the insurance; the final wording will make this clear.  
  "No, you don't. Although it would follow the normal working of cards, it would be unthematic to compensate for charging for the insurance." ([Jacob, 2019-04-16](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2187014/article/31756791#31756791))
- **Can Mons Insurance play a card that harms others when it cannot afford the 3 MC payouts?** Yes, because it can always pay 'as much as possible'. Any player can invoke triggers, so the hit player asks for the payout, but the active player chooses the order of effects and can pay when out of money, paying as much as possible.  
  "The active player can indeed play the card because you can always pay 'as much as possible'." ([Jacob, 2019-05-09](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2187014/article/31940356#31940356))
- **In solo, must Mons Insurance pay the neutral player when reducing production?** Yes. Production reduction is mandatory so compensation must be made; the reduction is made but not tracked, and the neutral player counts as a player.  
  "Production is mandatory, so compensation must be made. The reduction is made but not kept track of. The neutral player counts as a player." ([Jacob, 2019-05-23](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2187014/article/32049249#32049249))
- **In solo, must Mons Insurance pay when a card removes resources?** Resource destruction is optional, so it does not have to be performed and compensated.  
  "Resource destruction is optional, so doesn't have to be 'performed' and compensated." ([Jacob, 2019-05-23](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2187014/article/32049249#32049249))
- **In solo, must Mons Insurance pay when stealing resources?** Yes. Stealing removes resources from a player and so must be compensated.  
  "Stealing removes resources from a player, and so must be compensated." ([Jacob, 2019-05-23](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2187014/article/32049249#32049249))
- **When Mons Insurance is involved and a card steals resources (e.g. MC), can the insurance trigger between removing from the opponent and adding to yourself?** No. Stealing is a single effect, so the removal and the gain cannot be separated or interrupted by Mons Insurance. Had it been worded as remove + gain, the remove part could be omitted under the rules exception for such effects, which would be unthematic.  
  "B) is correct. Stealing is one effect." ([Jacob, 2019-12-16](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2331247/article/33592778#33592778))
- **As Mons Insurance, playing Deimos Down with your last MC and destroying plants while Optimal Aerobraking would give you MC, may you pay the insurance before taking the Aerobraking MC?** Yes. You decide the order of effects, so you can do the plant destruction and the insurance payout before taking the Optimal Aerobraking bonus.  
  "If you are Mons Insurance doing that, you can choose to do plant destruction and insurance payout before taking Aerobreaking bonus. You decide the order of effects." ([Jacob, 2019-12-18](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2331247/article/33609460#33609460))
- **Playing Giant Ice Asteroid against a player with 4 plants and Arctic Algae, may you place one ocean, activate Arctic Algae, destroy 6 plants, trigger Mons Insurance to pay 3 MC you don't have, and only then place the second ocean next to ocean(s) and collect its bonus?** Yes. Effects and triggers can be resolved in any order you choose, so Mons Insurance can use the order to avoid paying before the second ocean placement.  
  "Yes. Mons can be very sneaky as long as they follow the written rules..." ([Jacob, 2019-12-19](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2331247/article/33618871#33618871))
- **How does Mons Insurance' compensation work when a player is hit, and what if the payer has less than 3 MC?** Whenever someone gets hit by anyone, the Mons Insurance player must compensate them by giving 3 MC or all they have if less. If Mons Insurance itself gets hit, it pays itself. With only 2 MC, it pays 2 MC.  
  "Whenever someone gets hit by anyone, you have to compensate, and choose to either give them 3 MC or all you have." ([Jacob, 2020-02-02](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2361472/article/33946857#33946857))
- **When another player plays a microbe-tag card and Pharmacy Union loses 4 MC, does Mons Insurance have to pay Pharmacy Union 3 MC?** No. The loss is caused by Pharmacy Union's own ability, not by the player who played the microbe card, so Mons Insurance's payout is not triggered.  
  "No. It is not the microbe player that causes the loss, but Pharmacy Union's own ability." ([Jacob, 2020-06-19](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2451066/article/35146956#35146956))
- **If a player uses Ants to take a microbe from their own Tardigrades, does that count as stealing from 'another player' for Mons Insurance?** No. Taking your own resources does not count; the loss has to be inflicted on a player other than the one dealing the hurt. (If Mons itself were hurt, it would pay the insurance to itself, a net zero.)  
  "Taking your own stuff doesn't count. it has to be from another player than the one dealing the hurt." ([Jacob, 2020-08-14](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2331247/article/35577865#35577865))

### Philares

- **How does Philares' 'each new adjacency' trigger work?** It works like ocean adjacency bonuses: each new stretch of border created between Philares' tile and another player's tile gives a resource of choice, so a tile touching 2 opposing tiles gives 2 resources (can be different). Philares also benefits when other players create the border.  
  "Each new stretch of border created between Philares and another player's tiles gives Philares a resource of their choice on the player board." ([Jacob, 2019-05-01](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2195308/article/31875261#31875261)) `hedged`
- **Does Philares' ability trigger when an ocean is placed next to a tile?** No. Oceans are not owned, so they don't count. Creating a new adjacency means establishing a new permanent border between your land and that of an opponent; it refers to the result, not to what you do.  
  "Oceans are not owned, so they don't count." ([Jacob, 2019-12-30](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2337858/article/33675205#33675205))
- **If you place a tile next to two opposing tiles, or an opponent places a tile adjacent to two of your tiles, does Philares gain two resources?** Yes to both. Each time a non-ocean tile is placed, adjacencies are checked; if the new tile creates a new border between your tiles and an opponent's, the effect triggers separately for each stretch of border created, so the resources can be different. Philares also gets the bonus when other players create these borders.  
  "it triggers separately for each stretch of border created, so placing your tile next to 3 opposing tiles will give you 3 opportunities to take a standard resource" ([Jacob, 2020-01-09](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2345608/article/33749503#33749503))

### Pristar

- **If Pristar plays a Prelude that gives TR (UNMI Contractor), does it count as terraforming in generation 1 and block Pristar's ability?** Yes. Setup (playing corporations and Preludes) is included in generation 1, so gaining TR from such a Prelude blocks Pristar's ability for generation 1.  
  "Setup (playing corporations and preludes) is included in generation 1, so no, that would block Pristar's ability for generation 1." ([Jacob, 2019-11-24](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2318302/article/33443675#33443675))
- **Can the Turmoil corporations Pristar and Terralabs be played without the Turmoil expansion?** They should not be played without Turmoil: their effects are balanced against the TR revision rule in Turmoil, and Pristar has a hard time getting TR back.  
  "Turmoil Corporations like Pristar and Terralabs should not be played without Turmoil expansion, really." ([Jonathan, 2021-06-05](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2669157/article/37851931#37851931)) `intent` `inferred`
- **Can Pristar and Terralabs be used without Turmoil, and do they under-perform?** Pristar and Terralabs are designed to take advantage of the Reds ruling bonus (+1 TR if you are last in TR), so without Turmoil you may want to buff them a bit.  
  "Pristar and Terralabs are both designed to take advantage of the Reds ruling bonus (+1 TR if you're last in TR), so you might want to buff them a bit if playing without Turmoil." ([Jacob, 2025-12-14](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3627644/article/47022795#47022795)) `intent`

### Protected Habitats

- **Does Protected Habitats protect your plants, animals and microbes from global events?** No. Global events still affect you; Protected Habitats only stops opponents from removing them.  
  "Exactly. Global events still affect you." ([Jacob, 2019-11-25](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2318862/article/33448496#33448496))

### Red Tourism Wave

- **How does Red Tourism Wave count empty areas adjacent to your tiles?** It counts all empty areas (hexagons without tiles) adjacent to all your tiles. It counts hexes, not adjacencies, so a hex bordering two of your tiles counts once.  
  "It counts all empty areas (=hexagons without tiles on) adjacent to all your tiles. Note that it counts hexes, not adjacencies" ([Jacob, 2019-12-03](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2324045/article/33502759#33502759))
- **For Red Tourism Wave, do empty reserved ocean areas count, do cities not on Mars have zero adjacent empty areas, and do spaces beyond the board edge count?** Correct on all points: empty ocean/reserved areas count, cities not on Mars have zero adjacent empty areas, and spaces beyond the edge of the board do not count as empty areas.
