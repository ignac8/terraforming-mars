# Venus Next: official BGG rulings

47 rulings posted on BoardGameGeek by FryxGames, Stronghold Games and the Automa designers. [README.md](README.md) says who is who, how this was built, and what the tags mean. Under each heading the rulings run in date order, so a later one can overrule an earlier one.


## Rules


### Resources on Venus cards

- **Can standard-deck cards that add microbes to 'any card' (and Venus cards like Maxwell Base and Corroder Suits) put microbes on Thermophiles?** Yes. Maxwell Base and Corroder Suits can put microbes on Thermophiles, and Extreme-Cold Fungus can as well since the card wording allows it, even though some such combos are non-thematic. It was left that way for simplicity and playability.  
  "Yes, you can use Maxwell Base and Corroder Suits to put microbes on Thermophiles." ([Jacob, 2017-09-20](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1851430/article/26964203#26964203))
- **Which Venus cards restrict where resources can be added?** Stratopolis can only add floaters to a Venus card, while Floating Habs can add floaters to any card, not restricted to Venus. Maxwell Base and Corroder Suits can only add resources to Venus cards.  
  "Stratopolis (on Venus) can only add floaters to a Venus card, while Floating Habs (on Venus but could be elsewhere as well) can add floaters to any card" ([Jacob, 2017-09-20](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1851430/article/26964203#26964203))
- **What does the Venus icon next to a resource icon mean, and why do some cards say 'another Venus card' and Thermophiles 'any Venus card'?** The Venus icon is meant as an asterisk (restricting the target to Venus cards), like the steel icon on Aquifer Pumping's action cost. The four cards have the same mechanism of adding microbes, but only Thermophiles can collect them, hence 'another' versus 'any' wording.  
  "The Venus icon IS meant as an asterisk." ([Jacob, 2020-01-14](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2348654/article/33787082#33787082)) `inferred`

### Setup and expansion combinations

- **Should Venus Next be played with Corporate Era?** Venus Next is playable without Corporate Era, but for optimal experience and balance Corporate Era should be included; it is primarily balanced with it (e.g. science tag requirements are hard to satisfy without it).  
  "Well, Venus Next is certainly playable even without CE, but for optimal experience and balance, CE should be included." ([Jacob, 2018-01-04](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1913206/article/27872207#27872207)) `intent`

### Solar phase

- **When the Solar Cycle (world government terraforming) variant is used, should the game end if the last parameter is completed by the world government step?** No. The Solar Phase follows the Production Phase: Step 1 is to check end-game conditions; if not met, play another generation after this phase, and if met go immediately to end-game scoring including final greenery placement. Step 2 is World Government Terraforming: the first player raises 1 global parameter without getting any bonus. It is an official rule from Venus Next that can be used as a variant in any game.  
  "Step 1 - Check end game conditions. If not met, like in your case, continue to play another round after this Solar Phase. If met, immediatelt go to end game scoring including final greenery placement." ([Jacob, 2017-07-23](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1816918/article/26443321#26443321))
- **If the World Government's terraforming in the Solar Phase raises the last Mars parameter step, does the game end that generation?** No. The end game check (step 1 of the Solar Phase) happens before the World Government raises the last step, so another generation is played. That is the official rule; the order is so the upkeep phase is not performed when the game is ending. Players may house rule differently.  
  "The end game check is before the World Government raises the last step and therefore another generation is played. That is the official rule." ([Enoch, 2017-09-26](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1851190/article/27013833#27013833)) `house-rule-ok`
- **How does the Solar Phase work and what does it contain?** There is a new phase after the Production Phase, the Solar Phase. Step 1: check whether Mars is completely terraformed; if so the game ends as in the normal rules and the rest of the Solar Phase is skipped. Step 2: World Government Terraforming (used with Venus Next): the first player raises one global parameter (temperature, oxygen, ocean or Venus scale) without getting any bonus. More steps will be added by future expansions.  
  "The first step is to check if Mars is completely terraformed and if so, the game ends, just like in the normal rules, and the rest of the Solar Phase is skipped." ([Jacob, 2017-09-27](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1855450/article/27029475#27029475))
- **If the start player (World Government) maxes the last Mars parameter in the Solar phase, does the game end?** Correct about the timing: you play another generation, because the game end check happens before the parameter is raised.  
  "Correct about the tim7ng of game end." ([Jacob, 2018-07-19](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2027476/article/29564952#29564952))

### Solo game

- **Will the summer 2017 expansion be allowed in the Solo Terraformer Challenge?** Yes. The expansion for that summer will be allowed in the challenge because it does not change the conditions for solo play in any major way.  
  "Yes, the expansion for this summer will be allowed in this challenge, because it doesn't change the conditions for solo play in any major way." ([Jonathan, 2017-01-09](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1698984/article/24702081#24702081)) `variant` `inferred`
- **Is World Government Terraforming used in the solo game?** It is used in solo with the Venus Next expansion, where Venus must also be terraformed (another 15 steps) within the 14 generations. It cannot be used for solo without Venus.  
  "World Government Terraforming can not be used for solo without Venus." ([Jacob, 2017-07-23](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1816918/article/26444228#26444228))
- **Is World Government Terraforming optional in solo play with Venus Next?** No. It is always mandatory for solo play with Venus Next, because you must max the Venus scale as well as the 3 Mars parameters to win.  
  "World Government Terraforming is always mandatory for solo play with Venus Next" ([Jacob, 2018-01-04](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1913206/article/27871699#27871699))
- **How does Venus Next work in solo?** In solo with Venus Next you must also terraform Venus in the allotted time, and you get the help of World Government Terraforming; skipping WG Terraforming is not allowed.  
  "Skipping WG Terraforming is not allowed." ([Jacob, 2018-05-07](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1983367/article/29020516#29020516))
- **In TR63 solo with Venus Next, is the Venus terraforming requirement still needed, and is World Government Terraforming used?** In the TR63 solo variant the Venus terraforming requirement is completely ignored, but World Government Terraforming (WGT) still helps you reach bonuses on the tracks.  
  "you could try the TR63 solo, where the Venus terraforming requirement is completely ignored, but the WGT still helps you reach yummy bonuses on the tracks." ([Jacob, 2019-06-19](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2223324/article/32269453#32269453))
- **In solo with Venus Next, does the game end when the global parameters are maxed before generation 14?** The solo rules override the normal multiplayer rules. The solo game ends at generation 14 regardless of the global parameters (generation 12 with Prelude), so if you complete the parameters earlier you keep playing to score more points.  
  "The solo game ends at generation 14 no matter what the global parameters are, or generation 12 if using Prelude." ([Jacob, 2020-04-22](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2412654/article/34611124#34611124))
- **In solo with Venus Next, is completing the Venus scale required to win?** Yes: completing the Venus scale is required to win the solo game along with the other 3 parameters on Mars, unless you are using the TR63 variant introduced in Prelude.  
  "Completing the Venus scale is required to win the solo game, along with the other 3 parameters on Mars." ([Jacob, 2020-04-22](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2412654/article/34611124#34611124))
- **In generation 14 of a solo game, does World Government Terraforming fire when a Mars parameter remains?** No. In generation 14, World Government Terraforming will not fire, since the game ends in the end game step right before it.  
  "In generation 14, the World Government Terraforming will not fire, since the game ends in the End game step right before that." ([Jacob, 2020-04-22](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2412654/article/34611124#34611124))
- **In solo TR63 with Venus Next, should World Government Terraforming be used?** Yes, use WGT. It makes TR63 slightly harder since parameters fill faster, but helps reach requirements and bonus steps faster.  
  "You do use it, yes. It doesn't help very much in TR63, in fact it makes it a little harder since the parameters fill up faster." ([Jacob, 2021-08-20](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2223324/article/38312835#38312835))

### Venus track

- **Is the Venus scale an end game condition?** No. The Venus scale is a global parameter that can be increased, including by the World Government, but it is not an end game condition.  
  "remember that Venus scale is also a parameter that can be increased, but Venus scale is not an end game condition!" ([Enoch, 2017-09-26](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1851190/article/27013833#27013833))
- **Do Adaptation Technology, Special Design and Inventrix work on Venus requirements?** Yes. The Venus track is considered a global parameter, and the Venus rules clarify these cards work on the Venus scale as well. Morning Star Inc., on the other hand, only affects the Venus scale.  
  "The Venus track is considered a global parameter, and the Venus rules clarify that Adaptation Technology, Special Design and Inventrix do indeed work on the Venus scale as well." ([Jacob, 2018-02-18](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1942470/article/28304412#28304412))
- **Does the +2/-2 global requirement adjustment (card and corporation) also apply to the Venus track?** Yes. The Venus track counts as a global parameter, as clarified in the Venus rules, so the +/-2 adjustment applies to it too.  
  "Yes, the Venus track also counts. This was clarified in the Venus rules." ([Jacob, 2025-09-27](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3583118/article/46665962#46665962))

### World Government Terraforming

- **Who receives placement and track bonuses, and TR, when the world government raises a parameter in the Solar Phase?** The track and placement bonuses are triggered but go to the world government, not to the player: ocean placement bonuses go to the WG, the WG's extra heat production at -24 and -20 C is nullified since nobody plays the WG, and if the WG's raise reaches a threshold that places an ocean or raises another parameter, the WG gets the TR, not the player.  
  "the track bonuses are triggered, but to the world government, not to the player." ([Jacob, 2017-08-10](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1816918/article/26602289#26602289))
- **In the Solar Phase (world government terraforming), who gets bonus steps and placement bonuses when the parameter is raised?** Bonus steps are triggered but go to the world government (WG), which is treated as a player controlled by the first player; all normal rules apply. Example: the WG raises temperature to 0 C and gets 1 TR, which triggers an ocean placed by the WG (first player) for 1 TR plus placement bonuses (nothing happens); another player with Arctic Algae gets 2 plants because the WG is considered a player.  
  "Bonus steps are triggered too, but goes to the WG (controlled by the first player. Basically the WG is a player that does nothing else than this, and is controlled by the first player. All normal rules apply." ([Jacob, 2017-08-10](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1827494/article/26602453#26602453))
- **Does the world government have resources or production that can be targeted?** No. Unlike the neutral player in the solo variant, the WG is not considered to have any resources or production to mess with.  
  "In contrast to the neutral 'player' in the solo variant, the WG is not considered to have any resources or production to mess with." ([Jacob, 2017-08-10](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1827494/article/26602453#26602453))
- **Is the Solar Phase / World Government Terraforming mandatory?** The Solar Phase becomes an integral part of the game from Venus Next on, and World Government Terraforming (a step of it) is the norm with Venus Next. Using it without Venus Next, or playing Venus Next without it, are non-standard variants. New expansions will add more steps to the Solar Phase.  
  "The Solar phase will become an integral part of the game from Venus Next and on." ([Jacob, 2017-09-17](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1834034/article/26937649#26937649)) `superseded` by [a later post](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1855450/article/27029475#27029475)
- **Can Venus Next be played without World Government Terraforming, and WGT used without Venus Next?** Playing Venus Next without WGT is an official variant. Using WGT without Venus Next is not an official variant; it can be done, but then Venus Next will feel longer, which was not intended. For solo, WGT is only included when playing with Venus Next.  
  "You can play Venus Next without WGT as an official variant." ([Jacob, 2017-09-27](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1855450/article/27029475#27029475))
- **When the World Government terraforms, are bonus steps (temperature at 0 C, oxygen at 8%) and placement bonuses performed?** All placement bonuses and other bonuses are performed, but the benefits go to the WG, not the first player. The first player raises the bonus temperature and decides where to place the bonus ocean on behalf of the WG. The WG does not keep track of resources, so placement bonuses are irrelevant.  
  "All placement bonuses and other bonuses are performed, but the benefits go to the WG, not to the first player." ([Isaac, 2017-10-24](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1868973/article/27246645#27246645))
- **Is the World Government Terraforming step needed when playing Venus Next, with or without Corporate Era?** The Solar Phase is mandatory (it includes the end of game step), but there is an optional rule not to use the World Government Terraforming step. It is meant to speed up the game a bit when using Venus Next and is not intended for play without Venus Next (though many people do).  
  "The Solar Phase is mandatory (it includes the end of game step), but there is an optional rule not to use the World Government Terraforming step." ([Jacob, 2018-01-04](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1913206/article/27871699#27871699))
- **May World Government Terraforming be skipped in a multiplayer Venus Next game?** Venus Next adds the Venus parameter, a 6th milestone and award, and World Government Terraforming in the Solar phase after production. It can be skipped in multiplayer if you want a longer game.  
  "Skip VN:World Government Terraforming if you want a longer game" ([Jacob, 2018-05-07](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1983367/article/29020516#29020516)) `variant`
- **Must the World Government player choose Venus when raising a parameter?** The player must choose a non-maxed parameter, so if Venus was the only one left it would have to be chosen (but then the game would already have ended from the other parameters being maxed).  
  "But the player has to choose a non-maxed parameter, so if Venus was the only one left you would have to choose it." ([Jacob, 2018-07-19](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2027476/article/29564952#29564952))
- **When the World Government terraforms and hits a bonus (temperature bonus on the oxygen track, ocean bonus on the temperature track), do the bonuses occur?** Yes. Treat it as a neutral player (the WG) taking the action following the first player's suggestions. They even get the heat production bonus on the temperature track, although that has no game effect.  
  "Yes. Think of it as a neutral player (the WG) doing the action, follówing the suggestions of the first player." ([Jacob, 2018-10-04](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2070622/article/30152829#30152829))
- **Can the Venus Next World Government Terraforming rule be added to speed up a game?** Suggested as an optional speed-up: add the World Government terraforming rule from Venus Next if the game is still too slow.  
  "Add world government terraforming rule from Venus Next if it is still too slow." ([Jacob, 2021-09-20](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2727902/article/38491087#38491087)) `variant`

## Cards


### Atmoscoop

- **Can Atmoscoop's 'raise temperature or Venus' be played choosing temperature when it is maxed or one step from max while Venus is not?** Yes. The exception for maxed parameters is valid for the Venus scale too, so you can play either effect, deciding which: choose, then do as much as you can of that choice.  
  "The exception for maxed parameters is valid for the Venus scale, so you can play either effect - you decide." ([Jacob, 2018-07-16](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2025415/article/29535551#29535551))

### Celestic

- **Does Celestic's initial card reveal for floater icons apply to Stratospheric Birds, which requires paying a floater?** Yes, Stratospheric Birds is definitely a target since it has a floater icon.  
  "Yes, Stratospheric Birds are definitely a target since it has a floater icon." ([Jacob, 2017-11-02](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1870830/article/27327535#27327535))
- **Does the requirement of 5 floaters on Aerosport Tournament count as a floater icon for Celestic?** Yes, it does.  
  "Yes it does." ([Jacob, 2018-01-10](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1870830/article/27922341#27922341))

### Hydrogen to Venus

- **Does Hydrogen to Venus add 1 floater per Jovian tag to a single Venus card, or one floater to a Venus card for each Jovian tag (possibly different cards)?** All floaters go to the same card: add one floater per Jovian tag you have to a single Venus card.  
  "Correct. All floaters go to the same card." ([Jacob, 2021-03-21](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2625397/article/37306506#37306506))

### Nitrophilic Moss

- **With Manutech, can you play Nitrophilic Moss by raising plant production first, gaining the plants from Manutech, then paying the plant loss?** Yes. After declaring the card, checking the requirement and paying, the waiting effects are +2 plant production and -2 plant. Choosing production first makes Manutech add a +2 plant effect to the waiting effects, which you can then resolve before the -2 plant effect.  
  "If you choose the +2 production first, then Manutech will add a +2 plant effect to the waiting effects. You can then choose that effect before the -2 plant effect." ([Jacob, 2018-06-13](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2009076/article/29305758#29305758))
- **Is losing 2 plants on Nitrophilic Moss a requirement, so that it cannot be played without 2 plants?** The loss of 2 plants is part of the effects (shown among the lower-panel icons), not a requirement; the text is only a reminder. Effects can be chosen in any order but none can be ignored (apart from the three exceptions); if you cannot complete all effects in your chosen order, you take the card back.  
  "But losing 2 plant is part of the effect, as its icons are depicted in the lower panel among other effects of the card." ([Jacob, 2018-06-15](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2009076/article/29315504#29315504))
- **With Manutech you can play Nitrophilic Moss with no plants, since production gives plants that are then lost; is that correct?** Confirmed ('All correct'): this works. The same reasoning is confirmed for a Tharsis Republic player paying the 6 MC ocean cost on a Hellas ocean space after the city's income, and for placing an ocean and then removing plants from an Arctic Algae owner who just gained them.  
  "All correct." ([Jacob, 2018-06-17](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2009076/article/29327311#29327311)) `inferred`

### Predators

- **Can Predators take animals from Venusian Animals?** Yes (answered in thematic terms): Predators can attack animals on Venusian Animals, since predators may be brought to the other planet.  
  "Yes, if someone brings them in a shuttle to the other planet." ([Jonathan, 2017-04-20](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1764847/article/25671925#25671925)) `intent` `inferred`
- **Are Predators limited to Martian animals, i.e. can they not attack Venusian Animals?** Implied no restriction: Predators are not all Martian, so they can attack Venusian animals as well. Answered only as a rhetorical question.  
  "Who said the Predators are all Martian?" ([Jacob, 2017-04-25](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1764847/article/25721474#25721474)) `intent` `hedged` `inferred`
- **Can Predators remove animals from Venus-based animal cards, and Ants microbes from Venus microbe cards?** Not stated outright. The designer explains that existing cards could not be changed and new wording would have been complicated, so Predators/Ants work on Venus animal and microbe cards as printed ('hawks' flying over Venus).  
  "Those predators are hawks... Yeah, we couldn't change existing cards, and rewording the new new ones would have required quite complicated text and iconography." ([Jacob, 2018-03-03](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1950769/article/28442666#28442666)) `intent` `inferred`
- **Can Predators remove an animal from an opponent's Stratospheric Birds (a Venus card)?** Implied yes: the answer is that Predators can be seen as a multitude of species, e.g. Venusian hawks; no restriction to Mars cards is stated. This is a thematic interpretation.  
  "you could also see Predators as a multitude of species, like Venusian hawks for example..." ([Jacob, 2022-08-23](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2922265/article/40675439#40675439)) `hedged` `inferred`

### Sulphur-Eating Bacteria

- **Can Sulphur-Eating Bacteria's microbes be spent for MC as a stand-alone action, or only when paying for another card?** Sulphur-Eating Bacteria's action spends any number of its microbes to gain triple the MC as an effect of the action itself, not as payment for playing a card. Only Dirigibles works like steel or titanium, with floaters usable solely as payment for Venus cards.  
  "Dirigibles is the kind of effect your friend was thinking about, analogous to the use of steel and titanium. [...] Sulphur Eating Bacteria is formulated like Titanium Shuttles, and a multitude of microbe- and floater cards, which spend their resources to do something, not as part of the action of playing a card." ([Jacob, 2022-03-14](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2830879/article/39670382#39670382))

### Unexpected Application

- **Must the discard in Unexpected Application (and Mars University) be paid before the rest of the card resolves, e.g. can Venus be raised first so the 8% bonus card is the one discarded?** The intent of both cards was to pay (discard) first. Strictly, however, icons take precedence and 'do X to get Y' does not necessarily imply order, so the card can be paid later during the action. Fryxen's advice: play it as intended, but if someone insists, allow paying later; a printing may add an asterisk.  
  "In summary, play it as intended, but if someone insists, allow paying later." ([Jacob, 2025-09-18](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/3577088/article/46624092#46624092))

### Viron

- **Can Viron's action be used on an opponent's blue card that has already been used this generation?** No. Like all cards, effects affect only you unless specified otherwise (usually a red border around an icon, and always stated in the text), so Viron cannot use other players' actions.  
  "So no, Viron cannot use other players' actions." ([Jonathan, 2017-09-13](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1847706/article/26903254#26903254))
- **If a blue card offers action A or action B and you used A this turn, can Viron's action be used to do B?** Yes. You can choose either; Viron simply lets you use the card again.  
  "You can choose either. Viron simply lets you use the card again." ([Jacob, 2017-11-06](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1878294/article/27359153#27359153))
- **Can Viron use an opponent's blue card action?** No. Viron can only use your own blue card action (one of your blue cards with a red arrow signifying an action).  
  "Viron can only use your own blue card action (one of your blue cards with a red arrow signifying an action)." ([Jacob, 2018-10-12](https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1878294/article/30209348#30209348))
