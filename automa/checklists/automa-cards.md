# Automa card text

Text of every MarsBot bonus card (B01–B32) and MarsBot corporation (C01–C46), transcribed from scans of
the physical cards (https://tm.hadronikle.com/, Automa filter). Two independent readings agreed on every
name, tag, draft priority and line of text. Per the designer, when the card is clear it wins over the rulebook.

Tags and draft priorities are printed as icons; they are written here as tag names. "#N" on a track is
track position N in code (index 0 is the start square). "tracker" means the card replaces that track's
marker with the cube rather than placing it on a numbered space.

## Bonus cards

### B01 Meteor Shower

- You must lose 5 plants.  
  If you lost at least 3 plants or if an effect blocked you losing any plants, destroy this card.

### B02 Invasive Species

- You must lose 1 animal or 1 microbe (highest scoring if you have multiple).  
  If you are playing with *Venus Next* or *Colonies*, MarsBot gains 2 MC and 1 floater. Otherwise, it gains 5 MC.

### B03 Research and Development

- MarsBot draws and resolves a project card from the project deck.

### B04 Overachievement

- a. MarsBot claims a milestone if able, then destroys this card.  
  b. If it’s the 6th generation or later, MarsBot funds the award in which it is the most ahead of you (leftmost if tied), then destroys this card.  
  c. MarsBot gains 5 MC.

### B05 Expedited Construction

- If a city tile can be placed adjacent to 2 or more greenery or ocean tiles, MarsBot places a city there, and destroys this card.  
  Otherwise, no effect.
- *Use when NOT playing with Colonies.*

### B06 Lobbyists

- a. If the temperature global parameter is 1-2 steps away from a bonus step or completion, MarsBot raises the temperature 2 steps, and destroys this card.  
  b. If the oxygen global parameter is 1-2 steps away from a bonus step or completion, MarsBot places a greenery tile, raises oxygen 2 steps, and destroys this card.  
  c. If there is a space reserved for oceans adjacent to 2 ocean tiles, MarsBot places an ocean tile, and destroys this card.  
  d. MarsBot raises oxygen 1 step, places an ocean, or raises temperature 1 step, whichever is furthest from being complete.
- *Use when NOT playing with Venus Next.*

### B07 Local Neural Instance

- Place the MarsBot Local Neural Instance tile on the board adjacent to no tiles. Do not place it on an edge space, nor on or adjacent to any reserved spaces. Usual placement tiebreakers still apply. Then, destroy this card.  
  If the tile cannot be placed, MarsBot draws and resolves a project card from the project deck.

### B08 Corporate Competition

- **Badge:** T
- If MarsBot has at least 5 MC, resolve the closest funded award’s action, leftmost if tied. If MarsBot takes an action, it loses 5 MC. Otherwise, it draws and resolves another bonus card.  
  • Landlord: MarsBot places a greenery tile and raises oxygen 1 step.  
  • Banker: Advance the building or event track, whichever is least-advanced, building if tied.  
  • Scientist: Advance the science track.  
  • Thermalist: Advance the power track.  
  • Miner: Advance the space track.  
  • Venuphile: Advance the Venus track.
- *Use when playing on the Tharsis board.*

### B15 Lobbyists

- **Badge:** V

  b. If the oxygen track is 1-2 steps of a reward or completion, MarsBot places a greenery tile, raises oxygen 2 steps, and destroys this card.  
  c. If the Venus track is within 1-2 steps of a reward or completion, MarsBot raises Venus 2 steps.  
  d. MarsBot raises oxygen 1 step, places an ocean, or raises temperature 1 step, whichever is furthest from being complete.
- *Use when playing with Venus Next.*

### B16 Government Intervention

- **Badge:** V
- If it is an even-numbered generation, or if Venus is complete, MarsBot raises oxygen 1 step, places an ocean, or raises temperature 1 step, whichever is furthest from being complete.  
  Otherwise, MarsBot raises Venus 1 step.  
  MarsBot does NOT receive TR or MC for the effects of this card.  
  At the beginning of every generation, shuffle this into MarsBot’s action deck.
- *Use when playing with Venus Next.*

### B17 Expedited Construction

- **Badge:** [exp: colonies]
- a. If a city tile can be placed adjacent to 2 or more greenery or ocean tiles, MarsBot places a city there and destroys this card.  
  b. If MarsBot has 1 or 0 colonies, it builds one on a randomly selected colony tile where it doesn’t have one and gains 2 of the corresponding resource to its Shipping Board.  
  c. Otherwise, no effect.

### B18 Outer System Foothold

- **Badge:** [exp: colonies]

  Discard the top card of the bonus deck.
- *Use when playing with Colonies.*

### B19 Shipping Lines

- **Badge:** [exp: colonies]
- Select the colony tile with its most-advanced track. If tied, select one with a MarsBot colony. If still tied, select randomly.  
  MarsBot loses 1 MC, then it trades with this colony gaining 2 of the corresponding resource to its Shipping Board.  
  Starting with the second generation: At the beginning of every generation, shuffle this into MarsBot’s action deck.
- *Use when playing with Colonies.*

### B20 Extended Shipping Lines

- **Badge:** [exp: colonies]

- *Use when playing with Colonies.*

### B21 Party Politics

- **Badge:** [exp: turmoil]
- Place 1 MarsBot delegate, if able.  
  If there is at least 1 MarsBot delegate remaining in reserve and MarsBot has 5 MC, discard a card from the project deck faceup. If the discarded card’s cost is divisible by 3, MarsBot loses 5 MC and places another delegate.  
  At the beginning of every generation, shuffle this into MarsBot’s action deck.
- *Use when playing with Turmoil.*

### B22 Settlers

- **Badge:** [icon: white dome]
- Place one of MarsBot’s player markers on the map on a non-reserved area, using the usual tiebreakers. Before using the final tiebreak (flip a card), MarsBot first looks for the space with the most adjacent spaces that are reserved for oceans.  
  At the beginning of every generation, shuffle this into MarsBot’s action deck.
- *Use when MarsBot is playing the Arcadian Communities corporation.*

### B23 Rapid Sprouting

- **Badge:** ECOLINE
- If the Ecoline corporation card has a plant resource on it, remove it, MarsBot places a greenery tile, and it raises oxygen 1 step.  
  Otherwise, add a plant resource to the Ecoline corporation card.  
  At the beginning of every generation, shuffle this into MarsBot’s action deck.
- *Use when MarsBot is playing the Ecoline corporation.*

### B24 Supply & Demand

- **Badge:** FACTORUM
- MarsBot gains 3 MC, or as much as possible, from the Factorum corporation card.  
  If it gained 0 MC, advance the power track.  
  At the beginning of every generation, shuffle this into MarsBot’s action deck.
- *Use when MarsBot is playing the Factorum corporation.*

### B25 Do It Right

- **Badge:** [icon: Inventrix X logo]
- a. If the temperature global parameter is 1-2 steps away from a bonus step or completion, MarsBot raises the temperature 2 steps.  
  b. If the oxygen global parameter is 1-2 steps away from a bonus step or completion, MarsBot places a greenery tile and raises oxygen 2 steps.  
  c. If there is a space reserved for oceans adjacent to 2 ocean tiles, MarsBot places an ocean tile.  
  d. No effect.  
  At the beginning of every generation, shuffle this into MarsBot’s action deck.
- *Use when MarsBot is playing the Inventrix corporation.*

### B26 Venusian Lobby

- **Badge:** MSI
- MarsBot raises Venus 1 step and advances its Venus tag track.  
  Then, MarsBot raises oxygen 1 step, places an ocean, or raises temperature 1 step, whichever is furthest from being complete.
- *Use when MarsBot is playing the Morningstar Inc. corporation.*

### B27 Build, Build, Build

- **Badge:** PHILARES
- a. MarsBot places a city tile adjacent to one of your greenery tiles. If successful, it loses 5 MC.  
  b. MarsBot places a special tile matching a card in its played pile adjacent to one of your city tiles. If successful, destroy that card and MarsBot loses 3 MC.  
  c. MarsBot gains 3 MC, and shuffle this card back into the bonus deck.
- *Use when MarsBot is playing the Philares corporation.*

### B28 Diversification

- **Badge:** ROBINSON INDUSTRIES
- Advance the least-advanced track, topmost if tied.  
  MarsBot loses 4 MC, if able.  
  At the beginning of every generation, shuffle this into MarsBot’s action deck.
- *Use when MarsBot is playing the Robinson Industries corporation.*

### B29 Gray Eminence

- **Badge:** Septem Tribus
- Place 2 MarsBot delegates (if able) using the following priorities:  
  1. The party with the lowest number of MarsBot’s delegates.  
  2. The party with the lowest number of your delegates.  
  3. Random (flip a card).  
  For each delegate it couldn’t place, it gains 2 MC.  
  At the beginning of every generation, shuffle this into MarsBot’s action deck.
- *Use when MarsBot is playing the Septem Tribus corporation.*

### B30 Interface Hyperlink

- **Badge:** TYCHO MAGNETICS
- Draw project cards from the project deck equal to MarsBot’s space number on the power track (minimum 2). Select 2 cards from the drawn cards using the following priorities:  
  1. Science tag.  
  2. Most expensive.  
  3. Most tags.  
  4. Random.  
  Resolve the 2 selected cards, and discard the rest, then destroy this card.
- *Use when MarsBot is playing the Tycho Magnetics corporation.*

### B31 Government Subsidy

- **Badge:** UNMI
- MarsBot raises its TR 1 step.
- *Use when MarsBot is playing the UNMI corporation.*

### B32 Investors

- **Badge:** UTOPIA INVEST
- If it is an even-numbered generation, advance MarsBot’s least-advanced track (topmost, if tied), and regress its most-advanced (topmost if tied) track.  
  Otherwise, MarsBot gains MC equal to its least-advanced track space number.  
  At the beginning of every generation, shuffle this into MarsBot’s action deck.
- *Use when MarsBot is playing the Utopia Investments corporation.*
